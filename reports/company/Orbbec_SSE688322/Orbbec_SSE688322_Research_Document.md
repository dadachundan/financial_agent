## 1. COMPANY OVERVIEW

Orbbec (奥比中光科技集团股份有限公司) is a Chinese 3D-vision-sensing company that designs, manufactures, and sells depth cameras, LiDAR sensors, and integrated vision platforms used as the "eyes" of robots, payment terminals, biometric access devices, 3D scanners, and AR/VR systems. The company describes itself as building "the eyes of robots" and a "robotics & AI vision industry middle platform" (机器人与 AI 视觉产业中台) — language that became central to its 2025 repositioning and is repeated verbatim in the 2025 年度报告摘要 (Source: 2025 年年度报告摘要, p. 4, cninfo SSE:688322).

The principal business is the design, R&D, production, and sale of 3D vision-perception products in three product families: **3D vision sensors** (precision optical-measurement systems combining a depth-engine ASIC, depth-engine algorithm, generic or proprietary image sensor, proprietary optics, driver, and firmware that capture and output 3D information about people, objects, and space); **consumer-grade application devices** (turnkey appliances built around those sensors — most visibly facial-recognition payment terminals); and **industrial-grade application devices** (integrated 3D measurement systems for high-precision inspection) (Source: 2025 年年度报告摘要, p. 4, cninfo SSE:688322). Under the China Listed Companies Association's industry-classification guide, Orbbec is in "manufacturing — computer, communications and other electronic equipment manufacturing" (industry code C39).

The technology stack is unusually vertically integrated for a 3D-camera company. Orbbec is one of a handful of vendors worldwide that has commercialised **all six** of the major 3D-sensing technical paths — structured light (monocular and binocular), iToF, dToF, active/passive stereo, LiDAR, and industrial-grade 3D metrology (Source: 2025 年年度报告摘要, p. 11, cninfo SSE:688322). It designs its own depth-engine ASICs in-house under the MX-series brand (MX400 → MX6000 → MX6300 → MX6600 → MX6800, the last of which powers the Gemini 330 family and HONOR's first humanoid robot), and runs those chips on a Fabless model through third-party foundries (Source: company technology page, https://www.orbbec.com.cn/index/Page/index.html?cate=22; 2025 年年度报告摘要, p. 5). The company also designs in-house iToF image sensors, dToF SPAD CMOS, and diffractive optical elements for its structured-light projectors.

**Revenue model.** Orbbec sells direct to global customers — the 2025 年度报告摘要 explicitly states that the company "adopts a direct-sales-dominated model for domestic and overseas customers" (Source: 2025 年年度报告摘要, p. 6, cninfo SSE:688322). Average selling prices are not disclosed, but bench-marking by third-party robotics integrators puts a Gemini 335 active-stereo camera at roughly US$250, versus US$350 for the comparable Intel RealSense D435i and US$450 for a Stereolabs ZED 2i ([OpenELAB Gemini 335 vs RealSense D435i comparison](https://openelab.io/blogs/learn/comparing-3d-vision-technology-orbbec-gemini-335-vs-intel-realsense-d435i); [Society for Vision-based Robotics Center, 2025](https://www.roboticscenter.ai/blog/best-depth-cameras-robotics)). Femto-Bolt iToF cameras (positioned as the Microsoft Azure Kinect DK replacement) sell at roughly US$600.

**Scale indicators.** FY2025 revenue was RMB 940.74 million, up **66.66%** year-over-year from RMB 564.46 million in 2024 and RMB 360.01 million in 2023 (Source: 2025 年年度报告摘要, p. 13, cninfo SSE:688322). 2025 was the company's first profitable year: net income attributable to parent of **RMB 127.91 million** versus losses of RMB -62.91 million in 2024 and RMB -275.88 million in 2023. Non-GAAP net income (扣非净利润) of RMB 70.88 million is the more conservative figure, but importantly positive after years of nine-figure operating losses (Source: 2025 年年度报告摘要, p. 13, cninfo SSE:688322). The recovery extended into Q1 2026: revenue RMB 202.89 million (+6.19% YoY) and net income RMB 31.00 million (+27.48% YoY); the non-GAAP swing was even more dramatic at +531% to RMB 21.76 million as government-subsidy contribution fell (Source: 2026 年第一季度报告, p. 1, cninfo SSE:688322).

The balance sheet is conservative. Q1 2026 total assets were RMB 3.38 billion against just RMB 368 million of total liabilities — net equity of RMB 2.99 billion. Cash plus marketable securities plus long-term debt investments totalled approximately RMB 1.84 billion — i.e. most of the IPO and follow-on placement proceeds remain undeployed (Source: 2026 年第一季度报告, pp. 8-10, cninfo SSE:688322). Short-term borrowings of RMB 114 million are de minimis. Goodwill of just RMB 3.2 million confirms there has been no transformative M&A.

**Geographic footprint.** Headquartered at the Orbbec Technology Building, Nanshan District, Shenzhen (深圳市南山区西丽街道高新北一道 88 号奥比科技大厦). Manufacturing is split between Foshan, Guangdong (plant commissioned 2024) and a newly announced China-Vietnam dual hub (unveiled at CES 2026 in January 2026; [Orbbec CES 2026 press release](https://www.orbbec.com/company/news/)). Direct overseas sales grew approximately **80%** YoY in Q1 2026, suggesting an aggressive international ramp (Source: 2026 年第一季度报告, p. 2, cninfo SSE:688322). Korea, Japan, the EU, and the US are the principal export destinations, served via a network of distributors (DigiKey, Stemmer Imaging, B&H Photo, Atlantis Robotics, et al.) and resellers.

**People and customers.** Headcount is not separately disclosed in the 摘要, but Q1 2026 cash compensation of RMB 113.2 million annualises to roughly RMB 450 million — supporting an estimate of approximately 800-1,000 employees (Source: 2026 年第一季度报告, p. 14, cninfo SSE:688322). The company says it has shipped more than **5 million** 3D sensors cumulatively and serves **3,000+** customers and developers globally, of which more than **1,000 are robot OEMs** (Source: [Orbbec Hurun AI Top-20 press release, Jan 2026](https://www.orbbec.com/news/the-eyes-of-physical-ai-orbbec-ranks-as-sole-robotics-vision-leader-in-2025-hurun-ai-top-20/); company "About" page, https://www.orbbec.com.cn/index/CompanyProfile/index.html?cate=49). Domestic robotics revenue tripled YoY in Q1 2026 (Source: 2026 年第一季度报告, p. 2). Patent portfolio is approximately 2,000 filings related to 3D vision (company "About" page).

In short: a Shenzhen-headquartered, founder-controlled, A-share-listed, vertically integrated 3D-vision platform company — one of seven worldwide capable of volume-shipping million-unit-class array-based 3D sensors (the others being Apple, Microsoft, Sony, RealSense, Huawei, and Samsung, per the company's own assessment) (Source: 2025 年年度报告摘要, p. 11, cninfo SSE:688322) — that has just turned its first profit at the inflection of the embodied-AI cycle.

## 2. COMPANY HISTORY

**Founding (January 2013, Shenzhen).** Orbbec was founded by 黄源浩 (Huang Yuanhao, English nicknames "Howard" and "Jacky") in early 2013 as 深圳奥比中光科技有限公司. Huang's published origin story: while serving as a postdoctoral researcher in the Singapore-MIT Alliance for Research and Technology (SMART) Center, he saw Microsoft's first-generation Kinect (2010-2011) and concluded that consumer-grade structured-light 3D sensing was about to become a mass-market platform. He returned to China to build a domestic equivalent ([GDToday founder profile](https://www.newsgd.com/node_d36b0ef83f/63c7d5ab3f.shtml); [Baidu Baike entry](https://baike.baidu.com/item/奥比中光科技集团股份有限公司/54195925)).

**First product cycle (2014-2016).** The **Astra** family — monocular structured-light depth cameras at 850 nm — shipped in 2015, with the SDK open-sourced to GitHub in October 2015. Astra was positioned explicitly as a Chinese-designed alternative to Microsoft Kinect for Windows v1 and Asus Xtion. Early A/B/C-round backers included 弘德投资, 仁智资本, 金石投资 (CITIC affiliate), and 广发证券 ([36Kr financing history](https://36kr.com/p/1722529824769)).

**2017 — the Alipay breakthrough.** Orbbec's first commercial inflection came from 蚂蚁集团 (Ant Group), which selected its structured-light modules for in-store face-payment kiosks ("刷脸支付") — most prominently the Dragonfly (蜻蜓) terminals — making Orbbec the dominant domestic 3D-sensor supplier for Alipay's rollout. The relationship seeded a strategic round: in May 2018 Ant led a **>US$200 million Series D** at a roughly US$2 billion post-money valuation, with 赛富 SAIF, 松禾资本, 天狼星, 联想之星, and prior backers participating ([36Kr](https://36kr.com/p/1722529824769); [iyiou.com](https://www.iyiou.com/p/72853)). (Note: some secondary reports cite BlueRun Ventures and SoftBank — neither appears in the verified cap table.)

**2018-2021 — chip roadmap and dual-class structure.** Orbbec built out its in-house MX-series depth-engine ASIC lineage (MX400 → MX6000 → MX6300 → MX6600) and expanded into Astra+, Astra Mini, Astra Embedded, the Persee camera-computer, and early iToF concepts. By 2021 it had completed eight financing rounds at an implied post-money of approximately **RMB 17.86 billion**. On January 30, 2021 shareholders adopted a **dual-class share structure** with founder-held A-class shares carrying 5× the voting power of B-class — a STAR Market feature rarely exercised in practice (Source: 2025 年年度报告摘要, p. 16, cninfo SSE:688322).

**July 7, 2022 — STAR Market IPO.** Orbbec listed on Shanghai's STAR Market (科创板) under ticker **688322** at an offering price of RMB 30.99 per share, raising approximately **RMB 1.24 billion** — below the original RMB 1.85 billion target ([Jiemian](https://www.jiemian.com/article/7708626.html); [Pandaily](https://pandaily.com/3d-sensor-unicorn-orbbec-backed-by-alibaba-will-go-public-and-raise-over-1-8-billion-yuan/)). The stock opened +47%, peaked +57% intraday, and closed at RMB 42.10 (~RMB 17 billion market cap). Orbbec was branded "3D 视觉第一股" — the first pure-play 3D-vision listing on a Chinese exchange — under the STAR Market's special carve-out for unprofitable hard-tech issuers.

**October 2023 — Microsoft Azure Kinect handoff.** Microsoft discontinued the Azure Kinect DK developer kit and publicly endorsed Orbbec's **Femto Mega** (and later Femto Bolt) as the official replacement. Femto Mega was co-developed with Microsoft and NVIDIA — Microsoft contributed Azure Kinect ToF IP, NVIDIA contributed Jetson Nano integration, and Orbbec contributed the hardware and SDK ([Windows Central](https://www.windowscentral.com/accessories/microphones/microsoft-discontinues-kinect-again-likely-for-good-this-time); [PRNewswire, NVIDIA GTC 2024](https://www.prnewswire.com/apac/news-releases/orbbec-showcases-microsoft-azure-kinect-dk-replacement-at-nvidia-gtc-2024-302094399.html)). This was one of the most consequential strategic-vendor endorsements in 3D sensing — a marquee Western incumbent designating a Chinese company as its anointed successor.

**2024 — NVIDIA Isaac integration.** On June 3, 2024 Orbbec announced that its **Gemini 330** active-stereo series was integrated with NVIDIA's Isaac Perceptor stack for AMRs, becoming an NVIDIA Preferred Partner for robotics 3D perception. At ROSCon 2024 in October, Orbbec launched a turnkey out-of-the-box AMR developer kit (OPDK) preloaded with Isaac Perceptor and a GMSL2/FAKRA Gemini 335Lg variant for industrial robotic arms ([Orbbec ROSCon 2024 announcement](https://www.orbbec.com/news/orbbec-launches-out-of-the-box-amr-solution-with-nvidia-isaac-perceptor-at-roscon-2024/); [NVIDIA developer blog](https://developer.nvidia.com/)). This positioned Orbbec as the default Chinese alternative to Intel RealSense inside the NVIDIA robotics ecosystem.

**2024 — Foshan manufacturing plant online; 2024 FY revenue +56.79%.** Manufacturing capacity expanded with the Foshan plant; revenue rose to RMB 564.46 million from RMB 360.01 million, narrowing net loss to RMB -62.91 million from RMB -275.88 million (Source: 2025 年年度报告摘要, p. 13, cninfo SSE:688322). R&D intensity remained extremely high — 36.20% of revenue.

**April 20, 2025 — Tiangong Ultra wins Beijing humanoid half-marathon.** X-Humanoid's Tiangong Ultra, the winner of Beijing's first humanoid half-marathon, used Orbbec depth cameras for visual perception. This was a high-visibility validation event for the embodied-AI narrative.

**2025 — first profitable year + share-price re-rating.** FY2025 revenue **RMB 940.74 million (+66.66% YoY)**, net income attributable to parent **RMB 127.91 million (+303.3%)**, pre-tax profit **RMB 118.83 million (+281.6%)** — the first full-year profit since founding (Source: 2025 年年度报告摘要, p. 13, cninfo SSE:688322). Operating cash flow turned positive at RMB 82.72 million from -RMB 86.34 million in 2024. R&D intensity moderated from 36.20% to 21.53% as the revenue base scaled, but absolute R&D spend continued to grow. The stock re-rated dramatically on the embodied-AI / humanoid thesis: more than 300% gain over the year, peaking at RMB 103/share in February 2026 and pushing market cap to roughly RMB 40 billion (~US$5.5 billion). Three robotics-themed ETFs (华夏中证机器人 ETF, 易方达国证机器人产业 ETF, 永赢先进制造) appeared in the top-ten shareholder list — a clear marker of thematic-passive demand (Source: 2025 年年度报告摘要, p. 14, cninfo SSE:688322; 2026 年第一季度报告, p. 5).

**August 2025 — NVIDIA Jetson Thor partnership.** Orbbec announced full Gemini 330 compatibility with NVIDIA Jetson Thor, the next-generation humanoid-robot compute platform. The partnership was expanded at CES 2026 in January 2026, alongside the launch of an ultra-compact **Gemini 305** for wrist-mounted robotic arms and the unveiling of a **China-Vietnam dual manufacturing hub** ([Orbbec Jetson Thor news](https://www.orbbec.com/news/humanoid-robots-see-smarter-orbbec-3d-cameras-are-integrated-with-nvidia-jetson-thor/); [Orbbec CES 2026 coverage](https://www.orbbec.com/company/news/)).

**Lock-up unlock events.** July 7, 2025 marked the 36-month founder/insider unlock, releasing 34 million shares (8.48% of float) to six Zhuhai-LP concert parties — though directors/officers who held indirectly had personal lock-up extended to January 7, 2026 ([上海证券报, June 27 2025](https://paper.cnstock.com/)). A further 26.44 million shares (6.59%) unlocked in January 2026 ([Sina, Dec 26 2025](https://sina.com.cn/)). Ant Group's Shanghai Yunxin Venture Capital trimmed 12 million shares in Q1 2025 for RMB 723 million cash, taking its stake from ~13% pre-IPO down to 9.18% by year-end 2025 (Source: 2025 年年度报告摘要, p. 14).

**Q1 2026 — sustained momentum.** Revenue RMB 202.89 million (+6.19% YoY); non-GAAP NI +531% to RMB 21.76 million; direct overseas sales +80%; domestic robotics revenue 3× prior-year (Source: 2026 年第一季度报告, pp. 1-2, cninfo SSE:688322). Recent partnership wins from this period include the HONOR humanoid debut at MWC 2026 (using Gemini 330-series with the MX6800 ASIC), a Basler industrial-vision partnership at LogiMAT 2026, a LionsBot cleaning-robot deal, and Robbyant's LingBot-Depth spatial-perception foundation model trained on Gemini 330 data (March-April 2026, [Orbbec newsroom](https://www.orbbec.com/company/news/)).

The arc is straightforward: 2013 founding by a returnee optical-metrology academic → Kinect-replacement opportunity → Alipay anchor customer + Ant strategic round → 6-tech-path build-out and own-ASIC roadmap → loss-making STAR IPO in 2022 → Microsoft Kinect handoff + NVIDIA Isaac partnership in 2023-24 → first profit + embodied-AI re-rating in 2025-26.

## 3. MANAGEMENT TEAM

### 黄源浩 Huang Yuanhao — Founder, Chairman, CEO, Core Technical Personnel

The defining figure at Orbbec. Born 1980 in Chaozhou, Guangdong; PRC nationality with Hong Kong permanent residency. BS Peking University; MS National University of Singapore; PhD **City University of Hong Kong** in optical metrology. Postdoctoral positions at Hong Kong Polytechnic University (under Michael Y. Y. Hung, a pioneer of laser-speckle interferometry), Ryerson, CUHK, and the **Singapore-MIT Alliance for Research and Technology (SMART) Center** under MIT's George Barbastathis ([Baidu Baike](https://baike.baidu.com/item/奥比中光科技集团股份有限公司/54195925); [GDToday founder profile](https://www.newsgd.com/node_d36b0ef83f/63c7d5ab3f.shtml)). Published 20+ papers in *Optics Letters* and similar journals; designated a "National Talent Plan Expert"; founded Orbbec in January 2013.

Huang's economic interest is **27.15% directly + 8.49% via six Zhuhai concert-party LPs** (奥比中芯/中瑞/中鑫/中欣/中诚/中泰) — approximately **35.64% total**. With 5× A-class voting, his **voting interest is 60.18% alone, 76.78% with concert parties** — unambiguous controlling shareholder (Source: 2025 年年度报告摘要, pp. 15-17, cninfo SSE:688322). Forbes estimated his net worth at approximately US$1.4 billion in September 2025, rising to ~US$1.6 billion by mid-January 2026 on the embodied-AI rally ([VnExpress / Forbes](https://e.vnexpress.net/news/tech/personalities/how-chinese-engineer-howard-huang-become-billionaire-supplying-robotic-eyes-5070582.html)). At thirteen years of tenure he combines high technical depth (still authors patents, reviews chip architecture) with uncontested operating control — both a strength (decisive prioritisation, technical credibility with customers) and a risk (key-person dependency, see Section 9).

### 肖振中 Xiao Zhenzhong — Chief Technology Officer, Core Technical Personnel

Long-tenured deputy to Huang on the technical side; co-founder-era hire. Background in optical metrology and structured-light algorithms — participated in MOST "863" program projects and NSFC-funded research, and co-authored the China national standard "Optical 3D Geometric Measurement Specification for Forging/Stamping Parts and Their Dies" — Orbbec was the lead contributor to China's first national standard for optical 3D coordinate measurement systems, finalised July 2025 ([Orbbec national-standard announcement, Jul 10 2025](https://www.orbbec.com/company/news/)). Xiao directly holds 9.6 million shares (2.39% of capital, 1.32% of votes), making him the largest non-founder, non-Ant insider and the most heavily exposed executive after Huang to share-price performance (Source: 2025 年年度报告摘要, p. 14, cninfo SSE:688322). As CTO he oversees the R&D organisation across the depth-engine ASIC program, optics, algorithms, and the OrbbecSDK platform (with Windows/Linux/Android/macOS, ROS1/ROS2, OpenCV, and NVIDIA Isaac Sim integrations) — the cross-stack technical breadth that the company highlights as its core moat (Source: 2025 年年度报告摘要, p. 11; [Orbbec technology page](https://www.orbbec.com/company/technology/)).

### 陈彬 Chen Bin — Director, Chief Financial Officer, Head of Accounting

Born 1984. Joined Orbbec in approximately 2017-2018 as VP-Finance and Board Secretary before being promoted to CFO; currently holds the joint titles of Director, Chief Financial Officer (财务总监), and Head of Accounting (会计机构负责人), making him the senior finance principal across the consolidated group (Source: 2026 年第一季度报告, p. 10 — Chen Bin signs as both 主管会计工作负责人 and 会计机构负责人; [Baidu Baike Chen Bin entry](https://baike.baidu.com/item/陈彬/62240356)). He has represented Orbbec at the 2023 China Sensor & Application Technology Conference (Nov 12, 2023), giving a keynote on the industrialisation of 3D vision — an unusual public posture for a CFO and a tell that he sits closer to the strategy/IR axis than a traditional finance executive (Qianzhan.com bio). Chen has stewarded Orbbec through the STAR IPO, two follow-on placement attempts (one withdrawn, one twice-reduced and ultimately completed in early 2026 for approximately RMB 1.5 billion), and the 2025 turn-to-profit — including the transition off Tianjian CPA's qualified-attention disclosures around going-concern when losses were widest. His track record on cash management is strong: the company emerged from its loss years with RMB 1.84 billion in liquidity and de minimis debt (Source: 2026 年第一季度报告, pp. 8-10).

### 梅小露 Mei Xiaolu — Senior Vice President, Core Technical Personnel (Chief Chip Architect)

The least public of the executive team but arguably the most strategically important after Huang and Xiao given the company's vertical-integration thesis. Prior career spans the **Chinese Academy of Sciences, IBM, and AMD**, where he worked on ARM, PowerPC, and x86 core silicon (Source: 招股说明书 prospectus; CICC sponsor reports). At Orbbec, Mei heads the **MX-series depth-engine ASIC** program — the chips that turn raw depth data from structured-light projectors and stereo modules into usable point-cloud and depth-map output. The MX6800 (current generation) is the silicon inside the Gemini 330 family and HONOR's first humanoid robot. Given the deepening US export-control regime on advanced semiconductors (see Section 9), the ability to design and tape out a custom depth-engine ASIC at mature nodes through domestic foundries is a defensible technological asset that competitors relying on merchant silicon (Sony iToF imagers, Infineon/PMD chips, NVIDIA Jetson modules) cannot match for cost or sovereignty.

### Other senior officers

陈挚 (Chen Zhi) — Vice President, strategy/business development; has represented the company on the financing circuit since 2018 (e.g. 21世纪经济报道 capital forum October 2018 talk on staged financing for hardware startups). 靳尚 (Jin Shang) — Board Secretary (董秘), born 1987, holds a master's degree; previously head of board office and securities-affairs representative at Shenzhen Baoying Construction Holdings (深圳市宝鹰建设控股集团) (Source: 2025 年年度报告摘要, p. 4, cninfo SSE:688322).

### Board composition and governance

The board was reconstituted in October 2023; next election due October 2026. **Nine directors including three independents** — a standard STAR-Market composition. Huang is Chairman; Chen Bin sits on the board. The three independent directors chair the Audit Committee, Compensation & Nomination Committee, and Strategy Committee. **Tianjian CPA (天健会计师事务所, special general partnership)** has audited Orbbec continuously through the loss years and the 2024 audit fee was RMB 950,000 — a continuation arrangement, not a recent rotation (Source: 2024 年度报告 annex; [Sina Finance, Apr 22 2025](https://finance.sina.com.cn/)). Tianjian issued a **standard unqualified opinion** on the 2025 financials — a meaningful milestone after years of going-concern attention given accumulated losses still total **RMB 1.116 billion** at the parent-company level (Source: 2025 年年度报告摘要, p. 2, cninfo SSE:688322).

**Insider ownership and concert parties.** Founder side controls 35.64% of equity and 76.78% of votes. With three robotics-ETFs in the top-10 holders (华夏 / 易方达 / 永赢), Ant's Shanghai Yunxin at 9.18%, 周广大 at 6.72%, and the six Zhuhai LPs at 8.49%, free float available to passive flows is narrow — explaining the magnitude of the 2025-2026 share-price move on relatively modest ETF buying.

**Governance flags.** (1) The dual-class 5× voting wedge gives Huang absolute control even after substantial founder selling. (2) **Ant Group customer + shareholder overlap** prompted a CSRC inquiry at IPO; concentration has reduced as Alipay face-payment volumes plateaued and Ant trims its stake. (3) **Founder-era insider selling** — multiple pre-IPO shareholders announced disposal plans within 24 months of IPO while loss-making, drawing critical press in late 2024 ([Jiemian](https://www.jiemian.com/)). (4) **2023-2025 restricted-stock plan concentration** to only ~5 senior executives generating >5× paper gains. None disqualifying, but standard checklist items for STAR-listed founder-controlled tech.

## 4. PRODUCTS & SERVICES

Orbbec's catalogue spans more than thirty distinct sensor, camera-computer, and LiDAR SKUs organised across five technology families. A walk of [orbbec.com](https://www.orbbec.com) and [orbbec.com.cn](https://www.orbbec.com.cn) on May 16, 2026 enumerated the following lineup.

### A. Stereo-vision cameras — Gemini family

The current flagship line and largest revenue contributor. All Gemini cameras use Orbbec's in-house MX6800 ASIC and run both active and passive stereo modes. **Gemini 305 / 305g / 345Lg** (CES 2026 launches) — ultra-compact and global-shutter / long-range variants for wrist-mounted arms and AMRs. **Gemini 215** — consumer/AIoT compact (CES 2025). **Gemini 2 / 2 L / 2 XL** — high-volume base SKU (0.15-10 m range, 1280×800@30 fps depth, IMU, USB-C, <2.5 W) plus long-range and wide-FOV variants; adopted by a top-3 Japanese automaker for a hospital-logistics robot in August 2025. The **Gemini 330 series** (335, 336, 335L, 336L, 335Lg, 335Le, 435Le) is the modular workhorse family — 335 base, 336 with enhanced IR for reflective/transparent surfaces, 335L/336L long-range IP65, 335Lg with **GMSL2/FAKRA automotive serial interfaces**, 335Le with Gigabit Ethernet + PoE / IP67, and 435Le the long-range industrial variant (0.31-20 m+, ±1% at 2 m, <8 W) launched at Automate 2025 ([Orbbec products page](https://www.orbbec.com/products/)).

**Competitive-advantage verdict: yes — partial-to-strong moat.** Moat: **technology / IP** (in-house MX6800 ASIC eliminates merchant-silicon dependency) + **cost leadership** (Gemini 335 ~US$250 vs. Intel RealSense D435i ~US$350, [OpenELAB benchmark](https://openelab.io/blogs/learn/comparing-3d-vision-technology-orbbec-gemini-335-vs-intel-realsense-d435i)) + **ecosystem lock-in** (NVIDIA Isaac Perceptor preferred partner, OrbbecSDK with ROS1/ROS2, Jetson Thor integration). Closest competing product: **Intel RealSense D435i / D455** — at parity on optical performance, ahead on cost, ahead on roadmap velocity (5 new Gemini SKUs in 12 months).

### B. iToF time-of-flight cameras — Femto family

**Femto Bolt** — compact iToF as Azure Kinect DK replacement (depth 1024×1024@15 fps WFOV / 640×576@30 fps NFOV; 4K@30 fps RGB with HDR). **Femto Mega** — same sensor with embedded NVIDIA Jetson Nano; USB-C and PoE+. **Femto Mega I** — industrial ruggedised. **Femto W / Femto** — legacy SKUs ([Orbbec Femto pages](https://www.orbbec.com/products/tof-camera/femto-bolt/)).

## 6. INDUSTRY OVERVIEW

### Industry definition

Orbbec sits at the centre of the **3D vision perception industry** — the design and manufacture of optical sensors that capture three-dimensional information about people, objects, and space, distinct from traditional 2D machine-vision cameras. The relevant industry classification under China's standard is **C39 — computer, communications, and other electronic equipment manufacturing** (Source: 2025 年年度报告摘要, p. 5, cninfo SSE:688322). Adjacent industries include machine vision (largely 2D), LiDAR (single-task ranging), AR/VR optics, and embedded edge AI compute. The 3D-vision sub-industry spans six technical paths — monocular structured light, binocular structured light, indirect ToF (iToF), direct ToF (dToF), active/passive stereo, and mechanical/solid-state LiDAR — and Orbbec is one of the few companies that has commercialised all six.

### Market sizing

The most-cited authoritative analyst on this market is **Yole Group**, whose "3D Imaging & Sensing 2025" report — referenced by Orbbec itself in its 2025 年度报告摘要 — forecasts the **global 3D vision sensing market reaching approximately US$17.2 billion by 2028** (Source: 2025 年年度报告摘要, p. 5, cninfo SSE:688322; [Yole Group, "3D Imaging & Sensing 2025"](https://www.yolegroup.com/product/report/3d-imaging-and-sensing-2025/)). Yole's longer-dated outlook envisions **>US$18 billion by 2030**, with key drivers in automotive, industrial LiDAR, security/biometrics, and robotics.

Other third-party estimates show substantial methodological dispersion: [Precedence Research](https://www.precedenceresearch.com/3d-camera-market) US$7.21 bn in 2024 → US$35.25 bn by 2034 at 17.2% CAGR; [Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/3d-machine-vision-market) US$7.16 bn in 2024 → US$15.6 bn by 2030 at 14.3% CAGR (3D machine vision specifically); [Mordor Intelligence](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/3d-camera-market) US$26.66 bn in 2024 at 23.0% CAGR — likely including smartphone modules. For modelling, the Yole anchor (US$17.2 bn by 2028) is most defensible.

### High-growth pockets

The single steepest-slope sub-segment is **3D vision sensors for embodied-intelligent robots**, projected by [Statifacts](https://www.statifacts.com/outlook/3d-vision-sensor-for-embodied-intelligent-robots-market) at **US$104 million in 2024 growing to US$6.89 billion in 2034 — a 52.1% CAGR**. Yole's humanoid-robot forecast is consistent: US$6 billion by 2030 growing to US$51 billion by 2035 (~55% CAGR), with humanoid shipments rising from a few thousand in 2024 to ~136,000 in 2030 and 2+ million by 2035, and humanoids alone projected to take ~25% of the total 3D vision sensor market ([Yole humanoid forecast via Edge AI Vision Alliance, June 2025](https://www.edge-ai-vision.com/2025/06/humanoid-robots-to-have-a-14-fold-market-expansion-in-5-years/)).

Adjacent pockets: **embodied AI total market** US$4.44 bn (2025) → US$23.06 bn (2030) at 39.0% CAGR ([MarketsandMarkets](https://www.marketsandmarkets.com/Market-Reports/embodied-ai-market-83867232.html)); **face-recognition access control** US$3.23 bn → US$6.11 bn by 2032 at 9.7% CAGR ([Valuates](https://reports.valuates.com/market-reports/QYRE-Auto-2F9522/global-facial-recognition-access-control-solutions)); **in-cabin sensing** with EU GSR mandate tailwind ([IDTechEx](https://www.idtechex.com/en/research-report/in-cabin-sensing/972)); **AR/VR** — Vision Pro normalising dToF + structured-light coexistence ([TechInsights](https://www.techinsights.com/blog/lidar-technology-apple-vision-pro)).

### China-specific environment

Orbbec benefits from an unusual concentration of policy tailwinds. The 2025 年度报告摘要 cites supportive central-government documents including the 15th Five-Year Plan recommendation, the "AI+ Implementation Opinions", the Future Industries Innovation Opinions, and the New-Generation AI Development Plan (Source: 2025 年年度报告摘要, p. 5, cninfo SSE:688322). In November 2023 MIIT issued the **Humanoid Robot Innovation Development Guiding Opinions** targeting humanoid commercialisation by 2027; in March 2025 Shenzhen issued its **2025-2027 Embodied-Intelligence Robot Action Plan** targeting 10+ companies valued over RMB 10 bn and 20+ over RMB 1 bn revenue in the city by 2027 (Source: 2025 年年度报告摘要, p. 9). At the macro level China's 2025 central S&T budget was ~**RMB 398 bn (+10% YoY)** ([Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/china-to-spend-usd55-billion-on-r-and-d-in-2025-semiconductor-ai-and-quantum-computing-fields-to-benefit)), and Big Fund Phase III committed US$47.5 bn in May 2024 to chip self-sufficiency including image sensors ([Atlantic Council](https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/in-depth-research-reports/issue-brief/reprogramming-the-future-the-specialized-semiconductors-reshaping-the-global-supply-chain/)).

### Industry structure and dynamics

The 3D-vision-sensing supply chain has three tiers: **upstream** component suppliers (laser diodes, VCSELs, DOEs, image sensors, special compute silicon); **midstream** vision-solution vendors who design the sensor module, depth-engine ASIC, software, and SDK (where Orbbec sits, alongside RealSense, Stereolabs, Lucid, LIPS, Photoneo); and **downstream** application customers (robot OEMs, payment-system integrators, fitness brands). The industry has **high technical barriers** — Orbbec characterises the requirements as "full-stack technical R&D capability spanning system design, chip design, algorithm R&D, optics, software, and volume manufacturing" — and **moderate capital intensity** (low single-digit-billion-RMB lifetime investments to scale a fabless 3D-sensor business to revenue parity with Orbbec's current run-rate) (Source: 2025 年年度报告摘要, p. 10-11, cninfo SSE:688322).

The competitive structure is **moderately consolidating** in 2024-2026. Microsoft discontinued Kinect for Azure (October 2023). Intel spun out RealSense in July 2025 with a US$50 million Series A ([Intel Capital release](https://www.intelcapital.com/realsense-completes-spin-out-from-intel-raises-50-million-to-accelerate-ai-powered-vision-for-robotics-and-biometrics/)). Zebra Technologies acquired Photoneo (announced December 2024, closed Q1 2025) ([Zebra press release](https://www.zebra.com/us/en/about-zebra/newsroom/press-releases/2024/zebra-technologies-to-acquire-photoneo-expanding-its-portfolio-of-3d-machine-vision-solutions.html)). Ouster (LiDAR) acquired Stereolabs in February 2026 ([The Robot Report coverage](https://www.therobotreport.com/)). This means **three of the four Western pure-play 3D-vision competitors changed status in the 18 months ending Q1 2026** — a structural opening for a well-capitalised independent like Orbbec.

### Tech-segment trends

Per [Yole 2025](https://www.yolegroup.com/product/report/3d-imaging-and-sensing-2025/) and corroborating industry primers ([Revopoint](https://www.revopoint3d.com/comparing-three-prevalent-3d-imaging-technologies-tof-structured-light-and-binocular-stereo-vision/); [e-con Systems](https://www.e-consystems.com/blog/camera/technology/what-are-depth-sensing-cameras-how-do-they-work/)): structured light remains dominant in near-range biometrics; iToF dominates in automotive in-cabin and short-mid-range robotics; dToF (SPAD-based) wins in long-range and AR/VR LiDAR (iPhone Pro, Vision Pro); active stereo is the cheapest path and the bulk of AMR / outdoor robotics ASPs. ToF is gaining traction in navigation; structured light remains critical for biometric.

### Pricing dynamics

ASPs are falling in commodity robotics-stereo (Orbbec ~30% below Intel for comparable parts) but rising in the embodied-AI premium tier — humanoid OEMs are willing to pay for calibrated, synchronised RGB+depth+IMU modules. In-cabin auto iToF modules sell at **US$20-40 at 1,000-unit volumes** ([Edge AI Vision Alliance, March 2024](https://www.edge-ai-vision.com/2024/03/the-integration-of-dms-and-oms-offers-advancements-for-in-cabin-monitoring/)); industrial structured-light scanners like Photoneo MotionCam-3D and Mech-Eye remain US$5,000-15,000+. Orbbec spans this entire range from low-end OEM modules to industrial flagship units.

### Positioning framework

On a **price / features / scale** matrix:
- **Price tier**: Orbbec (Gemini ~US$250) and Percipio (~US$200-400) anchor the **low-cost commodity** quadrant; Intel RealSense (~US$350) is mid-tier; Stereolabs (~US$450) and Lucid (~US$1,500-3,000) are premium / industrial; Photoneo / Mech-Mind / Sony industrial (US$5,000-15,000+) are flagship.
- **Tech breadth**: Orbbec uniquely covers **all six 3D-sensing technical paths** (structured light, iToF, dToF, stereo, LiDAR, industrial metrology); most competitors specialise in 1-2 paths.
- **Scale**: Orbbec's 5+ million cumulative sensor shipments and 3,000+ customers/1,000+ robotics OEMs is among the largest installed bases globally outside of the smartphone-integrated stack (Apple TrueDepth, etc.). RealSense's "60% of AMRs/humanoids" claim is the only strictly comparable scale claim.

### Competitive advantages

(1) **Vertical integration through to depth-engine silicon.** The MX6800 ASIC and the lineage from MX400 onwards is a 12-year, multi-generation chip program — competitors that depend on merchant silicon (Jetson, Sony imagers, Infineon iToF) cannot match Orbbec's cost structure or sovereignty profile under the US export-control regime.
(2) **Microsoft Azure Kinect handoff.** No other 3D-vision company has been designated by Microsoft as the official successor product — a unique brand asset.
(3) **NVIDIA Isaac preferred partnership.** First Chinese 3D-vision vendor to achieve this; co-engineered the Gemini 335Lg with GMSL2/FAKRA at NVIDIA's request.
(4) **Breadth × volume × cost combination.** Few vendors can profitably serve both a Pollen Robotics academic-research SI (volume <100, ASP ~US$500) and an Alipay payment-terminal OEM (volume >10,000, ASP <$5 per module). Orbbec's six-tech-path catalog and own-ASIC cost structure makes this possible.
(5) **Domestic regulatory + policy alignment.** A-share-listed status, Shenzhen embodied-AI cluster designation, and inclusion in the Hurun China AI Top-20 (the sole pure-play robotics-vision firm) all bias domestic Chinese humanoid and AMR OEMs toward Orbbec.

### Competitive vulnerabilities

(1) **Geopolitical / export-control exposure.** As tensions over US-China semiconductor trade continue, Orbbec faces potential exclusion from US federal-procurement-adjacent customers and from defence-grade robotics, even though current revenue exposure is low.
(2) **Tesla Optimus camera-only architecture.** If Tesla's vision-only Full Self-Driving paradigm proves applicable to humanoids, the entire depth-camera TAM for humanoids could be smaller than embodied-AI bulls assume ([interestingengineering.com on Optimus](https://interestingengineering.com/innovation/comparing-boston-dynamics-atlas-optimus-humanoid-robots)).
(3) **Concentrated downstream demand.** Robotics revenue is concentrated in a small number of Chinese humanoid programmes; if NVIDIA's Jetson Thor or RealSense aggressively price-cuts, Orbbec's share could be squeezed.
(4) **Limited brand strength outside of OEM channel.** Orbbec's consumer-facing brand recognition is weak relative to Intel RealSense (legacy) and Apple TrueDepth — limiting pricing power.
(5) **Founder concentration / dual-class.** Limits external strategic interest and may cap multiple expansion.

### Market share

Orbbec claims approximately **70%+ share of China's service-robot 3D-vision market** (company "About" page) and — independently verified by **Interact Analysis** — approximately **72% of Korea's mobile-robot 3D-vision market in 2024** ([Orbbec Interact Analysis citation, Oct 20 2025](https://www.orbbec.com/news/)). Global share of all 3D-sensing is in the low single digits given the dominance of smartphone-integrated structured-light volumes (Apple alone ships ~200 million TrueDepth modules annually).

## 8. MARKET OPPORTUNITY (TAM)

### TAM, SAM, SOM

**TAM — global 3D vision sensing**: Yole projects **US$17.2 bn by 2028** reaching **>US$18 bn by 2030** (Source: 2025 年年度报告摘要, p. 5, cninfo SSE:688322; [Yole](https://www.yolegroup.com/product/report/3d-imaging-and-sensing-2025/)). A meaningful share of TAM is captured by smartphone-integrated structured-light (Apple TrueDepth), from which Orbbec is structurally excluded. Excluding smartphone, the merchant 3D-vision-sensor + LiDAR + 3D-camera-module market Orbbec can serve is roughly **US$8-10 bn by 2028-2030**.

**SAM — robotics, biometrics, industrial 3D, AR/VR, automotive in-cabin 3D**: combining embodied-intelligent-robots (US$6.89 bn by 2034 per Statifacts), face-recognition access control (US$6.11 bn by 2032), industrial 3D machine vision sensor/module layer (US$3-4 bn within Grand View's US$15.6 bn total), and AR/VR / in-cabin depth — Orbbec's SAM converges to **US$8-12 bn by 2030**, with embodied-AI fastest at 50%+ CAGR.

**SOM**: at current revenue of approximately US$130 million (RMB 940 M at ~7.2 RMB/USD), Orbbec captures 1-2% of SAM. Continuing 40-70% growth for four to six more years implies revenue of **US$1-2 billion by 2030**, or 8-15% share of SAM — putting Orbbec at the mid-cap merchant 3D-sensing scale of post-spinout RealSense. Within that range, the share-of-share-shift mechanic is what investors are actually paying for: Yole humanoid-sensor unit demand grows ~55% CAGR while merchant supply consolidates.

### Growth trajectory

The serviceable-market growth rate is materially above 3D-sensing TAM growth because Orbbec is disproportionately exposed to the fastest-growing sub-segments: **embodied-AI 3D vision sensors (52.1% CAGR per Statifacts)**, **humanoid robot sensor BOM (Yole: ~55% CAGR to 2030-2035 with humanoids taking ~25% of total 3D vision sensor market)**, and **NVIDIA-platform-anchored AMR perception**. By contrast, Orbbec's exposure to the slower-growing pockets — 3D face-payment (mature, single-digit growth), legacy industrial 3D machine vision (~14% CAGR) — is shrinking as a share of revenue.

### Penetration strategy

Three interlocking strategies are visible in 2025-2026 filings and announcements:
1. **NVIDIA ecosystem ride** — Isaac Perceptor preferred-partner status, Jetson Thor humanoid integration, GMSL2/FAKRA Gemini variants designed in collaboration with NVIDIA and Connect Tech. This positions Orbbec as the default Chinese sensor option for any robot built on the NVIDIA compute stack.
2. **International channel buildout** — direct overseas sales +80% YoY in Q1 2026 (Source: 2026 年第一季度报告, p. 2), five new distributors added in 2025, deepening Korea (72% share already), Japan (top-3 automaker hospital-logistics win August 2025; iREX 2025 Tokyo showcase), Germany (Basler partnership March 2026, LogiMAT and ProMat presence), and US (Automate, MODEX 2026, ROSCon).
3. **China-Vietnam dual manufacturing hub** — announced at CES 2026 in January 2026. Diversifies geopolitical risk and positions Orbbec for direct supply into Southeast Asian and (selectively) US logistics-robotics customers without China-of-origin tariff/regulatory exposure.

### Competitive context

With three Western pure-play competitors changing status in 18 months (Photoneo → Zebra Dec 2024, RealSense → Intel spinout July 2025, Stereolabs → Ouster Feb 2026, plus Microsoft Azure Kinect handed to Orbbec Oct 2023), Orbbec is the **only large independent, publicly listed, pure-play 3D-vision company** — and the only one in China. The risk is the mirror: a humanoid mega-buyer (Figure, 1X, or Tesla vertically integrating its own depth pipeline) doing a strategic supplier deal with RealSense or going camera-only would dent the TAM thesis.

## 9. RISK ASSESSMENT

### Company-Specific Risks

**Execution risk — humanoid revenue scale-up.** Orbbec's valuation prices in significant humanoid sensor revenue (3-5× revenue uplift over five years). Achieving that requires winning humanoid OEMs and volume-ramping at acceptable margin against price-sensitive Chinese programmes. Q1 2026 domestic robotics revenue tripled YoY (Source: 2026 年第一季度报告, p. 2), but humanoid unit volumes remain small (Yole: 136,000 cumulative humanoid shipments only by 2030), so per-design-win concentration is high.

**Customer concentration — Alipay legacy + Ant overhang.** Ant Group remains the second-largest shareholder (9.18%) and was historically the second-largest customer. Continued share-trims (RMB 723 M sold in Q1 2025; further unlocks through 2026) create technical pressure on the stock. Mitigant: non-controlling stake; customer base substantially more diversified than in 2018.

**Key-person dependency on founder.** Huang Yuanhao is Chairman, CEO, controlling shareholder (60.18% of votes), and core technical personnel. His combination of optical-metrology academic credibility and operating track record is irreplaceable near-term. Mitigant: long-tenured deputies (CTO Xiao Zhenzhong since founding, SVP Mei Xiaolu, CFO Chen Bin); no obvious internal CEO successor signalled.

**Technology obsolescence — vision-only paradigm risk.** Tesla's FSD pioneered "cameras + neural networks, no depth sensor." If embodied AI converges on vision-only for humanoids (Tesla Optimus is camera-only), the depth-camera TAM for humanoids could be smaller than bulls assume. Mitigant: most non-Tesla humanoid programmes (Figure, 1X, Agility, Boston Dynamics, Unitree, HONOR) specify dedicated depth sensors.

**Manufacturing concentration.** The China-Vietnam dual hub (January 2026) addresses this prospectively, but bulk of 2025-2026 production remains at the Foshan plant — single-site disruption would have outsized impact.

**Governance — dual-class entrenchment.** The 5× A-class voting wedge gives Huang absolute control even after substantial founder selling — a clarity-of-control positive, but it limits external strategic interest and may cap multiple expansion relative to one-share-one-vote peers.

### Industry / Market Risks

**Competitive intensity — RealSense post-spinout.** Now standalone and well-capitalised (US$50 million Series A in July 2025 with Intel Capital and MediaTek participation), RealSense has reasons to aggressively defend its claimed 60% AMR/humanoid share. Competitive price-cuts at the US$200-300 price point could compress Orbbec gross margins. Mitigant: Orbbec's own-ASIC cost structure provides headroom; the Microsoft Azure Kinect endorsement is a brand asset RealSense cannot replicate.

**Regulatory — US export controls and entity-list risk.** The expanded BIS Foreign Direct Product Rule (FDPR), the December 2024 addition of 140 Chinese entities to the entity list, and the January 2025 AI Diffusion Framework collectively constrain Chinese semiconductor companies' access to leading-edge foundry processes ([Congress.gov R48642](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/R48642); [BIS press release](https://www.bis.gov/press-release/department-commerce-revises-license-review-policy-semiconductors-exported-china)). Although Orbbec has not been added to any entity list, the risk of being named — given its NVIDIA Jetson Thor humanoid partnership and dual-use vision applications — is non-trivial. Mitigant: depth-engine ASICs use mature nodes available from domestic Chinese foundries.

**Technology disruption — neural-radiance-field 3D / monocular depth-from-AI.** Rapid advances in monocular-depth-from-AI (NeRF, Gaussian Splatting, monocular depth foundation models trained on internet-scale 2D imagery) raise the possibility that high-quality 3D perception can be obtained from low-cost RGB cameras plus AI compute, reducing the per-robot demand for dedicated depth sensors. Mitigant: latency, power efficiency, low-light performance, and direct-measurement reliability still favour dedicated depth sensors for safety-critical robotics.

**Market saturation — face-payment plateau.** Domestic face-payment adoption has plateaued as QR-code payments retain dominance for low-friction retail. Continued growth in this segment is constrained. Mitigant: this segment is a declining share of Orbbec's revenue mix.

### Financial Risks

**Profitability sustainability.** 2025 was the first profitable year; non-GAAP net income (excluding government subsidies and investment income) was RMB 70.88 million on RMB 940.74 million revenue — an 7.5% non-GAAP margin. R&D intensity is moderating (36.20% in 2024 → 21.53% in 2025 → 26.36% in Q1 2026) but remains very high; small revenue-growth misses could push the company back to non-GAAP losses. Mitigant: Q1 2026 non-GAAP NI rose 531% YoY confirming operating leverage is real.

**Accumulated losses constrain capital actions.** Parent-company accumulated losses of RMB 1.116 billion as of year-end 2025 prevent cash dividends until those losses are recovered (Source: 2025 年年度报告摘要, p. 2, cninfo SSE:688322). The company executed an RMB 48 million share buyback in 2025 — likely the only realistic near-term capital return.

**Foreign-currency exposure.** With direct overseas sales now ~80% growth rate and rising, RMB strength against USD/EUR/JPY/KRW would compress reported revenue. Q1 2026 FX impact on cash was -RMB 697,000 on a small base; this exposure will grow.

### Macroeconomic Risks

**China economic cyclicality.** Industrial-automation capex and service-robot deployment are pro-cyclical. A sharper-than-expected slowdown in Chinese consumer or industrial activity (currently mid-cycle) would compress robotics-segment revenue.

**US-China geopolitical risk.** Beyond export controls, tariff escalation could reduce Orbbec's competitiveness in US-headquartered customers (already a key driver for the Vietnam manufacturing hub). The trajectory of US-China tech-decoupling is the single largest exogenous variable. Mitigant: dual manufacturing footprint, NVIDIA ecosystem alignment, Microsoft endorsement, and direct presence in Korea, Japan, EU partially de-risk.

**Interest-rate sensitivity and asset re-rating.** Orbbec trades on a high-multiple growth-stock framework; a sustained rise in global discount rates would compress the multiple. The stock's >300% 2025 move came in a falling-rate environment.
