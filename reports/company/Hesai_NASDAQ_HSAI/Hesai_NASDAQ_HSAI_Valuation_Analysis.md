## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Hesai is the global volume leader in automotive-grade lidar (1.62 million units shipped in FY2025, ~3× the closest pure-play peer) and the only listed pure-play lidar maker to print a positive full-year GAAP profit (US$62M FY2025 net income). We initiate coverage with a **BUY** rating and a **US$28 / ADS 12-month price target**.

The thesis is operating leverage: revenue grew 46% in FY2025 with non-GAAP net margin of 18.2%, and management guides FY2026 unit shipments to 3.0–3.5 million (~85–115% volume growth) with a target production capacity of 4 million+ units. Our base-case projection (built bottom-up in the model) has revenue compounding at a **31% CAGR FY25–FY30** to RMB 9,973 million (US$1,366M), gross margin steady at 43%, and net income reaching RMB 1,920 million (US$263M) by FY30E. EBITDA scales from RMB 343 million in FY25A to RMB 2,415 million (US$331M) in FY30E.

At the current US$22.44 / ADS, the market is paying ~57× FY25 GAAP P/E and 8.1× FY25 P/S. Forward, that compresses to **~33× FY27E** and **~22× FY28E** P/E. Re-rating to peer median (Mobileye 25× FY26 P/E; ASML 32×; TSM 22×) on FY28E earnings supports US$25–32 per ADS. The exit-multiple DCF (10–14× FY30E EBITDA) supports US$24–38 per ADS. Weighted blended target lands at **US$28**.

**Key catalysts (12-month):**

1. Q1 FY26 results (guided RMB 650–700M revenue, +24-33% YoY) — execution proof point against the FY26 3.0–3.5M unit guide.
2. Multi-lidar L3+ start-of-production at Li Auto, Xiaomi, Changan in 2H26–2027.
3. JT128 humanoid lidar ramp at Unitree, HONOR Robot, Galbot — base case 70K units in FY26 vs. 12K in FY25.
4. Stock Connect inclusion of HK 2525 listing (eligibility expected after 6-month seasoning).
5. Potential favourable resolution of any residual 1260H actions and resumption of US OEM volume.

**Key risks to price target:**

1. China ADAS attach-rate plateau (-15% impact on PT if attach rate caps at ~25%).
2. Robosense undercutting on volume bids, compressing ADAS gross margin below 35% (-10%).
3. Renewed Section 1260H Chinese-military-company listing or Commerce entity-list action (-25%).
4. Tesla-style vision-only stacks gaining mind-share, structurally compressing lidar TAM (-15%).
5. China EV demand normalising as NEV subsidies expire (-10%).

### 2.3 Terminal value and EV

**Method A — Gordon perpetuity (g = 3.0%, WACC = 11.5%):**
- Terminal value = FCF₃₀ × (1+g) / (WACC − g) = 1,187,508 × 1.03 / 8.5% = **RMB 14,390 million**
- PV of TV = 14,390 × discount factor 0.5803 = **RMB 8,350 million**
- Sum PV of FY26–FY30 FCF = **RMB 1,430 million**
- **EV (Gordon) = RMB 9,780 million** → terminal value is 85% of EV (too sensitive)
- Equity = 9,780 + 7,536 (cash + investments) − 727 (debt) = RMB 16,589 million
- **Implied price = US$15.52 / ADS** (base); US$13.00 (bear at WACC 12.5%, g 2%); US$22.10 (bull at WACC 10.5%, g 3.5%)

**Method B — Exit multiple (10× FY30E EBITDA):**
- FY30E EBITDA = EBIT 1,945 + D&A 470 = **RMB 2,415 million** (US$331M)
- Terminal value = 2,415 × 10 = **RMB 24,149 million**
- PV of TV = 24,149 × 0.5803 = **RMB 14,013 million**
- **EV (Exit Mult) = RMB 15,442 million**
- Equity = 15,442 + 7,536 − 727 = RMB 22,252 million
- **Implied price = US$20.82 / ADS** (10× base); US$24.50 (12× mid); US$38.00 (14× bull)

### 3.3 Multiple selection and rationale

For Hesai we apply **EV/Revenue NTM and NTM+1** rather than EV/EBITDA, because (a) most lidar peers have negative EBITDA (the multiple is undefined), and (b) Hesai's margin trajectory is the most material valuation driver — using EV/EBITDA on FY28E captures that. We also include EV/EBITDA FY28E and forward P/E FY28E as secondary cross-checks.

**Discount/premium to peer median justification:**

- Hesai trades at **6.0× LTM EV/Rev vs lidar peer median 5.3×** — a 13% premium. Justified by (1) the only-profitable status, (2) the volume scale advantage (10×+ peer median), (3) the operating cash flow positive track record. We argue the premium should *widen* into 1.4–1.7× peer median over the next 12 months as the FY26 ramp validates the operating leverage story.
- Hesai's NTM EBITDA margin (12%) is the only positive number in the lidar peer set; the entire pure-play comp is "growth-at-any-cost" except Hesai.

### Why BUY

1. **First-mover profitability lock-in.** Hesai is the only pure-play lidar maker with positive GAAP net income — every other lidar peer is burning cash. This structural advantage compounds: profits fund R&D and capacity, accelerating the cost-curve gap vs. Robosense / Ouster / Innoviz.
2. **Operating leverage runway.** Modeled FY25 → FY28 net income CAGR of ~89% (from US$62M to US$168M) on revenue CAGR of ~38%. Each incremental RMB of revenue drops materially more to the bottom line as R&D % falls from 26% in FY25 to ~15% in FY28.
3. **Multi-lidar L3+ adoption.** Li Auto, Xiaomi, Changan have all confirmed multi-lidar (3–6 lidars per vehicle) L3+ programs with start of production in 2026–2027. This multiplies content/vehicle even as unit ASPs decline.
4. **Humanoid optionality.** JT128 design wins at Unitree (2026 China Spring Festival Gala), HONOR Robot, Galbot, Magiclab. Lawnmower backlog alone is >10M units. We model 70K humanoid units in FY26 (vs 12K FY25) growing to 800K by FY30 — visible upside if humanoid TAM materialises faster than the consensus 2030 forecast.
5. **Capital-markets de-risking.** Sept 2025 HK dual-primary listing brings ~RMB 4.4bn of fresh capital, eligibility for Stock Connect within 12 months, and reduces US-listing concentration risk.

### Why NOT a strong BUY

- 8.1× P/S already prices a meaningful re-rating.
- Single-SKU concentration (AT128 was 60.9% of FY24 revenue).
- US-China geopolitical overhang — a renewed 1260H listing would re-set the multiple by 25–35%.
- Competition from Robosense at the volume end is intensifying.
