## 1. DCF Valuation

### 1.1 Methodology

We model 5 years of explicit unlevered free cash flow (UFCF) from FY2026E through FY2030E, with a terminal value computed via the Gordon Growth model. Operating assumptions are derived from Cadence's FY2025 segment data and FY2026 revenue/margin guidance ([CDNS 10-K FY2025, MD&A and Selected Financial Data](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000813672/000081367226000016/cdns-20251231.htm); [CDNS Q1 FY2026 8-K — earnings release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000813672/000081367226000044/cdns04272026ex9901.htm); [CDNS Q1 FY2026 CFO commentary](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000813672/000081367226000044/cfocommentary04272026ex9902.htm)).

### 1.2 Cash Flow Build ($M)

The FY26E revenue base ($6,175M) sits at the midpoint of management's $6.125B–$6.225B FY2026 guide ([CDNS Q1 FY2026 8-K, raised FY2026 outlook](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000813672/000081367226000044/cdns04272026ex9901.htm)). Forward-year revenue compounds at ~13–14% — consistent with the trailing 5-year CAGR disclosed in [CDNS 10-K FY2025 — Selected Financial Data](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000813672/000081367226000016/cdns-20251231.htm) and the AI-driven demand commentary in the [Q1 FY2026 CFO Commentary](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000813672/000081367226000044/cfocommentary04272026ex9902.htm).

### 1.3 WACC Build

The risk-free rate uses the prevailing 10-yr UST yield ([U.S. Treasury Daily Yield Curve, 2026](https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?type=daily_treasury_yield_curve&field_tdr_date_value=2026)). Equity risk premium is set conservatively above Damodaran's January 2026 implied ERP of 4.23% to reflect mid-cycle conditions ([Damodaran, "Historical Implied Equity Risk Premiums", 2026](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/datafile/histimpl.html); [Damodaran, "ERP: Determinants, Estimates and Implications — 2026 Edition", SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6361419)). Beta uses Yahoo Finance's 5-yr monthly figure of ~1.13 for CDNS, rounded down to 1.05 to reflect Cadence's lower-volatility recurring-revenue mix relative to broader semis ([Yahoo Finance — CDNS key statistics](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/CDNS/)). Capital structure (3% debt / 97% equity) reflects Cadence's net-cash balance sheet — ~$3.0B cash vs. ~$2.5B total debt — disclosed in the [CDNS 10-K FY2025 — Balance Sheet](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000813672/000081367226000016/cdns-20251231.htm).

## 5. Price Target & Recommendation

**12-month price target: $400**
**Recommendation: BUY**
**Implied upside: +18.3%**

### 5.1 Key Catalysts

1. **Hexagon Design & Engineering acquisition closed 2026-02-23** — adds MSC Nastran + Adams to system-design portfolio at ~€2.7B purchase price (70% cash / 30% stock); Cadence guides ~$160M incremental FY2026 revenue, ~$0.28 non-GAAP EPS dilutive in 2026 and accretive in 2027 ([Cadence press release, "Cadence to Acquire Hexagon's Design & Engineering Business"](https://www.cadence.com/en_US/home/company/newsroom/press-releases/pr/2025/cadence-to-acquire-hexagons-design--engineering-business.html); [CDNS 8-K, 2025-09-04 — Equity Purchase Agreement](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000813672/000081367225000126/cdns-20250904.htm)).
2. **FY2026 guide raise** — management raised to $6.125B–$6.225B (+15-17% YoY) on Q1 strength ([CDNS Q1 FY2026 8-K — earnings release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000813672/000081367226000044/cdns04272026ex9901.htm)); further raises likely on AI-design demand per the [CFO commentary](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000813672/000081367226000044/cfocommentary04272026ex9902.htm).
3. **Cerebrus & JedAI agentic-AI traction** — Cadence Cerebrus + JedAI cumulative use across **>1,000 tapeouts** ([Cadence press release — "ChipStack AI Super Agent", 2026-02-10](https://www.cadence.com/en_US/home/company/newsroom/press-releases/pr/2026/cadence-unleashes-chipstack-ai-super-agent-pioneering-a-new.html)); pricing-power inflection if customers move to value-based licensing.
4. **EU AI Act (Aug 2 2026)** — high-risk obligations take effect with fines up to 7% of worldwide turnover ([EU AI Act, Article 99: Penalties](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/99/)); Cadence flagged the regulation as a risk in its [FY2025 10-K — Risk Factors](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000813672/000081367226000016/cdns-20251231.htm); near-term overhang resolution would re-rate the stock.
5. **China revenue recovery** — Cadence disclosed that BIS rescinded the May 23 2025 EDA-export license requirement on July 2 2025 ([CDNS 10-K FY2025 — BIS letters & China discussion](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000813672/000081367226000016/cdns-20251231.htm)); China revenue normalised meaningfully in 2H 2025.

### 5.2 Key Risks

1. **BIS settlement overhang** — Cadence agreed to pay $140.6M in aggregate net penalties/forfeitures and pleaded guilty to one count of conspiracy to commit export control violations involving China's National University of Defense Technology, with a 3-year probation and compliance-program obligations ([CDNS 8-K, 2025-07-28 — BIS/DOJ settlement disclosure](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000813672/000081367225000109/cdns-20250727.htm); [DOJ press release — Cadence agrees to plead guilty, 2025-07](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/cadence-design-systems-agrees-plead-guilty-and-pay-over-140-million-unlawfully-exporting)).
2. **Hexagon integration risk** — though smaller than Ansys/Synopsys, Hexagon D&E is Cadence's largest-ever acquisition at ~€2.7B; integration distraction could compress margins ([CDNS 8-K, 2025-09-04 — EPA](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000813672/000081367225000126/cdns-20250904.htm)).
3. **AI Act compliance** — EU AI Act effective 2026-08-02 with fines up to €35M or 7% of worldwide turnover ([EU AI Act, Article 99: Penalties](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/99/)); cost burden uncertain.
4. **Chinese domestic EDA competition** — Cadence's 10-K names Huada Empyrean, Avatar, Xpeedic, X-EPIC, Primarius Technologies, Univista and Giga-DA as emerging China-domiciled competitors ([CDNS 10-K FY2025 — Competition](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000813672/000081367226000016/cdns-20251231.htm)); geopolitical decoupling could accelerate share loss, especially after Empyrean's [2025-03 acquisition of Xpeedic](https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20250318VL202/eda-empyrean-technology-design-software-acquisition.html).
5. **Multiple compression** — at ~43x forward P/E ([Yahoo Finance — CDNS Statistics](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/CDNS/key-statistics/)), any deceleration below 12% revenue growth would compress multiples sharply toward peer median of ~38x ([Yahoo Finance — SNPS / ANSS comparable multiples](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/SNPS/)).

### 5.3 Why BUY

- Clean balance sheet (~$0.5B net cash, derived from $3.0B cash less $2.5B debt) ([CDNS 10-K FY2025 — Balance Sheet](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000813672/000081367226000016/cdns-20251231.htm)) vs. SNPS's post-Ansys leverage ([SNPS 10-K FY2025 — Long-term debt schedule](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000883241/000088324125000028/snps-20251031.htm); [SNPS 8-K Ex-99.1, deal-close, 2025-07-17](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000883241/000119312525158488/d800809dex991.htm)) — full optionality on M&A and capital return.
- Three-horizon AI narrative (Infrastructure AI, Physical AI, Life Sciences AI) — Cadence's "Intelligent System Design" strategy is articulated in the [CDNS 10-K FY2025 — Business overview](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000813672/000081367226000016/cdns-20251231.htm) and reinforced in the [Hexagon D&E acquisition press release](https://www.cadence.com/en_US/home/company/newsroom/press-releases/pr/2025/cadence-to-acquire-hexagons-design--engineering-business.html).
- GAAP and non-GAAP operating margins — Q1 FY2026 delivered 29.3% GAAP operating margin ([CDNS Q1 FY2026 8-K — earnings release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000813672/000081367226000044/cdns04272026ex9901.htm)) and 44%+ non-GAAP margin per [CFO commentary](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000813672/000081367226000044/cfocommentary04272026ex9902.htm) — best-in-class profitability among large-cap software vs. [ADBE](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000796343/000079634325000004/adbe-20241129.htm) and [INTU](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000896878/000089687825000035/intu-20250731.htm).
- All-organic mid-teens growth with FY2025 ending backlog of $7.8B and $3.8B of remaining performance obligations expected within the next 12 months — ~53% NTM revenue visibility on the $6.175B FY26 guide midpoint ([CDNS 10-K FY2025 — Remaining performance obligations](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000813672/000081367226000016/cdns-20251231.htm)).
- Hexagon D&E acquisition directly attacks Ansys's structural-analysis stronghold, signalling Cadence will not cede multi-physics ([Cadence press release, "Cadence to Acquire Hexagon's D&E Business"](https://www.cadence.com/en_US/home/company/newsroom/press-releases/pr/2025/cadence-to-acquire-hexagons-design--engineering-business.html); [ANSS 10-K FY2024 — Product offering competitive positioning](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001013462/000101346225000009/anss-20241231.htm)).
