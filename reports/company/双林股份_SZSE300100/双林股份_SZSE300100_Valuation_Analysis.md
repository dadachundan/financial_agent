**Investment thesis (one sentence).** Shuanglin's HDM-led auto-parts business is fundamentally healthy ([双林股份 2025 年年度报告](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-03-25/1225028868.PDF); HDM #1 in China at 32.8% market share per [新浪财经 双林 A+H 招股书报道, 2026-03-29](https://finance.sina.com.cn/cj/2026-03-29/doc-inhsrzha3593148.shtml)), but at ¥30 the stock prices in option value of the reverse-planetary roller-screw and smart-corner-module pipelines that has not yet been validated by any formal定点 (program award) ([澎湃新闻 — 双林 "蹭热度"：暂无客户、无收入](https://m.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_30271692)); meanwhile Q1 2026 results (revenue −10.4%, NI −47.0%) confirm material pricing-pressure flow-through ([双林股份 2026 年第一季度报告](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-29/1225225491.PDF)), leaving every realistic methodology — DCF Base, EV/EBITDA peer median, P/E NTM peer median, P/E forward 2027E peer median — implying intrinsic value below the current quote.

**Three reasons the rating is SELL, not HOLD:**
1. **All five core methodologies give an intrinsic price below ¥30** — DCF Base ¥17, EV/EBITDA NTM median ¥24, P/E NTM 2026E median ¥21, and the heaviest-weighted P/E forward 2027E median ¥27. Only the DCF Bull (¥35) clears the current price, and that requires a confirmed humanoid-robot OEM定点 win that has not yet been awarded ([Sensitivity tab in financial model](双林股份_SZSE300100_Financial_Model_2026-05-18.xlsx); humanoid robot screw sourcing landscape per [机器人大讲堂 — 双林股份拟定增15亿元布局人形机器人](https://www.leaderobot.com/news/5584)).
2. **Q1 2026 hard data confirms downside vector** — revenue contracted 10.4% YoY and parent NI dropped 47.0%; ex-non-recurring NI fell 39.1%, ruling out the "one-off" explanation. With Top-5 customer concentration at 51.6% (FY2024 reached 56.5% per IPO prospectus; H1 2025 = 54.3%), OEM-driven price negotiations have direct margin transmission — annualizing Q1 puts FY2026 EPS run-rate around ¥0.55–0.65, vs. the current implied of ~¥0.91 ([双林股份 2026 年第一季度报告](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-29/1225225491.PDF); [双林股份 2025 年年度报告](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-03-25/1225028868.PDF); [新浪财经 — 双林招股书解读, 2025-09-25](https://finance.sina.com.cn/stock/aigc/ggxg/zgs/2025-09-25/doc-infrsfyp8810597.shtml)).
3. **Multiple compression risk dominates near-term** — current TTM P/E ~34x is above the auto-parts peer median (~22x) ([东方财富 双林股份 行情, 300100](https://data.eastmoney.com/stockdata/300100.html); peer multiples cross-checked against [拓普集团 2025 年年度报告](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-03-24/1225026446.PDF) and [双环传动 2025 年年度报告](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225170753.PDF)). If FY2026E results match the Q1 trajectory, both EPS *and* multiple normalize against the investor, with 2-handle EPS at 22–25x P/E implying ¥15–18 — well below our ¥24 target.

**Three reasons the rating is SELL, not stronger SELL ('Strong Underperform'):**
1. **Bull-case DCF supports ¥35** if roller-screw量产 (target June 2026 line start) certifies yield and the first humanoid OEM定点 lands by 2027 — both binary events with non-trivial probability ~20–30%. Tesla's Optimus is targeting 50k–150k units in 2026 (per Musk, then 1m/yr by end-2026 for Gen-3) and uses 14 roller-screws per unit, creating a credible asymmetric payoff for Chinese suppliers like 双林 ([36Kr — Who builds the joints for Tesla's million robots, 2025](https://eu.36kr.com/en/p/3728136166797832); [RobotReport — Schaeffler humanoid factory deployment](https://www.therobotreport.com/schaeffler-humanoid-partner-build-deploy-hundreds-robots/)).
2. **Structural underlying improvement** — FY2025 ex-non-recurring NI +36.6% on revenue +11.7% confirms HDM platform-mix shift toward NEV is real and operating leverage exists. The Q1 weakness may yet reverse on second-half OEM pricing stabilization ([双林股份 2025 年年度报告](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-03-25/1225028868.PDF)).
3. **Balance sheet is conservative** — net debt only ~¥600m vs. ¥17 bn market cap; even with HKEX A+H IPO dilution (¥0.8–1.5 bn), the capital structure remains low-risk ([新浪财经 — 双林股份 A+H 招股书, 2026-03-26](https://finance.sina.com.cn/stock/hkstock/ggscyd/2026-03-26/doc-inhsiqkn0097893.shtml)). This shields downside in a scenario where one or more emerging-product programs deliver — limiting absolute loss potential vs. peers with stretched balance sheets.
