## 1. Company Overview

Contemporary Amperex Technology Co., Ltd. — known universally by its English acronym **CATL** and in Chinese as **宁德时代** — is the world's largest manufacturer of lithium-ion batteries for electric vehicles (EVs) and grid-scale energy storage. Headquartered in Ningde, Fujian, China, the company designs and produces cells, modules, packs and full battery systems across the dominant chemistries (NMC ternary, lithium iron phosphate/LFP, and now sodium-ion), plus battery materials, mineral resources, swapping infrastructure and a growing portfolio of "zero-carbon" energy services. CATL is dual-listed on the ChiNext board of the Shenzhen Stock Exchange (A-share code 300750, listed June 2018) and the Main Board of the Hong Kong Stock Exchange (H-share code 03750, listed 20 May 2025) ([宁德时代 2025 年年度报告, p. 10](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-03-10/1225002214.PDF)).

**What the company does and how it makes money.** CATL sells four main product lines: (a) **EV traction battery systems** — cells, modules and packs for passenger cars, commercial vehicles, electric ships and even eVTOL aircraft; (b) **stationary energy-storage systems** ("ESS") — utility-scale, industrial/commercial, residential and now data-centre back-up; (c) **battery materials & recycling**, mainly through subsidiary Brunp 邦普循环, which closes the loop on cobalt/nickel/lithium; and (d) **battery mineral resources** (own lithium mines and joint ventures). In 2025 these segments contributed RMB 316.5 bn (74.7%), RMB 62.4 bn (14.7%), RMB 21.9 bn (5.2%) and RMB 6.0 bn (1.4%) of revenue respectively ([宁德时代 2025 年年度报告, 第 24 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-03-10/1225002214.PDF)). The dominant revenue model is contract manufacturing of customised battery systems sold under multi-year master supply agreements to global automakers and storage system integrators, with prices indexed to cathode-material costs and renegotiated periodically.

**Where they operate.** China remains the production heartland — bases in Ningde, Liyang, Yibin, Zhaoqing, Jining and the new Zhongzhou and Fuding clusters — but CATL is rapidly globalising. Plants are operating or under construction in Erfurt, Germany (joint with first European customer); Debrecen, Hungary (the company's largest greenfield outside China); Spain (a 50:50 JV with Stellantis announced 2024); Nevada/Michigan in the US (licensing arrangements with Ford and Tesla rather than wholly-owned facilities, given Inflation Reduction Act constraints); and an Indonesian battery-materials park (a JV with PT Antam covering nickel mining, refining, precursor, cathode and cell manufacturing). Roughly **30.6% of 2025 revenue came from overseas customers** ([宁德时代 2025 年年度报告, p. 25](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-03-10/1225002214.PDF)), up from 30.5% a year earlier.

**Scale indicators.** In 2025 the company shipped **661 GWh of lithium-ion batteries** (+39% YoY), generated **RMB 423.7 bn of revenue** (+17% YoY) and **RMB 72.2 bn of net profit attributable to shareholders** (+42% YoY, a record). Operating cash flow reached RMB 133.2 bn (+37%); year-end total assets stood at RMB 974.8 bn ([宁德时代 2025 年年度报告, p. 11](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-03-10/1225002214.PDF)). The company employed roughly 185,800 people at year-end, including **22,901 R&D staff (12.3% of the workforce)** ([宁德时代 2025 年年度报告, p. 30](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-03-10/1225002214.PDF)). Effective production capacity reached 772 GWh with another 321 GWh under construction; capacity utilisation was 96.9%. Reported R&D spend was **RMB 22.1 bn or 5.23% of sales**, up from RMB 18.6 bn in 2024 ([宁德时代 2025 年年度报告, p. 30](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-03-10/1225002214.PDF)). Per third-party tracker SNE Research, CATL held roughly **37–38% global EV-battery share** and **38–43% global ESS-battery share** in 2025 ([SNE Research, "Global EV Battery Monthly Tracker", 2026-02](https://www.sneresearch.com/en/insight/release_view/279)).

**Valuation snapshot (16 May 2026).** Shares last traded at **RMB 423.6** on the Shenzhen ChiNext board, implying a market cap of approximately **RMB 1.96 trillion** (~USD 270 bn) on 4.41 bn shares outstanding (A+H combined). **Interpretation.** CATL trades at a **discount to peers on P/E (24× vs. ~35× median)** even though it has the highest absolute profit pool, highest margins (FY25 net margin 17%) and largest market share. The relative P/E compression reflects three things: (i) Korean peers are loss-making, distorting the median; (ii) BYD and Panasonic have diversified earnings (autos, white goods) that the market values differently; and (iii) markets continue to price meaningful **geopolitical risk** — US Inflation Reduction Act and CHIPS-Act-style restrictions effectively cap CATL's direct US sales, and the 2024 listing on the Pentagon's "1260H" Chinese-Military-Companies list (since contested by CATL) creates an overhang. **On P/S, CATL's 4.2× is roughly 3× the China-listed peer median (~1.4×)**, which is justified by CATL's structurally higher gross margin (~25%) versus the 10–15% margin profile of Gotion/BYD's battery unit. The current multiple sits roughly at the 3-year median; it is neither a value trap nor obviously stretched. The investment debate has shifted from "is CATL a growth story?" to "can it defend share against vertically-integrating customers and Korean/Japanese incumbents in the US?" — a debate covered in Sections 5, 7 and 9.

The company has also become a **dividend payer**: management committed to distributing 50% of net profit, with cumulative dividends approaching **RMB 100 bn** after the FY25 payout ([致股东的信, 2025 年年度报告, p. 4](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-03-10/1225002214.PDF)).

## 2. Company History

CATL was founded in December 2011 in Ningde, Fujian, by **曾毓群 (Robin Zeng Yuqun)** and a core team of battery scientists — including 黄世霖 (Huang Shilin), who served as vice-chairman until 2022 and remains the company's third-largest shareholder. Zeng had spent the prior decade running **Amperex Technology Limited (ATL)** in Hong Kong, the consumer-electronics lithium polymer battery business founded in 1999 that he later sold to Japan's TDK (TYO:6762) in 2005. CATL was originally carved out of ATL as the "automotive division" to focus on the then-nascent EV market, with ATL/TDK as a minority partner. By 2015 Chinese government policy ring-fencing the EV battery market to local suppliers (the so-called "白名单" whitelist) forced TDK to divest, leaving CATL fully Chinese-controlled — a regulatory pivot that proved foundational ([2024 年年度报告, p. 31](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-03-15/1222806982.PDF)).

The first major commercial breakthrough came in **2012**, when CATL signed a development contract with BMW to supply batteries for the **BMW Brilliance Zinoro** EV — a relationship that established CATL's credibility with Western OEMs. By **2017**, CATL had overtaken Panasonic (then Tesla's sole supplier) to become the world's #1 EV battery maker by GWh shipped, a position it has held every year since per SNE Research's annual rankings.

**June 11, 2018** — CATL completed an A-share IPO on the Shenzhen ChiNext board at RMB 25.14/share, raising RMB 5.5 bn. The stock has since split-adjusted into the top decile of Chinese equities by market cap. The years 2019–2021 were a period of explosive scale-up: revenue grew from RMB 45.7 bn (2019) to RMB 130.4 bn (2021), with shipments rising from 40.3 GWh to 133.4 GWh as **Tesla** (Shanghai Gigafactory ramp from 2020), **Geely 吉利**, **NIO 蔚来**, **Li Auto 理想**, **XPeng 小鹏**, **GAC 广汽** and **SAIC 上汽** all joined the customer list.

**Key milestones** ([CATL Investor Day decks 2022–2025 archived on company IR](https://www.catl.com/en/investors/) and [HKEX prospectus, 2025-05](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2025/0509/2025050900290.pdf)):
- **2019**: First overseas factory — Erfurt, Germany — broke ground (since operational, supplying BMW iX3).
- **2020**: CTP 1.0 ("Cell-to-Pack") platform launched, eliminating module casing and lifting volumetric energy density by ~15–20%.
- **2021**: Launched first-generation sodium-ion battery — the first major global battery maker to commercialise the chemistry. Began Hungary (Debrecen) plant construction with a planned 100 GWh capacity.
- **2022**: **Qilin 麒麟** CTP 3.0 launched, the first mass-produced battery exceeding 255 Wh/kg gravimetric density.
- **2023**: **Shenxing 神行** super-fast-charge LFP battery launched — the first LFP to support 4C charging (10–80% in ~10 minutes), addressing the historic weakness of LFP versus ternary chemistry. Strategic partnerships announced with Ford (US licensing model) and Tesla (continued Shanghai supply).
- **2024**: Stellantis 50:50 JV announced for a Spain plant (50 GWh by 2026). "Tianheng 天恒" 6.25 MWh ESS unit launched, becoming the global standard for utility-scale storage. Brunp Recycling reached 130 kt/yr black-mass capacity.
- **2025**: **HKEX H-share listing on 20 May 2025** at HKD 263/share, raising approximately **HKD 41.0 bn (~USD 5.2 bn)** — at the time the world's largest IPO of the year. The H-share IPO supplied capital for the Hungary plant and global expansion ([HKEX listing announcement, 2025-05-20](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2025/0520/2025052000346.pdf)). Also launched Naxin 钠新 (second-gen sodium-ion), Xiaoyao 骁遥 dual-core battery, Tianxing 天行 commercial-vehicle battery and TENER Stack ESS family. By year-end the swapping network reached **1,325 stations** (Chocolate 巧克力 light-vehicle: ~1,000; Qiji 骐骥 heavy-truck: ~300) ([致股东的信, p. 2](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-03-10/1225002214.PDF)).

**Strategic pivots.** Three transitions stand out. First, the **2019–2022 ESS pivot** — adding stationary storage to what had been an EV-only company, an early bet that has produced a ~RMB 60+ bn segment. Second, **2022–2024 globalisation under IP risk** — the move from pure exports to a "license + local manufacturing" model (Ford BlueOval City in Michigan, Tesla in Nevada) that lets CATL collect royalties while sidestepping IRA "foreign entity of concern" rules. Third, **2025's "全域增量" (all-domain incremental growth) framing** — pushing batteries into electric ships (~900 vessels deployed), eVTOL aircraft (subsidiary Autoflight 峰飞航空 received the world's first ton-class eVTOL airworthiness triple-certification), heavy-truck swap stations, and zero-carbon industrial parks. Whether this widens or dilutes the franchise is the central forward debate.

**Key acquisitions** have included the controlling stake in **Brunp Recycling 邦普循环** (recycling, since 2013), **Lithium Americas's Cauchari–Olaroz JV** stake (lithium feedstock, 2021), and a minority position in Indonesia's **Vale Indonesia / nickel mining** (announced 2024). Most growth has been organic.

## 3. Management Team

CATL is unusually founder-driven. Chairman/CEO Zeng controls more than 26% of voting equity through his investment vehicle Xiamen Ruiting and personal holdings; the management bench is built almost entirely from internal promotions out of ATL or from the founding R&D team. The board is structured 9 directors — 4 executive, 2 non-executive, 3 independent non-executive — after a 2025 governance reform that **abolished the supervisory board and merged it into the board** in line with HK listing requirements ([宁德时代 2025 年年度报告, 第 53 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-03-10/1225002214.PDF)).

### Robin Zeng Yuqun (曾毓群), 57 — Founder, Chairman of the Board, Executive Director and CEO

Zeng is the dominant figure in the global battery industry. Born in Lankou (蕉城区), Ningde — the town that would become CATL's namesake — he graduated from **Shanghai Jiao Tong University in 1989** with a BSc in marine engineering, then joined **Fujian SAE Magnetics** (a TDK subsidiary). In 1999 he co-founded **Amperex Technology Limited (ATL)** in Hong Kong, building it into the dominant supplier of polymer cells to consumer-electronics OEMs including Apple. He sold ATL to TDK in 2005 and stayed as President/CEO until 2011, when he spun out the automotive division as CATL. He earned a PhD in condensed matter physics from the Institute of Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences in 2006 ([宁德时代 2025 年年度报告, 第 47 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-03-10/1225002214.PDF)). Zeng has chaired CATL since 2017 and added the CEO/总经理 title in August 2022 after long-time CEO 周毅 stepped down. His reported FY2025 compensation was RMB 5.69 million ([宁德时代 2025 年年度报告, 第 53 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-03-10/1225002214.PDF)) — modest, but his economic interest is overwhelmingly via the equity (~22.45% via Xiamen Ruiting alone, worth roughly RMB 440 bn at current prices). Zeng is widely cited for the strategic principles "造电池容易，但造好电池很难" ("making batteries is easy, making good batteries is hard") and his focus on quality defect rates (DPPB, defects-per-billion). The press has tracked his interview cadence, including a notable 2024 interview with the Norwegian sovereign wealth fund's CEO Nicolai Tangen ([NBIM "In Good Company" podcast, 2024-10-24](https://www.nbim.no/en/podcast/in-good-company/season-3/robin-zeng-catl/)). Track record: from a standing start in 2011 he has built the world's most valuable battery franchise, with 14 consecutive years of #1 EV-battery market share globally. Key-person dependency is material — see Section 9.

### Pan Jian (潘健), 50 — Co-Chairman and Executive Director

Pan has been with the company since 2012 (then ATL) and serves as Co-Chairman following the 2025 governance restructure. He oversees global capital markets, M&A and overseas operations including the Hungary build-out. His background spans investment banking (early-career at China Galaxy Securities and UBS Securities) and corporate finance. He has been the public face for CATL's HKEX listing, IRA-related US dialogues and the Stellantis JV negotiation. Pan also sits as an independent director of Shanghai Chenguang Stationery (上海晨光文具, SSE:603899). Reported FY2025 compensation: RMB 330 k ([宁德时代 2025 年年度报告, 第 53 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-03-10/1225002214.PDF)).

### Li Ping (李平), 57 — Vice-Chairman and Executive Director

Li is one of CATL's earliest employees from the ATL era and a long-standing operating leader. He runs key strategic-investment vehicles for the group — RCS Investment, Shida Investment Holdings, Rainbow Rich Profits and Shanghai Shida Investment Management — through which CATL holds shares in non-consolidated startups (lithium juniors, materials companies, automotive technology). He served as a director of EV-motor company Shanghai Panji Power Technology (now part of the Geely orbit) until early 2026. Li was historically a "concert-party" shareholder with Zeng; that arrangement formally ended in February 2024 ([2024 年度报告 § 重要事项 — 关于股份减持的承诺, p. 95](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-03-15/1222806982.PDF)). FY2025 compensation: RMB 352 k.

### Zheng Shu (郑舒), 46 — Chief Financial Officer

Zheng joined CATL in April 2016 to lead the finance department and has been CFO/财务总监 since June 2017 — spanning the A-share IPO (2018), all annual reports since 2017, and the 2025 H-share IPO. He holds dual bachelor's degrees in accounting and computer science from Fuzhou University (2002), is a CIMA-certified management accountant (CGMA), and previously served as CFO of Nasdaq-listed Sohu Changyou (CYOU), regional budget manager at Huawei's overseas operations, and finance leader at Wanding Silicon Steel Group ([宁德时代 2025 年年度报告, 第 50 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-03-10/1225002214.PDF)). Under Zheng's tenure, CATL has built a fortress balance sheet — RMB 133 bn of operating cash flow in 2025, net cash position even after RMB 90+ bn of annual capex, and AA-rated credit by Chinese rating agencies. Reported FY2025 compensation: RMB 2.83 million.

### Tan Libin (谭立斌), 57 — VP, Chief Customer Officer and Co-President of Sales

Tan owns the global customer-facing organisation. He joined the group in 1999 (then ATL Dongguan) and rose through sales to head ATL's polymer-cell sales by 2013. He moved to CATL in December 2015 and now serves as VP, Chief Customer Officer, and co-president of the marketing system. His career prior to ATL spans Dongguan Sinco Electronics (1991–1998) and Dell China NPI (1999–2001). Bachelor's in mechanical design from Zhejiang University (1991). Tan has been the architect of CATL's named-customer wins from Tesla through to the recent Stellantis/Volvo Cars/Mercedes-Benz contracts. He also chairs CATL Kestar 科士达科技 and CATL Quick Charging (上海快卜新能源) ([宁德时代 2025 年年度报告, 第 51 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-03-10/1225002214.PDF)). FY2025 compensation: RMB 2.64 million.

### Governance & Insider Ownership

The 9-member board has three independent directors: **Prof. Wu Yuhui (吴育辉, Xiamen University accounting professor)**, **Lin Xiaoxiong (林小雄)** and **Zhao Bei (赵蓓)**. Independent-director ratio meets both ChiNext (≥1/3) and HKEX requirements. Total reported FY2025 board-and-executive compensation was RMB 24.5 million — modest in absolute terms but supplemented by six equity-incentive cycles since IPO ([宁德时代 2025 年年度报告, 第 53 页 & 第 43 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-03-10/1225002214.PDF)). Insider ownership: Xiamen Ruiting (Zeng's vehicle) holds 22.45%, Huang Shilin 9.21%, Ningbo Lianhe Chuangxin (employee co-invest vehicle) 6.23%, Li Ping 4.33%, so insiders/founders together control ~42% of A-share float; HKSCC Nominees holds 3.42% in H-shares ([宁德时代 2025 年年度报告, 第 97 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-03-10/1225002214.PDF)). The actual controller is Zeng (Chinese-Hong-Kong national).

**Track-record assessment.** The team has delivered 14 straight years of global #1 share, expanded gross margin from ~22% (2018) to ~25% (2025) despite intense LFP price competition, and successfully executed both a 2018 ChiNext IPO and a 2025 HKEX dual-listing. The principal governance risk is founder-CEO concentration — Zeng simultaneously holds Chairman, CEO and controlling shareholder roles, with the supervisory board having been abolished in late 2025. Mitigants: a strong CFO, independent-director ratio meeting global norms, ESG rating of MSCI AA, and the H-share listing's higher disclosure burden.

## 4. Products & Services

CATL groups its catalogue into four reporting segments. Below is the full enumeration from the [CATL global website product navigation tree](https://www.catl.com/en/) and the latest [annual-report disclosure](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-03-10/1225002214.PDF).

### 4.1 EV (Power) Battery Systems — 74.7% of FY25 revenue

The flagship segment. Branded product families:

- **Shenxing 神行 / Shenxing Pro / Shenxing-Plus** — fast-charging LFP. The 2nd-gen (2024) achieves 4C charging (10–80% SOC in ~10 min) and 800-km CLTC range, the 3rd-gen Shenxing Pro launched 2025 pushes density to ~205 Wh/kg ([CATL Shenxing product page](https://www.catl.com/en/research/products/202308.html)). **Competitive advantage: yes — technology moat.** Closest competing product: **BYD Blade 2.0** (LFP, ~2C charge, slightly higher pack density via cell-to-body integration). CATL is ahead on charge speed and OEM win-rate (Avatr, IM, Zeekr, Avatar, Chery), at parity on cost.
- **Qilin 麒麟 CTP 3.0** — premium ternary/LFP cell-to-pack. Up to 255 Wh/kg, 1000-km range packs. Powers the Li Auto MEGA, Avatr 11, Zeekr 001 FR. **Advantage: yes — technology + scale moat.** Closest competing product: **LG Energy Solution NCM811 + AESC integration kit**; CATL ahead on volumetric density.
- **Xiaoyao 骁遥 Dual-Core** (2025 launch) — hybrid-architecture pack combining NMC and sodium-ion cells in the same pack, optimising winter range and cost. First-of-its-kind. **Advantage: yes — IP moat.** No direct competitor product yet.
- **Naxin 钠新 (Sodium-Ion 2nd-gen, 2025)** — 175 Wh/kg sodium-ion, retains performance at –40°C. Targets entry-level EVs and 2-wheelers. **Advantage: yes — first-mover.** Closest competitor: BYD/Farasis pilot sodium lines (later-stage development).
- **Tianxing 天行 (Commercial-Vehicle)** — 2024/25 family for heavy trucks and light commercial vans. **Advantage: yes — scale + service network (Qiji swap stations).** Closest competitor: CALB / EVE Energy commercial pack.
- **Ningxing 凝聚态 (Condensed Battery)** — semi-solid, ~500 Wh/kg, targeted at eVTOL aircraft. Powers AutoFlight's "Kaiyu" certified eVTOL. **Advantage: yes — technology moat.** Closest competitor: QuantumScape and Solid Power (still pre-commercial); CATL is ahead in commercialisation cadence.
- **Tianheng 天恒 / TENER Stack** — co-platform that crosses into the ESS segment (covered below).

In passenger-car-segment terms, FY25 revenue split was approximately 60% LFP / 40% ternary by GWh according to industry trackers ([SNE Research, 2026-02](https://www.sneresearch.com/en/insight/release_view/279)). The blended ASP fell ~10% YoY on lithium-carbonate deflation but unit volume rose ~39%, producing the segment's 25% revenue growth ([宁德时代 2025 年年度报告, 第 24 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-03-10/1225002214.PDF)).

### 4.2 Energy Storage Systems (ESS) — 14.7% of FY25 revenue

CATL is the global #1 in utility-scale ESS by GWh per [SNE Research 2026-02](https://www.sneresearch.com/en/insight/release_view/279). Product families:
- **EnerC 6.25 MWh container** — flagship utility-scale unit.
- **TENER 天恒** — 6.25 MWh unit launched 2024, world-record 5-year zero-degradation guarantee.
- **TENER Stack** (2025) — "Two-in-One" modular configuration that drops install time and improves system-level density.
- **EnerOne** — residential/C&I racks.
- **EnerD** — DC-block solution for AI / data-centre back-up power, a 2025 growth area.

**Competitive advantage: yes — scale, cost and 5-year guarantee are unmatched.** Closest competing products: **Sungrow PowerTitan 2.0** (Chinese DC-block competitor — closer on price but trails on cycle-life), **Tesla Megapack 3** (Tesla integrates its own cells; comparable performance but smaller addressable market outside US), **LG Energy Solution ESS** (recovering from 2020–2022 fire-related recalls).

### 4.3 Battery Materials & Recycling — 5.2% of FY25 revenue

Subsidiary **Brunp Recycling 邦普循环** is the world's largest closed-loop battery recycler, processing **210 kt of spent batteries and materials in 2025 (+63% YoY) and producing 24 kt of regenerated lithium salts (+40%)** ([致股东的信, 2025 annual report, p. 2](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-03-10/1225002214.PDF)). Product portfolio: NMC cathode precursors, lithium carbonate, regenerated lithium salts, ternary cathode active materials. **Advantage: yes — regulatory & scale moat (China's "白名单" certified recyclers).** Closest competitor: **Umicore** (Belgium, KRX), **Li-Cycle** (NYSE, in financial distress).

### 4.4 Battery Mineral Resources — 1.4% of FY25 revenue

Lithium mining operations in Yichun (Jiangxi, lepidolite), JV interest in Sichuan, Bolivian salar JV, and the Indonesian nickel battery park. Revenue is small because much output is consumed internally rather than sold. **Advantage: partial — vertical-integration hedge rather than standalone profit centre.** Closest competitor: BYD Findreams Mining unit; Tianqi 天齐 and Ganfeng 赣锋 are merchant suppliers.

### 4.5 Adjacent / "全域增量" services

- **Chocolate 巧克力 swap stations** — passenger-car battery swap network, **>1,000 stations live**; partnerships with GAC, Changan, FAW, SAIC, Chery; financial-services tie-up with Sinopec (China's largest fuel-retail network) and CMB Financial Leasing.
- **Qiji 骐骥 swap stations** — heavy-truck swap, **>300 stations** covering 26 provinces. Aligned with FAW Jiefang, Shaanxi Heavy Auto, etc. (>30 standardised tractor and dump-truck models). In 2025 the combined Chocolate + Qiji network completed **>1.15 million swap events with ~80 GWh of energy delivered** ([致股东的信, p. 3](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-03-10/1225002214.PDF)).
- **AutoFlight 峰飞航空** — eVTOL subsidiary (acquired equity 2023). World's first ton-class eVTOL with airworthiness triple-certification; a 5-ton-class eVTOL completed first public flight in 2025.
- **Marine battery systems** — nearly **900 electric vessels** powered by CATL packs by year-end 2025; a "ship-shore-cloud" zero-carbon shipping platform was launched for inland waterway and coastal shipping.
- **Zero-carbon industrial parks** — projects with Hainan, Shandong Dongying, Fujian Ningde, Jiangsu Yancheng listed in China's national-level pilot programme ([2025 年年度报告, 第 23 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-03-10/1225002214.PDF)).
- **Industrial robotics / data-centre back-up** — CATL has explicitly named **humanoid-robot battery applications** in its 2024 and 2025 disclosures, with the **E30P 2-wheeler cylindrical battery, Xuebao 雪豹 family and high-power lithium cells** marketed for "electric motorcycles, electric two-wheelers, electric tools, and data centres" ([2025 年年度报告, 第 23 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-03-10/1225002214.PDF)). Industry sources flag CATL as a supply candidate for Unitree, Figure AI, Agility Robotics and 1X — but contract specifics are undisclosed. Battery-as-a-service for industrial robots is in pilot deployments under the Chocolate/Qiji infrastructure umbrella.

### 4.6 Flagship vs. long-tail

The 1–3 products that drive the business are **(1) Shenxing fast-charge LFP**, **(2) Qilin CTP-3 ternary**, and **(3) TENER ESS modules**. Together these account for the majority of FY25 revenue. Sodium-ion, Condensed (eVTOL) and the swap/services lines are strategically important but small dollar-revenue contributors as of FY25. Recent launches (Shenxing Pro, Naxin 2nd-gen, TENER Stack, Xiaoyao dual-core, Tianxing II commercial) all hit the market in 2025; nothing material was sunsetted.

## 6. Industry Overview

### 6.1 Industry definition

CATL operates at the intersection of **(a) lithium-ion battery manufacturing** — itself spanning the cell, module, pack and BMS value chain — **(b) electric mobility** (passenger NEVs, commercial vehicles, marine, low-altitude eVTOL), **(c) stationary energy storage** for the power grid (utility-scale, C&I, residential), and increasingly **(d) integrated energy services** (swapping, V2G, zero-carbon parks). The principal industry codes in Chinese statistical classification are **C3825 锂离子电池制造** (cell manufacturing) and **C3839 其他电池制造**, with adjacent SIC/NAICS codes 335912 (US, primary batteries) and 27.20 (EU NACE).

### 6.2 Market size and growth

**EV batteries.** Global EV-battery deployment hit **1,187 GWh in 2025, up 31.7% YoY**, on global NEV sales of **21.47 million units, up 21.5%** per SNE Research's annual print quoted in CATL's annual report ([2025 年年度报告, 第 16 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-03-10/1225002214.PDF) citing [SNE Research](https://www.sneresearch.com/en/insight/release_view/279)). Industry consensus has the market reaching **~2,500 GWh by 2030** under base-case adoption.

**Stationary energy storage.** Global ESS-battery shipments reached **550 GWh in 2025, up 79% YoY** per the same SNE source — making ESS the fastest-growing application segment of the battery industry. Drivers: increased wind/solar penetration requiring grid balancing, rising data-centre / AI back-up demand, falling LCOE of LFP-based ESS, and Inflation Reduction Act / EU NZIA subsidies.

**Total lithium-ion shipments** (EV + ESS + consumer + 2-wheelers) reached approximately **1,900 GWh in 2025**. The **IEA** projects global annual clean-energy investment of **~USD 4.5 trillion by 2050** in its Net-Zero scenario, with batteries the single largest hardware line item ([致股东的信, citing IEA](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-03-10/1225002214.PDF) — primary [IEA, "World Energy Investment 2025"](https://www.iea.org/reports/world-energy-investment-2025)).

### 6.3 Key trends and drivers

1. **Chemistry mix shift** — LFP overtook NMC in 2024 as the dominant chemistry on a GWh basis. LFP's cost advantage and CATL/BYD's progress on energy density (Shenxing, Blade) have eroded NMC's range premium. By 2025 LFP was ~65% of China shipments and ~45% globally. Korean players (LGES, Samsung SDI) have only just begun mass-producing LFP, putting them structurally behind on cost.
2. **Sodium-ion commercialisation** — first commercial cells from CATL (2021) and HiNa (2023); 2025 second-generation product (Naxin) addresses cold-weather range. Targets entry-tier passenger cars, 2-wheelers, ESS — could displace ~5–10% of LFP volume by 2030.
3. **Solid-state acceleration** — Toyota, Samsung SDI and Chinese players (CATL's "凝聚态" semi-solid, QingTao, WeLion) all targeting 2027–2030 SOP. CATL's Condensed Battery is the first commercial semi-solid for eVTOL.
4. **Vertical integration by customers** — Tesla 4680, BYD Findreams, Stellantis ACC, VW PowerCo, Ford BlueOval — all top customers building captive cell capacity. This is the single biggest structural risk to merchant battery makers.
5. **Geopolitical fragmentation** — IRA "foreign entity of concern" rules, EU Critical Raw Materials Act, India PLI scheme, Korea K-Battery alliance. CATL has responded with the "license + local manufacturing" model rather than direct ownership in restricted markets.
6. **Recycling / circular economy** — EU Battery Regulation (2027 minimum recycled content), China extended-producer-responsibility rules. Brunp is uniquely positioned.
7. **Service-layer expansion** — swap stations, energy-as-a-service contracts, V2G — converts a one-time hardware sale into recurring revenue. CATL has the largest swap network globally.

### 6.4 Regulatory environment

The regulatory landscape is exceptionally complex and asymmetric:
- **China** — "白名单" (Whitelist) battery supplier qualification, NEV subsidies (now expired), dual-credit policy (NEV credit trading), MIIT new-vehicle technology directives, GB/T national safety standards (GB38031, GB38032).
- **EU** — Battery Regulation 2023/1542 (CO2 footprint declarations from 2025, recycled content from 2027), Critical Raw Materials Act (own-source / processed-in-EU thresholds), Net-Zero Industry Act subsidies for European cell manufacturing.
- **US** — Inflation Reduction Act §30D (consumer EV tax credit excludes "foreign entities of concern" — CATL falls under FEOC definitions, making direct CATL exports to US OEMs essentially uncompetitive); §45X advanced manufacturing tax credit; the 2024 NDAA "1260H" list. CATL has structured Ford and Tesla relationships as licensing rather than direct supply to navigate these constraints.
- **India** — PLI ACC battery storage scheme (Reliance, Ola, ACC won 2022/2023 tenders; CATL not eligible as foreign company).
- **Korea / Japan** — broadly open but national champions favoured for public-procurement / strategic ESS programmes.

### 6.5 Industry structure

**Globally, the top-5 EV-battery makers control ~80% of GWh shipped** — a textbook oligopoly. Within China the top-3 (CATL + BYD + CALB) hold ~80% of domestic EV-battery production. **Supplier power** is moderate: lithium, cobalt, nickel and graphite are increasingly concentrated (lithium → SQM/Albemarle/Tianqi/Ganfeng, cobalt → Glencore/CMOC, graphite → BTR/Shanshan). **Buyer power** is high — large auto OEMs aggressively dual-source — but switching costs are also high (a 24+ month re-qualification per platform). **Substitutes** include fuel cells (limited progress to date) and sodium-ion (in-family substitution rather than a true alternative). **Barriers to entry** are very high: ~USD 100 m+ per GWh of capex, 3–4 year ramp to break-even, IP and trade-secret moat around manufacturing process, and supplier-qualification cycles that take 18–24 months at OEMs.

## 8. Market Opportunity (TAM)

**Top-down TAM.** SNE Research's reported 2025 lithium-ion deployments — 1,187 GWh EV + 550 GWh ESS + ~150 GWh consumer/2-wheelers/tools — totalled approximately **1,900 GWh**. At a weighted-average blended ASP of ~USD 100/kWh (LFP ~USD 80/kWh, ternary ~USD 120/kWh, ESS DC-block ~USD 95/kWh after 2024–25 price compression), the **2025 industry TAM is approximately USD 190 bn**.

**Forward projection.** Base-case industry trackers ([BloombergNEF "Long-Term Electric Vehicle Outlook 2025"](https://about.bnef.com/electric-vehicle-outlook/), [Wood Mackenzie Energy Storage Service 2025](https://www.woodmac.com/research/products/power-and-renewables/global-energy-storage-monitor/), [IEA "World Energy Investment 2025"](https://www.iea.org/reports/world-energy-investment-2025)) converge on a **2030 lithium-ion battery deployment of ~3,200–3,800 GWh** — implying TAM growth at a 11–14% CAGR from 2025 — with ESS the fastest-growing sub-segment (~25% CAGR vs. ~10% for EV). At a blended USD 75/kWh by 2030 (continued cost decline), the **2030 TAM is in the USD 250–290 bn range** — roughly a 40–50% expansion in dollar terms despite price deflation.

**SAM (CATL serviceable).** CATL's serviceable market is **(a) global EV-battery merchant market ex-captive volume** (~75% of total ~80% of merchant EV battery; ~700 GWh by 2030 base case) and **(b) global ESS** (~80% addressable; ~900–1,000 GWh by 2030). With minor exposure to consumer/2-wheeler/tool batteries, SAM in 2030 is roughly **2,400–2,700 GWh**, equivalent to **USD 180–200 bn** at projected prices.

**SOM (realistic CATL share).** CATL's 2025 share — ~37% in EV batteries and ~38–43% in ESS — translates to actual deployment of ~661 GWh against an industry of ~1,737 GWh (38% blended). If CATL holds that 38% share through 2030 (a defensible base case given scale advantages but pressured by IRA + customer in-sourcing), 2030 deployment would be **~900–1,000 GWh, generating USD 70–80 bn of battery revenue alone** — a ~40% increase over 2025 in dollar terms (helped by ESS mix and offset by ASP decline). Add adjacencies (services, recycling, materials), and CATL's revenue could reach **RMB 650–750 bn by 2030** under base-case assumptions.

**Bull case.** Faster ESS adoption (data-centre / AI back-up + grid build-out), CATL holding mid-40s share in ESS, sodium-ion expanding the 2-wheeler addressable market, eVTOL commercialisation, and a meaningful humanoid-robotics battery market (~25–50 GWh by 2030 per [Goldman Sachs "The promise of humanoid robots", 2024-12](https://www.goldmansachs.com/insights/articles/the-global-market-for-robots-could-reach-38-billion-by-2035)). Bull case 2030 revenue: RMB 900 bn+.

**Bear case.** US revenue stuck at licensing royalties (~USD 0.5–1.0 bn), Europe local-content rules force CATL to build expensive own-IP plants, Korean players close LFP cost gap, captive cells take 25%+ of the OEM merchant market. Bear case 2030 revenue: RMB 500 bn (flat from 2025 in real terms).

**Penetration strategy.** CATL's playbook for 2026–2030 is fivefold: (i) **localise** — Hungary, Germany, Spain, Indonesia, US licensing — to navigate trade barriers; (ii) **diversify chemistry** — sodium, semi-solid, condensed, dual-core — to address every cost/performance segment; (iii) **expand into ESS** — TENER, AI/data-centre back-up, virtual power plants; (iv) **monetise services** — swap stations, BaaS, V2G; (v) **enter humanoid robotics and low-altitude aviation** — early but optionality-creating moves into segments where CATL's scale and reliability advantage matter most.

## 9. Risk Assessment

### Company-Specific Risks

**1. Customer in-sourcing / vertical integration.** Tesla (4680), BYD (own cells), Stellantis (ACC), VW (PowerCo), Ford (BlueOval, licensed from CATL itself), Mercedes (with CATL but also ACC) — all top-10 customers operate captive cell capacity. Even if licence royalties continue, CATL loses the ASP and margin attached to direct cell sales. Industry tracker MarkLines estimates captive cells could rise from <15% of EV-battery deployment in 2025 to ~30% by 2030. **Severity: high.** Mitigants: customer-specific chemistry/IP, scale-driven cost lead, willingness to enter licensing/JV structures that monetise IP without losing relationships.

**2. Customer concentration (top-1 ~14%, top-5 ~39%).** Disclosed top-5 share has held at 37–39% over three years ([2025 年年度报告, 第 28 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-03-10/1225002214.PDF)). Top customer (commonly understood to be Tesla) is also a vertical integrator (4680 production). Loss of any single top-3 customer would cut revenue 7–14% in a single year. The above-30% top-5 threshold means this is an explicit risk, though below the 50% "material" line. **Severity: medium-high.** Mitigants: 250+ customer relationships, multi-year master agreements with rolling POs, deep engineering integration creating switching costs.

**3. Key-person / founder dependency.** Robin Zeng is Chairman, CEO, controlling shareholder (22.45% via Ruiting + indirect), single-handedly shapes strategy, founded the company, and controls the equity vehicle ([2025 年年度报告, 第 97 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-03-10/1225002214.PDF)). The 2025 governance reform abolished the supervisory board. Death, incapacity, or regulatory action against Zeng would be highly disruptive. **Severity: medium.** Mitigants: deep operating bench (Pan, Li, Tan, Zheng), HKEX listing enhances disclosure and discipline, independent-director ratio compliant.

**4. Technology obsolescence (solid-state, sodium-ion, fuel cell).** Toyota plans solid-state mass production from 2027; QuantumScape and Solid Power are well-funded. If a true solid-state breakthrough arrives before CATL's Condensed and "semi-solid" lines mature, CATL's vast LFP/NMC capex base could be partially stranded. **Severity: medium.** Mitigants: own solid-state R&D pipeline, semi-solid Condensed Battery already commercial in eVTOL, ability to retrofit existing lines.

**5. Geopolitical / 1260H listing / US restrictions.** The US Department of Defense placed CATL on its "1260H" Chinese-Military-Companies list in January 2024 (CATL strongly contests the designation and has filed legal challenge). The IRA's FEOC definition effectively bars CATL from US consumer-EV-credit-eligible vehicles. Any tightening (e.g. extension to ESS) would shrink CATL's US revenue runway further. **Severity: medium-high.** Mitigants: licensing model with Ford/Tesla, Indonesian and Hungarian footprint, China/EU/RoW core markets remain robust.

**6. Capacity over-build / utilisation.** Year-end 2025 capacity 772 GWh + 321 GWh under construction. If EV adoption slows or captive cell capacity comes on faster, China-industry capacity utilisation (already ~70% sector-wide in 2024) could pressure pricing further. **Severity: medium.** Mitigants: CATL's own utilisation was 96.9% in 2025 vs. industry ~70%, demonstrating share concentration at the top.

### Industry / Market Risks

**7. Competitive intensity / price war.** LFP cell prices fell ~60% from 2022 peak; FY25 EV-battery system revenue grew only 25% despite 39% volume growth, implying ~10% ASP decline. Korean peers' new LFP capacity and Chinese second-tier aggression (CALB, EVE, Gotion) will sustain pricing pressure. **Severity: high.** Mitigants: CATL's premium chemistries (Shenxing, Qilin) earn pricing premium; cost-leadership maintained via scale and process improvements.

**8. Regulatory change (EU CRMA, US IRA, China subsidy phase-out).** EU local-content thresholds tightening from 2027; US 30D FEOC rules; potential new tariffs in India, Mexico, Brazil; China NEV subsidies phased out (some risk to demand). **Severity: medium.** Mitigants: localised manufacturing strategy, ESG/recycling leadership pre-empts EU rules.

**9. Substitute technology — hydrogen fuel cell, sodium-ion cannibalisation.** Hydrogen FCEV remains niche outside heavy trucks; sodium-ion is in-family for CATL. **Severity: low.** Mitigants: in-house sodium product line.

**10. Cycle / EV demand slowdown.** China NEV growth has decelerated from 100%+ (2021) to ~22% (2025); European EV demand has weakened with subsidy cuts; US growth stalled. A multi-year flat phase before next adoption wave would compress utilisation and pricing. **Severity: medium.** Mitigants: ESS growth (still 79% YoY) offsets EV moderation.

### Financial Risks

**11. Capex intensity and working-capital cycle.** Investing-cash-flow outflow was RMB 94.5 bn in 2025 (up 93% YoY) on heavier capex and financial-product purchases ([2025 年年度报告, 第 30 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-03-10/1225002214.PDF)). Receivables top-5 customers represent 26.5% of total receivables ([2025 年年度报告, 第 209 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-03-10/1225002214.PDF)) — large counterparty exposure if any major customer faces distress. **Severity: low-medium.** Mitigants: RMB 133 bn operating cash flow, net cash position, dividend payout still 50% of net income.

**12. Valuation / multiple-compression risk.** At 24× TTM P/E and 4.2× TTM P/S, CATL is **not** in the >50× P/E or >15× P/S "stretched" zone — multiples are roughly at 3-year median. Still, ~RMB 1.96 tn market cap embeds continued ~15% revenue CAGR through 2030 and stable ~25% gross margin; a 200 bps gross-margin compression on price war + a 5-pt growth disappointment would justify a P/E re-rating to ~18× — implying ~25% downside. **Severity: low-medium.** Mitigants: dividend yield (~2%) provides downside cushion, recurring ESS / services revenue strengthens earnings quality.

### Macroeconomic Risks

**13. FX exposure.** ~30.6% of revenue is overseas, much priced in USD and EUR with multi-quarter lag. RMB strength against USD (a 5% appreciation scenario) would compress reported revenue ~1.5%. **Severity: low.** Mitigants: natural hedge via overseas factories sourcing locally; FX hedging programme.

**14. Geopolitical tail risk (Taiwan, US–China tech decoupling escalation).** A severe US–China escalation could trigger further export controls on battery technology, Chinese counter-measures restricting key cathode/precursor exports, or sanctions on specific subsidiaries. **Severity: medium (low probability, high impact).** Mitigants: localised production reduces single-point-of-failure; recycling business is geography-agnostic.

**15. Commodity price volatility.** Lithium carbonate spot fell from ~RMB 600,000/t (Nov 2022) to ~RMB 80,000/t (2025) — beneficial on the cost side but creating inventory write-down and pricing-renegotiation risk. Cobalt, nickel and graphite swings have similar effects. **Severity: low-medium.** Mitigants: vertical integration into mining, multi-source contracts, dynamic pricing clauses.
