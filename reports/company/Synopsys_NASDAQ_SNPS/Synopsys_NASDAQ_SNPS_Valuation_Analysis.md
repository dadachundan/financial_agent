## 1. DCF Valuation

### 1.1 Methodology

We model 5 years of explicit unlevered free cash flow (UFCF) from FY2026E through FY2030E, with a terminal value computed via the Gordon Growth model. Operating assumptions derive directly from the [SNPS FY2025 10-K, filed 2025-12-22](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/883241/000088324125000028/snps-20251031.htm) (segment revenue, cost structure, capex, working capital) and FY26 management guidance disclosed in the [Q4 FY25 earnings release 8-K, 2025-12-10](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000883241/000119312525314200/d29055dex991.htm) and reiterated in the [Q1 FY26 earnings release 8-K, 2026-02-25](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000883241/000119312526071601/d921168dex991.htm).

## 5. Price Target & Recommendation

**12-month price target: $580**
**Recommendation: BUY**
**Implied upside: +15.4%** (vs. Wall Street consensus 12-month target ~$540 — [Yahoo Finance — SNPS analyst estimates](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/SNPS/analysis/))

### 5.1 Key Catalysts

1. **Ansys synergy realization** — first integrated combined-capability products planned for 1H 2026; cross-sell into Ansys's broad aerospace/auto/industrial customer base, where the [Ansys 10-K FY2024](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001013462/000101346225000009/anss-20241231.htm) notes that no single customer exceeds 5% of revenue, implying long-tail cross-sell breadth ([Synopsys Completes Acquisition of Ansys, 2025-07-17](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/synopsys-completes-acquisition-of-ansys-302507582.html)).
2. **Margin recovery** — GAAP op margin compressed by Ansys-related amortization of acquired intangibles ($405m in FY25), which steps down annually as the intangible base amortizes ([SNPS FY2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/883241/000088324125000028/snps-20251031.htm); [SNPS Q4 FY25 financial supplement, 2025-12-10](https://s201.q4cdn.com/778493406/files/doc_earnings/2025/q4/supplemental-info/Synopsys-Q4-FY2025-Financial-Supplement.pdf)).
3. **Debt paydown** — $13.5bn closing debt with management already having repaid $1.75bn and guiding ~$1.9bn FY26 free cash flow toward further paydown; covenant easing would enable buyback resumption (the board re-authorized a $2bn repurchase program in Q1 FY26) ([SNPS Q1 FY26 8-K earnings release, 2026-02-25](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000883241/000119312526071601/d921168dex991.htm); [SNPS FY2025 10-K — Long-term debt note](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/883241/000088324125000028/snps-20251031.htm)).
4. **China foundry-customer normalization** — FY25 China revenue fell 22% ex-Ansys, driven by export-control disruption and weakness at a single major foundry customer disclosed in the 10-K ([SNPS FY2025 10-K — MD&A geographic segment](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/883241/000088324125000028/snps-20251031.htm)); recovery is upside.
5. **AI-tool monetization** — the Synopsys.ai DSO.ai/VSO.ai/TSO.ai suite plus the recently expanded Copilot generative-AI capabilities create price/value uplift if customers migrate to outcome-based licensing ([Synopsys press release "Expanding AI Capabilities for its Leading EDA Solutions", 2025-09-03](https://news.synopsys.com/2025-09-03-Synopsys-Announces-Expanding-AI-Capabilities-for-its-Leading-EDA-Solutions)).

### 5.2 Key Risks

1. **Ansys integration risk** — largest deal in company history at ~$34.9bn closed value; the [SNPS FY2025 10-K risk factors](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/883241/000088324125000028/snps-20251031.htm) explicitly cite scale risk and channel-model differences between Ansys's partner-led go-to-market and Synopsys's direct sales model.
2. **Design IP margin pressure** — adjusted Design IP segment margin compressed by ~16 points YoY in Q3 FY25 (from 36.7% to 20.1%) on China export disruption, a major-foundry-customer weakness, and roadmap missteps ([Futurum Group, "Synopsys Q3 FY 2025: IP Business Weakness", 2025-09](https://futurumgroup.com/insights/synopsys-q3-fy-2025-ip-business-weakness-and-transformation-pose-concerns-but-long-term-thesis-unchanged/); [SNPS Q3 FY25 earnings release 8-K, 2025-09-09](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000883241/000119312525199178/d56931dex991.htm)).
3. **Debt overhang and covenant restrictions** — $13.5bn of debt with covenants disclosed in the 10-K that limit buyback and dividend capacity and constrain M&A optionality ([SNPS FY2025 10-K — Long-term debt and covenants](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/883241/000088324125000028/snps-20251031.htm)).
4. **Customer concentration** — FY2025 results explicitly cite a major-foundry customer headwind in Design IP, leaving single-customer dependence as a residual vulnerability ([SNPS FY2025 10-K MD&A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/883241/000088324125000028/snps-20251031.htm)).
5. **Goodwill impairment** — $26.9bn of goodwill on the post-Ansys balance sheet; any synergy shortfall could force a non-cash impairment ([SNPS FY2025 10-K — Goodwill and intangible assets](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/883241/000088324125000028/snps-20251031.htm)).

### 5.3 Why BUY

- Combined silicon-to-systems platform is structurally unmatched: only player offering EDA + Design IP + multi-physics simulation under one roof ([Synopsys Completes Acquisition of Ansys, 2025-07-17](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/synopsys-completes-acquisition-of-ansys-302507582.html)).
- $11.4bn backlog entering FY26 — the highest in company history, up from $10.1bn the prior quarter ([SNPS Q4 FY25 earnings release 8-K, 2025-12-10](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000883241/000119312525314200/d29055dex991.htm)).
- FY26 guide of $9.56–$9.66bn revenue implies +36% YoY at the midpoint (full-year Ansys contribution of ~$2.9bn); operating leverage should accelerate margin recovery as integration costs roll off ([SNPS Q4 FY25 8-K guidance](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000883241/000119312525314200/d29055dex991.htm); [Q4 FY25 prepared remarks](https://s201.q4cdn.com/778493406/files/doc_earnings/2025/q4/transcript/SNPS_Q425_Prepared_Remarks.pdf)).
- Combined TAM expanded to ~$31bn vs. ~$15–19bn EDA-only — meaningfully larger long-term opportunity per management's January 2024 investor materials accompanying the deal ([Synopsys to Acquire Ansys press release, 2024-01-16](https://news.synopsys.com/2024-01-16-Synopsys-to-Acquire-Ansys,-Creating-a-Leader-in-Silicon-to-Systems-Design-Solutions); [Ansys press release "Synopsys to Acquire Ansys", 2024-01-16](https://www.ansys.com/news-center/press-releases/1-16-24-synopsys-acquires-ansys)).
- Ansys is the highest-quality acquisition target in engineering software, with $2.5bn+ revenue and a diversified customer base across aerospace, auto, and industrial verticals ([Ansys 10-K FY2024](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001013462/000101346225000009/anss-20241231.htm)); one-time transaction friction does not impair long-term economics.

## Appendix B — Notes on Methodology

- DCF uses unlevered (firm) cash flow: NOPAT + D&A – Capex – ΔWC
- Terminal value via Gordon Growth: UFCF_T+1 / (WACC – g)
- WACC computed via CAPM for cost of equity + after-tax cost of debt, weighted by capital structure
- Comps use NTM (FY26E) metrics for consistency
- Football-field "weighted blended target" is for reference only; final price target reflects qualitative judgment overlay
