## 1. COMPANY OVERVIEW

Suzhou Leaderdrive Harmonious Drive Systems Co., Ltd. (苏州绿的谐波传动科技股份有限公司, SSE:688017, hereafter "Leaderdrive" or "the company") is a Suzhou-based precision-transmission specialist that designs, manufactures and sells harmonic reducers (谐波减速器), planetary roller screws (行星滚柱丝杠), integrated mechatronic actuators, and intelligent-automation tooling for industrial robots, humanoid robots, machine tools, semiconductor equipment, medical devices and new-energy capital goods ([2024 年年度报告, 第 13 页](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-29/1223107562.PDF)). The company is the largest domestic Chinese maker of harmonic reducers — the small, high-precision, high-reduction-ratio gearboxes that sit in every robot wrist and arm joint — and is widely treated by sell-side analysts and government planners as the only mainland Chinese name with the scale, IP and process know-how to credibly contest Japan's Harmonic Drive Systems (TYO:6324, "HDS") for the global #2 slot.

**What it sells, in plain English.** A harmonic reducer is a thin, pancake-shaped gearbox that converts the high-speed, low-torque output of a servo motor into the low-speed, high-torque, very-low-backlash motion that robotic joints require. Every six-axis industrial robot uses roughly 3–6 such reducers in its smaller (wrist/forearm) joints, paired with cycloidal "RV" reducers in the heavier base/shoulder joints. A humanoid robot — depending on degrees of freedom and dexterous-hand design — uses anywhere from 14 to 40+ rotary reducers plus a similar count of linear actuators built around planetary roller screws. Leaderdrive's harmonic-reducer line (the N-, Y-, E-, LCS, LHD, LHS, LCD, lightweight and ultra-miniature series) is the operational core of the business; the planetary-roller-screw line, the KGU/KAH/KAT/KAS rotary-actuator line, the KGR/KUR/KCR rotary-table line, and the EHA/AGP/AES/ECM/ATB grinding-and-polishing end-effectors are derivatives meant to capture more of the per-robot bill of materials ([2024 年年度报告, 第 13–17 页](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-29/1223107562.PDF)).

**How it makes money.** Leaderdrive sells primarily to robot-arm OEMs and machine-tool/automation equipment integrators on a mix of direct sales (long-term, large customers) and distributor sales (smaller accounts and overseas channels), with FOB-Suzhou export shipping for international customers. Pricing is per-unit; volume rebates apply for major OEMs. Gross margin on harmonic reducers and metal components was 36.1% in 2024, on mechatronic products 41.1%, on intelligent-automation equipment 50.9%, and on the (high-margin, low-volume) machine-tool components 50.3% ([2024 年年度报告, 第 39–40 页](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-29/1223107562.PDF)). Blended gross margin has compressed from 48.7% (2022) to 41.1% (2023) to 37.5% (2024) as the company pushed mix toward higher-volume industrial-robot sales, ran new capacity through depreciation, and absorbed price competition from domestic peers — a margin trajectory that is one of the most contested data points in the bull / bear debate on the stock.

**Where it operates.** Manufacturing, R&D and headquarters are in Wuzhong District, Suzhou, Jiangsu (苏州市吴中区木渎镇尧峰西路 68 号) ([2024 年年度报告, 第 6 页](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-29/1223107562.PDF)). Domestic revenue was RMB 337.8m in 2024 (87% of mainbusiness sales), exports RMB 44.9m (13%, up 49% YoY); the company has overseas sales / R&D platforms in Singapore and Germany, and acquired Germany's Haux precision-components assets in 2024 to localise EU supply and add complementary process technology ([2024 年年度报告, 第 12 页 and 第 40 页](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-29/1223107562.PDF)).

**Scale.** The reporting entity had 823 employees at year-end 2024, of whom 118 (14.3% of headcount) were R&D staff; R&D spend was RMB 49.6m, or 12.8% of revenue ([2024 年年度报告, 第 11 页](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-29/1223107562.PDF)). Revenue was RMB 387.4m in FY2024 (+8.8% YoY) and net profit attributable to shareholders was RMB 56.2m (-33.3% YoY) ([2024 年年度报告, 第 7 页](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-29/1223107562.PDF)). The growth story is best read off the 2025 cadence: Q3-2025 standalone revenue was RMB 155.2m (+49.9% YoY) and 9M-2025 revenue RMB 406.7m (+47.4% YoY); 9M-2025 net profit was RMB 93.7m (+59.2% YoY) ([2025 年第三季度报告, 第 1–3 页](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-10-30/1224173896.PDF)), already exceeding full-year 2024 in nine months. The acceleration is driven, per the Q3 commentary, by "订单和产能同步增长" (orders and capacity growing together) — primarily a recovery in the underlying industrial-robot end market plus initial pilot orders for humanoid programs.

**Valuation snapshot.** Leaderdrive traded at RMB 105.21 per share as of mid-May 2026 with 198m shares outstanding for a market cap of approximately RMB 20.8bn (~USD 2.9bn). On trailing twelve-month (TTM) figures (Q4-2024 + Q1–Q3-2025, total revenue ~RMB 514m and net profit ~RMB 117m), the TTM P/E is roughly **178×** and the TTM P/S roughly **40×** ([Eastmoney 东方财富, 688017 行情](https://quote.eastmoney.com/sh688017.html)). The 3-year P/E range (2023–2026) spans roughly 70× to 230×, with the multiple troughing during the 2023 down-cycle for industrial-robot capex and re-rating sharply during the 2024–2025 humanoid-robot narrative wave. Peer multiples: HDS (TYO:6324) trades at ~95× TTM P/E and ~7× TTM P/S ([Kabutan 6324](https://kabutan.jp/stock/?code=6324)); Nabtesco (TYO:6268) at ~25× P/E and ~1.4× P/S ([Kabutan 6268](https://kabutan.jp/stock/?code=6268)); Shuanghuan Driveline (双环传动, SZSE:002472) at ~38× P/E and ~3.5× P/S ([Eastmoney 002472](https://quote.eastmoney.com/sz002472.html)); Zhongdadi (中大力德, SZSE:002896) at ~95× P/E and ~6× P/S ([Eastmoney 002896](https://quote.eastmoney.com/sz002896.html)); and Laifu Harmonious Drive (来福谐波) is private/pre-IPO with no listed multiple — its last disclosed funding round in 2024 implied a valuation north of RMB 5bn ([36Kr report on Laifu Series C, 2024-06](https://36kr.com/p/2799634123845761)).

The 178× TTM P/E is far above both the A-share industrial median (~25–30×) and the broader humanoid-supplier basket (50–90×). The cause is unambiguous: the stock is the **most directly leveraged pure-play in the world** on the humanoid-robot reducer thesis. With humanoid programs from Tesla, Unitree, UBTECH, Fourier, Agibot and Xpeng each projected to need 14–40 rotary reducers and 6–14 planetary roller screws per unit (versus 3–6 reducers per industrial six-axis robot today), and with Leaderdrive enjoying a 25%+ China share in harmonic reducers ([中商产业研究院 2024 谐波减速器行业报告](https://www.askci.com/news/chanye/20240819/1432155855293.html)) and an in-house roller-screw line just entering small-volume shipments, the market is pricing in a multi-year volume step-change. That is consistent with a sector premium — call it a "AI/humanoid hardware proxy" premium — but it leaves the stock acutely sensitive to (a) any delay in humanoid program ramps, (b) faster-than-expected commoditisation of domestic harmonic reducers, and (c) a sector-rotation reversal. We treat the valuation as a material **Section 9 risk** (see Section 9.4 below).

## 2. COMPANY HISTORY

Leaderdrive's origin story sits inside a story about late-1990s Chinese precision-metalworking. The Zuo (左) family — brothers Zuo Yuyu (左昱昱, the current chairman) and Zuo Jing (左晶, the current vice-chairman) — had been building Suzhou Hengjia Metal Products (苏州恒加金属制品有限公司, "Hengjia"), a contract precision-machining shop, since the early 2000s. By 2003 Hengjia was producing flexible bearings, gear teeth and other small precision parts for industrial customers, and by the late 2000s the team was actively studying the harmonic-drive principle as a way to move up the value chain from machined parts to assembled gearboxes ([2024 年年度报告, 第 57 页 — 高管简历](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-29/1223107562.PDF)).

The corporate entity that ultimately listed as 688017 was incorporated in 2011 ("Leaderdrive Limited", 绿的有限) as a sister company to Hengjia, with the explicit mandate of commercialising a homegrown harmonic-reducer design ([Leaderdrive 公司简介](https://www.leaderdrive.com/about.html)). The first generation of products was based on a conventional double-circular-arc tooth profile competitive with mid-tier Japanese and Korean offerings. The breakthrough that defined the company commercially came around 2013–2015, when the R&D team — under technical chief Li Qian (李谦) — moved off the standard tooth profile and developed what they branded the **"P-tooth" (P 齿形)** harmonic meshing system, claiming higher torsional stiffness, longer service life and a smoother torque ripple than the incumbent Japanese designs ([2024 年年度报告, 第 13 页](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-29/1223107562.PDF)).

The 2014–2018 period was the company's first commercial inflection: domestic robot-arm OEMs — primarily Estun (埃斯顿, SZSE:002747), Inovance (汇川技术, SZSE:300124), Siasun (新松机器人, SZSE:300024) and EFORT (埃夫特, SSE:688165) — needed a domestic reducer source to escape Harmonic Drive Systems' notorious 6–12-month allocation queues during the 2017 robot boom, and Leaderdrive was the only company with both the IP and the production yield to fill the gap. The company doubled production capacity twice in that window; by 2018, Chinese sell-side coverage was uniformly framing Leaderdrive as "国产替代第一股" (first listed national-substitution play) in robotics components ([东方财富 - 绿的谐波 上市公开发行说明书](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2020-08-25/1208236965.PDF), prospectus).

**IPO and first listed year (2020).** Leaderdrive listed on the Shanghai STAR Market (科创板) on 22 September 2020 at an issue price of RMB 34.06 per share, raising approximately RMB 800m to fund the "annual 500,000-unit precision-reducer capacity expansion" project at the Suzhou Mudu site ([Leaderdrive IPO prospectus / 招股说明书, 2020-09](http://www.sse.com.cn/disclosure/listedinfo/announcement/c/new/2020-09-15/688017_20200915_2.pdf)). The stock more than tripled in its first week — peaking around RMB 215 — on the same humanoid-and-cobot theme that drives the multiple today, then de-rated through 2021 and 2022 as broader industrial-automation demand softened.

**2021–2023: capacity build-out, cyclical air pocket.** Revenue peaked at RMB 445.7m in 2022 and then declined to RMB 356.2m in 2023 as the post-COVID industrial-capex cycle rolled over and Chinese collaborative-robot pricing collapsed ([2024 年年度报告, 第 7 页](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-29/1223107562.PDF)). The 500,000-unit capacity expansion was largely complete by year-end 2024, with the company stating that "the project has fully reached its production-capacity target and 2025 will see steady ramp" ([2024 年年度报告, 第 12 页](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-29/1223107562.PDF)). The trough proved short: 9M-2025 revenue is already up 47% YoY.

**2024: humanoid pivot and German acquisition.** In 2024 the company made three strategic moves: (1) it acquired the German precision-components business of Haux (绿的谐波收购德国 Haux 公司资产), giving it an EU manufacturing footprint and complementary surface-treatment / machining IP; (2) it formally rolled out the planetary-roller-screw line, branded for the linear-actuator joints of humanoids; and (3) it set up the Jiangsu Provincial Key Laboratory of Embodied-Intelligence Robotics (江苏省具身智能机器人技术重点实验室) at its Suzhou site and won a national 重点研发计划 grant for a "high-power-density integrated linear servo joint" project ([2024 年年度报告, 第 11–12 页](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-29/1223107562.PDF)).

**2024 follow-on offering.** In late 2024 the board approved a private placement (定向增发) targeted at strategic investors; the placement closed in 2025 and was the dominant driver of the +159.8% YoY change in financing cash flow in the 2024 annual report ([2024 年年度报告, 第 39 页](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-29/1223107562.PDF)). Proceeds fund the **"新一代精密传动装置智能制造项目"** (next-generation precision-transmission intelligent manufacturing project), which began construction in 2025 and is expected to add another ~600,000 units/year of harmonic-reducer capacity plus dedicated planetary-roller-screw lines.

**2025 to date: order book inflection and humanoid pilot programs.** The Q3-2025 release flagged that revenue, net profit and operating cash flow all grew at >45% YoY simultaneously, a combination that had not occurred since 2018 ([2025 年第三季度报告, 第 1 页](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-10-30/1224173896.PDF)). Management has been notably more vocal about humanoid wins in 2024–2025 investor-day language than in any prior period, including direct references to Unitree, UBTECH and Fourier in trade-press coverage, and indirect references (via Tier-1 module integrators) to Tesla Optimus's supply chain ([中国机器人网 2025-09 报道](https://www.robot-china.com/news/202509/leaderdrive_humanoid.html)).

His public profile is unusually low-key for a high-profile A-share founder — he gives few interviews and has historically delegated investor-facing communication to the company secretary and vice-chairman — but the operational pattern through Leaderdrive's history (rapid capacity adds during 2017–2018 and 2020–2024, willingness to absorb gross-margin compression to defend share, aggressive R&D as a percent of sales) is consistent with a long-horizon technical founder. ## 4. PRODUCTS & SERVICES

Leaderdrive groups its catalogue into four buckets: **(A) harmonic reducers and precision components**, **(B) planetary roller screws**, **(C) mechatronic / electrohydraulic integrated products**, and **(D) intelligent-automation equipment / robotic end-effectors** ([2024 年年度报告, 第 13–18 页](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-29/1223107562.PDF)). The first bucket is the company's economic core; the others are deliberate vertical extensions designed to capture more of the per-robot bill of materials and to defend pricing.

### A. Harmonic reducers and precision components

This is the **flagship business** and generated RMB 325.4m of revenue in 2024, or 84% of total ([2024 年年度报告, 第 40 页](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-29/1223107562.PDF)). The line consists of nine technical series:

1. **N series** — a long-life, high-cycle harmonic reducer using a specialised flex-spline and bearing process plus tooth-profile optimisation, targeting "fast-cycle, high-reliability, low-maintenance" duty cycles. Typical end-application: industrial six-axis robot wrist joints. **Competitive advantage: yes (technology + scale moat).** Closest competitor product is HDS's CSG/CSF series ([HDS product page](https://www.hds.co.jp/english/products/lineup/csgcsf/)); the N series is at parity on rated torque and slightly behind on torsional-stiffness specs at small frame sizes.
2. **Y series** — the company's flagship high-precision series, built on its proprietary "third-harmonic" technology that replaces the conventional second-harmonic meshing scheme. Marketed as ultra-high precision, high load capacity, high stiffness, low vibration. End application: precision assembly, semiconductor handlers, medical robotics. **Competitive advantage: yes (proprietary IP, P-tooth + third-harmonic).** Closest competitor: HDS CSD series; Y series is at parity on positioning accuracy and meaningfully ahead on price.
3. **E series** — vibration-optimised, with reformulated heat-treatment and sealing for 3–5× lower grease leakage than the prior generation. Target: semiconductor lithography / inspection equipment and medical robots. **Advantage: partial (process moat).** Closest competitor: Nabtesco RV-E (in a different gear topology); HDS SHF/SHG (in harmonic). Behind on installed base, at parity on clean-room specs.
4. **LCS / LCS(G) series** — cup-shaped flex-spline, standard cylindrical design with key-coupled wave generator. General-purpose. **Advantage: partial.** Commoditised slice; competes directly with Laifu Harmonic, Leaderdrive's main domestic harmonic-only rival.
5. **LHD series** — ultra-thin hollow-shaft flanged flex-spline; flat profile for height-constrained installations. **Advantage: yes (specialised geometry).** HDS has a similar SHD line; pricing typically 30–40% below.
6. **LHS / LHS(G) series** — hollow flanged standard-cylinder, integrated wave-generator with its own input shaft and bearing support; fully sealed. Designed for applications needing bevel-gear or timing-belt input drive. **Advantage: partial.** Direct competition from HDS SHF and Laifu.
7. **LCD series** — ultra-thin cup-shaped flex-spline, compact and lightweight, designed for end-of-arm or client-side joints. **Advantage: yes.** A favourite for collaborative-robot wrist joints; competes with HDS SHD.
8. **Lightweight harmonic (轻量型谐波)** — built around lightweight materials (likely aluminium-alloy flex-spline) and optimised structure to keep performance constant while cutting mass. The company claims "30%+ weight reduction at equivalent output force" ([2024 年年度报告, 第 11 页](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-29/1223107562.PDF)). **Advantage: yes (humanoid-tailored).** This is the line most directly aimed at humanoid customers, where every gram of mass at a distal joint costs disproportionate torque at the proximal joints. Closest competitor: HDS's recent UR-series humanoid-optimised line.
9. **Ultra-miniature harmonic (超小型谐波)** — outer diameter down to 6 mm, designed for dexterous-hand finger joints, surgical-robot wrist tools, and miniature semiconductor handlers ([2024 年年度报告, 第 14 页](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-29/1223107562.PDF)). **Advantage: yes (geometry + process moat).** This is a deeply specialised niche; only HDS, Sumitomo and a small number of Japanese workshops produce comparable sub-10mm reducers.
10. **Three-component harmonic (三组件式谐波)** — sold as the three loose components (circular spline, flex-spline, wave-generator) so OEMs can integrate the bearing pack themselves. **Advantage: partial.** Used mostly by sophisticated humanoid integrators (e.g. dexterous-hand makers) who want to build their own bearing structure.

Alongside these reducer SKU families, the segment includes **customised precision components** — stainless-steel, aluminium, iron and copper machined parts, partly internal use and partly third-party contract manufacturing for industrial-robot, electrical-equipment and energy-equipment customers ([2024 年年度报告, 第 14 页](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-29/1223107562.PDF)). This is the legacy Hengjia metalworking business.

### B. Planetary roller screws (行星滚柱丝杠)

A relatively new product line, formally rolled out in 2024 and disclosed for the first time as a distinct SKU family. Planetary roller screws use threaded rollers orbiting a threaded shaft to convert rotary into linear motion with much higher load capacity, stiffness, shock resistance and life than ball screws ([2024 年年度报告, 第 15 页](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-29/1223107562.PDF)). The humanoid use-case is critical: each linear joint in a humanoid leg / hip uses one roller screw plus driver / encoder; a humanoid with 6 linear joints needs 6 screws. The high-end global market is dominated by Rollvis, GSA Technology (acquired by Schaeffler), and Ewellix; in China, Hengli Hydraulics, Beite Technology and Hanjiang Technology are the visible competitors. Leaderdrive's own assessment ([2024 年年度报告, 第 20 页](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-29/1223107562.PDF)) is honest about the gap: while Chinese single-piece accuracy can match international levels, "noise rises and accuracy stability degrades during volume production" and "accuracy holding is only two-thirds of foreign high-end products." That said, the company has explicit IP positions on (a) cross-scale multi-physics simulation models for tooth design, (b) external-thread profile grinding, (c) large-aspect-ratio internal-thread grinding, and (d) a quick-assembly process for high-consistency volume builds. **Advantage: partial today; potentially strong moat in 24–36 months.** Closest competitor: Beite Technology (恒立液压's roller-screw subsidiary).

### C. Mechatronic / electrohydraulic integrated products

These are integrated "joint modules" combining a harmonic reducer, a frameless torque motor, a high-resolution absolute encoder, optional brake and a controller — i.e. the full electromechanical actuator a humanoid or cobot integrator needs ([2024 年年度报告, 第 15–17 页](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-29/1223107562.PDF)). The series:

- **KGU rotary executor** — ultra-light, ultra-thin, very-high torque density; built-in hollow absolute encoder + low-voltage servo driver; optional torque sensing. Explicitly positioned for "intelligent robots" — the language used in the filing for humanoid-class machines.
- **KAH rotary executor** — integrated harmonic + frameless torque motor + high-accuracy absolute encoder + smart sensor; 10 arc-second positioning accuracy; up to 800 N·m torque; cable/air-tube hollow bore.
- **KAT rotary executor** — KAH variant with a larger hollow bore, 2 arc-second accuracy, up to 1,000 N·m torque, aimed at semiconductors and 3C.
- **KDE bus-type servo driver** — EtherCAT/CANopen, high-bandwidth, vibration suppression, adaptive parameter optimisation, strong EMC.
- **KMF frameless torque motor** — low cogging, low vibration, F-class insulation, carbon-fibre rotor sleeve.
- **KAS rotary executor** — high power-density servo motor variant.
- **Linear executor** — high-precision planetary-roller-screw-based linear module + direct-drive motor. The humanoid-leg play.
- **KGR / KUR / KCR five- and four-axis CNC turntable** — high-rigidity, high-precision turntable assemblies for machine tools; ±1.5 arc-second indexing accuracy, 1.2 arc-second repeat accuracy. This product family targets the high-end Chinese machine-tool market (DMG-Mori, Mazak, Tongtai, Yiwa) and competes against Japanese turntable specialists like Tsudakoma and Nikken.
- **Electro-hydraulic actuator (EHA)** — a compact, integrated hydraulic actuator combining servo motor, pump, tank, actuator and sensors.
- **Valve-controlled actuator** — electro-hydraulic servo valve + actuator + position/pressure sensors.

Mechatronic revenue was RMB 52.6m in 2024 (+56.7% YoY); this segment is the **second-fastest-growing line** and is the one most leveraged to the humanoid-volume thesis. **Advantage: yes (system-integration moat).** Closest direct competitor: HDS's integrated actuator line for collaborative robots, plus domestic challengers like Inovance's own joint modules.

### D. Intelligent-automation equipment / robotic end-effectors

Robot-end tooling — adaptive grinding/polishing tools, flexible deburring/chamfering machines, automated thread go/no-go inspection, hydraulic polishing tools, FMS lines, digital-factory packages ([2024 年年度报告, 第 17–18 页](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-29/1223107562.PDF)). The branded sub-lines are AGP (adaptive grinding/polishing), AES (floating electric spindle), ECM (electro-hydraulic compliance module), ATB (triangular sanding belt) and a roller-hemming floating module aimed at automotive body shops. This is a small, high-margin (50.9% gross margin in 2024) segment that demonstrates customer-facing application know-how but is not central to the investment thesis.

### Flagship vs. long-tail; recent launches

The **flagship trio driving the equity story** is: (i) harmonic reducers — the lightweight and ultra-miniature series specifically, both directly humanoid-tailored; (ii) planetary roller screws — the new linear-motion line; and (iii) the KGU/KAH/KAS integrated rotary actuators that bundle reducer + motor + encoder + control. The legacy general-purpose harmonic series (LCS, LHS), the precision-components contract-manufacturing business, the CNC turntables (KGR/KUR/KCR) and the EHA / AGP grinding-and-polishing tools are stable contributors but not the marginal-value drivers.

**Last-12-month launches:** the **ultra-miniature (down to 6mm OD) harmonic series**, the **lightweight humanoid harmonic series with claimed 30%+ mass reduction**, the **first production lots of planetary roller screws**, and **more than 10 different SKU configurations of linear servo joints** built on the in-house roller screw — all disclosed in the 2024 annual report as 2024 launches ([2024 年年度报告, 第 11–12 页](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-29/1223107562.PDF)). No discontinued / sunset products were disclosed.

The 2024 revenue split by downstream industry is: **industrial-and-service-robot components RMB 276.9m (71.5% of mainbusiness sales, gross margin 36.6%)**; mechanical equipment components RMB 66.6m (17.2%, GM 30.9%); CNC-machine-tool components RMB 31.5m (8.1%, GM 50.3%); medical-device components RMB 7.0m (1.8%, GM 51.4%); other RMB 0.7m (0.2%) ([2024 年年度报告, 第 39–40 页](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-29/1223107562.PDF)).

The largest single customer (~9% in 2024) is below the 10% "must-name" trigger so identities are masked in the filing; trade-press cross-references and channel checks strongly suggest the named buyers among the top five are some combination of **Estun (埃斯顿, SZSE:002747), Inovance (汇川技术, SZSE:300124), Siasun (新松, SZSE:300024), Efort (埃夫特, SSE:688165), JAKA, ELITE Robot, Han's Robot and Rokae** ([Leaderdrive 投资者关系互动平台 — 2024 年问答](http://sns.sseinfo.com/qa.do?id=1450)). However, the concentration trend is worth tracking: if any one humanoid OEM — Tesla, Unitree or Xpeng — ramps to 50,000+ units while others are still in pilot, the top-1 could spike into the high teens within 12–18 months. The supply contract structure with major customers is a master-supply agreement with rolling 12-month purchase orders, with pricing renegotiated annually — this is standard for the segment and provides modest visibility but not multi-year revenue lock-in.

**Named humanoid customers.** Trade press and the 2024 annual-report colour-commentary on the "embodied-intelligence robotics" laboratory and the linear-servo-joint program imply working relationships with the leading Chinese humanoid programs. The names that recur in coverage and that are credible based on the product-fit profile are **Unitree Robotics (宇树科技, Hangzhou)**, **UBTECH (优必选, HKEX:9880)**, **Fourier Intelligence (傅利叶智能, Shanghai)**, **Agibot (智元机器人, Shanghai)**, **Xpeng Motors (小鹏 Iron humanoid program, HKEX:9868 / NYSE:XPEV)** and **Leju Robotics (乐聚机器人)**. Tesla Optimus exposure is the central bull-case wildcard: Leaderdrive has not been disclosed as a direct named supplier to Tesla, but several Tier-1 module integrators bidding for Optimus joint-module supply (notably Sanhua Intelligent Controls, Tuopu Group and certain Taiwanese motor module makers) source harmonic reducers from a small pool that is dominated by HDS, Leaderdrive, Laifu and Shenzhen-based Sumitomo joint ventures. The company's own language on this is cautious: management has avoided naming any specific humanoid OEM in periodic-disclosure filings and has confined humanoid commentary to investor-day Q&A.

**Go-to-market.** Domestic sales (87% of total) use a mix of direct and distributor channels — long-term and large-volume customers and customers in the Suzhou/Yangtze-Delta region get direct sales; smaller and out-of-region customers are served by certified distributors under the 经销商管理制度 ([2024 年年度报告, 第 19 页](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-29/1223107562.PDF)). Export sales (13% of total, growing 49.4% YoY in 2024) are settled on FOB terms; the new German / Singapore platforms are intended to convert the export channel from container-load-driven distributor sales into a localised direct-service model. Sales cycle for a new customer typically runs 6–12 months for sample qualification, 12–24 months for design-in, then 12+ months of volume ramp — i.e. an OEM win in 2026 starts contributing meaningful revenue in 2028.

**Key partnerships.** Most-cited public partnerships are (a) the National Key R&D Program "high-power-density integrated linear servo joint" project (with co-PIs from Beihang and Tsinghua), (b) the Jiangsu Provincial Key Laboratory of Embodied-Intelligence Robotics, and (c) the joint manufacturing-process work with the acquired Haux (Germany) for European supply ([2024 年年度报告, 第 11–12 页](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-29/1223107562.PDF)). The independent director from ABB (Li Gang) is a soft signal that the ABB Shanghai facility is a customer or design partner, though this has not been formally disclosed.

## 6. INDUSTRY OVERVIEW

The relevant industry is **precision robotic transmission**, which decomposes into three sub-industries: (1) **harmonic reducers** (for industrial-robot wrist/forearm joints, cobot all joints, humanoid most joints); (2) **cycloidal "RV" reducers** (for industrial-robot base/shoulder joints, heavier humanoid joints); and (3) **planetary roller screws** (for humanoid linear joints, electric-actuation conversions of hydraulic systems, semiconductor stage drives). Leaderdrive plays in (1) and (3) directly and is developing an "RV-equivalent" product extension on the cycloidal side ([2024 年年度报告, 第 11 页](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-29/1223107562.PDF)).

**Market size — harmonic reducers.** Global harmonic-reducer revenue was approximately **USD 1.6bn in 2023** by IFR / GGII consensus, of which roughly USD 600m was inside China ([GGII 高工产业研究院 2024 谐波减速器市场报告](https://www.gg-ii.com/article-90-1-1.html)). The Chinese market is forecast to grow at a 15–18% CAGR over 2024–2028 under a "base case" (industrial-robot installations growing 8–10% with continued reducer-content gain) and at a 25–35% CAGR under a "humanoid-bull" case (modest humanoid volume ramp by 2028). HDS alone has ~50% global share; Leaderdrive has ~25% Chinese share and 8–10% global share; Laifu Harmonious has ~10% Chinese share; everyone else (Sumitomo, Shenzhen Hanyi, Zhongdadi, etc.) is in low single digits.

**Market size — planetary roller screws.** Global revenue is much smaller today — estimates cluster around **USD 200–400m in 2023** — but the addressable expansion is dramatic if even one large humanoid program reaches mass volume. A Tesla Optimus running 14–28 roller screws per unit at 100,000 units/year would alone add USD 200–400m of demand. Bulge-bracket sell-side notes have published a wide range of 2030 TAM estimates from USD 1bn to USD 8bn depending on humanoid adoption assumptions ([东吴证券 2024 人形机器人关键零部件深度报告](https://www.dwzq.com.cn/research/202412/20241215_humanoid_components.pdf)). The high end (~USD 8bn) assumes humanoid volumes of ~1–2m units globally by 2030 with 14+ screws per unit — aggressive but not impossible if Tesla, Unitree and Chinese national-program funded humanoid manufacturers all reach scale.

**Growth drivers.**
- **Industrial-robot installation cycle.** Global industrial-robot installations crossed 540,000 units in 2023 with China at 290,000 ([IFR World Robotics 2024](https://ifr.org/ifr-press-releases/news/world-robotics-2024-record-557000-industrial-robots-installed-in-china)). Sustained 8–10% global growth through 2028 is the consensus base case.
- **Collaborative-robot (cobot) penetration.** Cobots use harmonic reducers in every joint (versus 3–6 in a traditional six-axis); cobot volume growing 30%+ YoY adds disproportionate reducer demand.
- **Humanoid robot programs.** This is the dominant 2025–2030 growth narrative. The reducer-per-unit content step-change is roughly **5–10× per humanoid versus per industrial robot**.
- **Semiconductor capex normalisation.** The E-series and KAT series are leveraged to semiconductor handler/lithography spend, where 2025 was a recovery year after 2023's down-cycle.
- **National-substitution mandate.** China's "Made in China 2025" and subsequent industrial-policy documents explicitly target domestic precision-reducer self-sufficiency; Leaderdrive is one of the most visible beneficiaries.

**Regulatory environment.** Few hard regulatory constraints — harmonic reducers and roller screws are not export-controlled in either direction at the component level (though dual-use export-control jurisdiction over precision threaded-rod grinding machines is a real input-side constraint, see Section 9). The company is a principal drafter of several Chinese national standards for precision reducers ([2024 年年度报告, 第 13 页](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-29/1223107562.PDF)).

**Industry structure.**
- **Buyer power: moderate-to-high.** Robot OEMs are concentrated (top 10 globally take 70%+ of unit volume) and second-source aggressively; Tier-1 humanoid integrators are even more concentrated.
- **Supplier power: moderate.** Input materials (bearing-grade steel, frameless-motor copper) are commoditised; precision-grinding machine tools (for thread grinding) are a single-source bottleneck dominated by German and Swiss machine builders (Studer, Reishauer, EMAG) and Japanese (Okuma, Mori) — Leaderdrive has flagged this dependency directly ([2024 年年度报告, 第 20 页](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-29/1223107562.PDF)).
- **Threat of substitutes: low in the near term.** Direct-drive motors are a theoretical substitute for harmonic reducers in some applications but lose on torque density and cost; the harmonic-reducer category is structurally protected for the next decade.
- **Competitive intensity inside China: rising.** Laifu and Zhongdadi are pushing volumes; price competition in the low-end LCS-equivalent products has accelerated, which is the proximate cause of Leaderdrive's gross-margin compression.
- **Barriers to entry: very high.** A successful harmonic-reducer line requires (a) proprietary tooth-profile IP, (b) ultra-precision grinding/measurement infrastructure (USD tens of millions of capex), (c) 5–10 years of process-yield learning curve, and (d) deep customer-qualification cycles (12–24 months per OEM). Few credible new entrants in the last decade.

## 7. COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE

### Direct competitors — harmonic reducers

**1. Harmonic Drive Systems (HDS, TYO:6324).** The Japanese incumbent and inventor of the harmonic-drive principle; ~50% global market share, dominant in Japan, Europe, North America. ~JPY 60bn revenue (FY2024), 15%+ gross margin profile, multi-decade design-in relationships with KUKA, Fanuc, ABB, Yaskawa, Universal Robots, and Tesla (for Optimus). Trades at ~95× P/E TTM, ~7× P/S TTM ([Kabutan 6324](https://kabutan.jp/stock/?code=6324)). HDS is **ahead** on installed base, brand, supply discipline, and the very smallest frame sizes; **at parity** with Leaderdrive on mid-frame harmonic torque-density specs; **behind** on price (HDS premium ranges from 30% to 100%+ depending on SKU) and on capacity availability during shortages.

**2. Laifu Harmonious Drive (来福谐波, private, pre-IPO).** Leaderdrive's principal domestic harmonic-reducer rival. Founded in 2010, based in Zhejiang. Estimated ~10% of Chinese harmonic-reducer volume. Raised a sizeable late-stage round at a reported >RMB 5bn valuation in 2024 and has been pursuing a STAR Market IPO ([36Kr Laifu 2024 funding](https://36kr.com/p/2799634123845761)). Laifu is **behind** Leaderdrive on scale, brand depth and the higher-spec / smaller-frame series, but is aggressive on price and has won significant share at mid-tier Chinese cobot OEMs (notably JAKA and some lines of ELITE Robot). Real ongoing pressure on Leaderdrive's commoditised SKUs.

**3. Sumitomo Heavy Industries (TYO:6302).** Japanese conglomerate with a smaller but global harmonic-reducer line (the Cyclo line is actually their cycloidal product, but they also sell harmonic). At parity on engineering; behind on share and pricing.

**4. Shenzhen Hanyi Precision (深圳汉宜精密).** Smaller Chinese player. Behind on most dimensions.

### Direct competitors — RV / cycloidal reducers

**5. Nabtesco (TYO:6268).** Japanese category leader in cycloidal RV reducers (the large, base-of-arm reducers); 60%+ global share in cycloidal. Not a direct head-to-head with Leaderdrive's harmonic-reducer line but **shares the industrial-robot reducer market** and is the comp the sell-side most often pairs with Leaderdrive ex-HDS. Trades at ~25× P/E TTM, ~1.4× P/S TTM — substantially cheaper than Leaderdrive and a useful re-rating reference point.

**6. Shuanghuan Driveline (双环传动, SZSE:002472).** China's leading independent precision-gear maker, with a growing RV / cycloidal reducer line (sold under the "Huandong" / 环动 brand). Trades at ~38× P/E TTM, ~3.5× P/S TTM ([Eastmoney 002472](https://quote.eastmoney.com/sz002472.html)). Shuanghuan is **ahead** in cycloidal RV-equivalent products; Leaderdrive is **ahead** in harmonic; the two companies overlap on humanoid-program shortlists.

**7. Zhongdadi (中大力德, SZSE:002896).** Ningbo-based, broad precision-transmission catalogue — harmonic, cycloidal, planetary, and worm-gear. Smaller than Leaderdrive in harmonic but a credible secondary supplier to mid-tier robot OEMs. Trades at ~95× P/E TTM, ~6× P/S TTM. The TTM P/E being similar to Leaderdrive's reflects the same narrative-premium dynamic, applied to a smaller and less-pure-play name.

### Direct competitors — planetary roller screws

**8. Hengli Hydraulics (恒立液压, SSE:601100).** Established hydraulics / linear-motion player with a dedicated roller-screw subsidiary (Hengli Cixi). Larger and better-capitalised than Leaderdrive's roller-screw line. **Ahead** on roller-screw scale and machine-tool feedback; **behind** on integration into a humanoid joint-module roadmap.

**9. Schaeffler (ETR:SHA) / Ewellix.** Acquired Ewellix in 2022; global roller-screw category leader.

**10. Rollvis (Swiss, private).** Niche, ultra-high-spec roller-screw incumbent.

### Indirect / adjacent competitors

**11. Inovance (汇川技术, SZSE:300124).** China's largest servo-motor and drive maker, and the most likely to vertically integrate into harmonic reducers. Has been a Leaderdrive customer and is now developing its own joint-module line for collaborative and humanoid applications. **Key strategic wildcard** — both customer and emerging competitor.

**12. RaaS / direct-drive motor disruptors.** Companies like Genesis Robotics' "LiveDrive" or various university-spin-off direct-drive motor projects are theoretical substitutes, but practical penetration remains tiny.

### Positioning summary

**Price**: Leaderdrive is the **cost leader** in mid- and high-tier Chinese harmonic reducers, typically priced 30–50% below HDS for equivalent specs. Laifu undercuts Leaderdrive by ~15–25% on commoditised SKUs.

**Features / performance**: Leaderdrive is **at parity** with HDS on mid-frame torque density and accuracy; **behind** HDS on ultra-small (<14mm) frame sizes and on documented design-in maturity; **ahead** of all other Chinese rivals on the proprietary P-tooth and third-harmonic IP plus the lightweight / ultra-miniature series tuned for humanoid joints.

**Scale**: Leaderdrive's 500,000-unit/year nameplate capacity (with another 600,000 under construction) is roughly half of HDS's effective annual capacity and 3–5× the next-largest Chinese competitor.

**Vulnerabilities.** (i) Inovance vertical-integration risk — a single customer becoming a competitor would be material. (ii) Gross-margin compression — three consecutive years of decline (48.7 → 41.1 → 37.5%) raises the question of whether the pricing umbrella is permanently lower or whether 2024 was a trough. (iii) Roller-screw execution — the company is openly behind global leaders today, and the humanoid bull case demands it close the gap inside 24 months. (iv) Single-site manufacturing concentration in Suzhou.

## 8. MARKET OPPORTUNITY (TAM)

**TAM (2028).** A defensible mid-case TAM for Leaderdrive's addressable precision-transmission market (harmonic reducers + planetary roller screws + integrated joint modules) in 2028 is approximately **USD 5–7bn** globally, of which roughly **USD 2.5–3.5bn** is in China.

Decomposition of the 2028 mid-case TAM:
- **Harmonic reducers (industrial + cobot)**: USD 2.4bn global by 2028, applying a 9% CAGR off the ~USD 1.6bn 2023 base ([GGII 2024 报告](https://www.gg-ii.com/article-90-1-1.html)).
- **Harmonic reducers (humanoid pilot volumes)**: USD 0.5–1.5bn by 2028, assuming 200,000–500,000 humanoid units sold globally with ~12 harmonic reducers each at ~USD 150 ASP.
- **Planetary roller screws (industrial + humanoid)**: USD 0.8–1.5bn by 2028, growing from a ~USD 300m 2023 base with humanoid contributing 50–60% of the increment.
- **Integrated rotary actuator / joint modules**: USD 1.0–1.5bn by 2028, capturing some of the value previously sold separately as reducer + motor + driver.

**SAM (Leaderdrive's serviceable share) in 2028.** Leaderdrive's serviceable addressable market — the share of the TAM where it has product fit, distribution and qualification — is roughly **USD 2.5–4bn**, comprising essentially all of China + the export-to-EU channel via the Haux platform + early Tier-1 humanoid OEM positions. Excluded: the >USD 1bn HDS-locked Japanese / Yaskawa / Fanuc captive supply that Leaderdrive realistically will not win on a competitive bid.

**SOM (Leaderdrive's likely realised share) in 2028.** A mid-case 2028 revenue projection — RMB 2.5–3.5bn (USD 350–500m) — would correspond to roughly **10–15% of TAM** and **15–20% of SAM**. This is consistent with the company's current ~10% global / 25% China share in harmonic reducers, with upside from the roller-screw ramp and humanoid OEM wins, partially offset by domestic price competition. A bull case of >RMB 5bn 2028 revenue requires either a Tesla Optimus award or a domestic humanoid program reaching 100,000+ units/year by 2028.

**Penetration strategy.** Leaderdrive's stated growth strategy ([2024 年年度报告, 第 10–12 页](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-29/1223107562.PDF)) is six-pronged: (1) deepen the harmonic-reducer technology lead through the third-harmonic-and-P-tooth roadmap; (2) close the roller-screw gap and bring it to production-grade quality within 18–24 months; (3) productise the joint-module bundle so customers can buy a complete actuator rather than parts; (4) localise EU manufacturing through Haux to shorten lead times for European OEMs; (5) build a Singapore + Germany overseas sales-and-engineering footprint; (6) lift gross margin via mix and scale once the new "next-generation precision transmission intelligent manufacturing project" capacity is on-line.

## 9. RISK ASSESSMENT

### 9.1 Company-Specific Risks

**1. Gross-margin compression / pricing erosion (severity: high).** Blended gross margin has fallen from 48.7% (2022) to 41.1% (2023) to 37.5% (2024) — a 1,100 bps compression in two years on the back of mix shift, depreciation from new capacity, and price pressure from Laifu Harmonious and other domestic rivals ([2024 年年度报告, 第 37 页](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-29/1223107562.PDF)). Even with the strong 2025 volume recovery, gross margin remains under pressure. If the company cannot push high-spec humanoid-class SKUs and integrated joint modules into the mix fast enough, gross margin could trough at 30–35% rather than recover to the 45%+ pre-2022 levels.

**2. Key-person dependency on the Zuo family and Li Qian (severity: medium-high).** The chairman, vice-chairman and the CTO together hold the institutional memory on the proprietary P-tooth design, the third-harmonic process and the volume-build know-how. The 2024 succession to Zhang Yuwen (36) as GM is a sensible long-horizon move but transfers concentrated execution responsibility to a relatively young executive who has not yet been tested through a full down-cycle.

**3. Customer-vertical-integration risk (severity: medium-high).** The two-customer-becomes-competitor scenario is the most asymmetric company-specific risk. Inovance (a top-5-class customer) is publicly developing in-house joint modules and could materially reduce its harmonic-reducer buy from Leaderdrive within 24 months. Estun has similar capability. Mitigant: the customer's own roller-screw / harmonic-reducer build-out is non-trivial and will take 3–5 years; switching costs for already-designed-in robot platforms are real.

**4. Single-site manufacturing concentration (severity: low-medium).** All current production sits at the Suzhou Mudu site. The next-generation capacity expansion is being built at the same campus. A single-site disruption (fire, flood, regulatory action, regional power constraint) would interrupt revenue with no immediate redundancy. Mitigant: the German Haux site adds a second physical footprint, though at small initial scale.

**5. Customer-concentration trend risk (severity: low — disclosure-driven monitoring).** 2024 top-5 = 34.7%, top-1 = 9.06% — both below the standard "material" thresholds. The 3-year top-5 trend (35.99 → 37.59 → 34.71) is stable. Concentration could spike if a single humanoid OEM ramps disproportionately; flagged as monitoring item rather than current material risk.

**6. Roller-screw execution risk (severity: medium).** The 2024 annual report ([第 20 页](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-29/1223107562.PDF)) candidly acknowledges that domestic roller-screw accuracy degrades at volume and that holding-accuracy is two-thirds of foreign high-end. The humanoid bull case requires Leaderdrive to close this gap. If it does not, the linear-actuator share of the TAM goes to Hengli, Schaeffler or imports.

### 9.2 Industry / Market Risks

**7. Humanoid-robot ramp delay (severity: high).** The valuation premium embedded in the stock requires meaningful humanoid volumes by 2027–2028. Optimus, Unitree, UBTECH and Xpeng programs are all early-stage; any combination of cost overruns, safety regulation, productisation delay, or AI-foundation-model limitations could push volumes to 2030 or beyond. A 2–3 year slip would force a meaningful de-rate.

**8. Domestic harmonic-reducer price competition (severity: high).** Laifu and other entrants are aggressively pursuing share, which has already shown up in Leaderdrive's gross margin. If Laifu's IPO funds a multi-year price war, the structural gross-margin level may stay in the 30s.

**9. Industrial-robot cyclicality (severity: medium).** ~71% of 2024 revenue came from industrial / service-robot OEM components. This cycle is correlated to broader manufacturing capex. A 2026–2027 China manufacturing-capex slowdown would hit the legacy bucket hard.

**10. Geopolitical export-control on input equipment (severity: medium).** The precision-thread-grinding machines required for the highest-accuracy roller-screw lines are sourced from German and Swiss specialists (Studer, Reishauer, EMAG) and Japanese (Okuma) and have been the subject of Western export-control tightening for advanced applications. Leaderdrive flagged this dependency directly ([2024 年年度报告, 第 20 页](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-29/1223107562.PDF)).

### 9.3 Financial Risks

**11. Valuation / multiple-compression risk (severity: high).** TTM P/E of approximately 178× and TTM P/S of approximately 40× is roughly **6× the A-share industrial median (P/E ~30×)** and **2× the closest sector comp Zhongdadi at ~95×**. The stock is implicitly pricing in a multi-year humanoid-volume step-change. A negative trigger — Tesla Optimus volume disappointment, a humanoid-program safety incident, a domestic price-war headline, a Laifu IPO that funds an aggressive market-share grab, or simply a sector rotation out of "AI hardware proxies" — could compress the multiple to a more defensible 50–80× P/E range (a 50–70% drawdown from current levels) even without a meaningful change in 12-month forward earnings. The 3-year multiple range (~70× to ~230×) demonstrates how violently these multiples move on narrative.

**12. FX exposure on growing exports (severity: low-medium).** Export sales (RMB 44.9m, +49.4% YoY in 2024) are denominated in EUR and USD with no formal hedge program disclosed ([2024 年年度报告, 第 37 页](http://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-29/1223107562.PDF)). RMB appreciation against EUR/USD by 5–10% would compress export gross margins; the absolute exposure is currently modest but is the segment growing fastest.

### 9.4 Macroeconomic Risks

**13. China industrial-capex sensitivity (severity: medium).** The industrial-robot end market is procyclical to Chinese manufacturing capex, which is leveraged to property completion (still weak), auto-sector capex (mixed), and export-driven manufacturing demand. A 2026 China industrial-capex slowdown would hit the 71%-of-revenue industrial-robot bucket.

**14. US-China decoupling / tariff escalation (severity: low-medium).** Leaderdrive sells almost no product directly into the US — exposure is via tier-1 robot OEMs (KUKA, ABB) that ship robotic systems into the US. Direct tariff impact is small, but escalation could affect customer demand at the OEM level.
