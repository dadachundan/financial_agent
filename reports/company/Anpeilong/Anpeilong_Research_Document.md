## 1. COMPANY OVERVIEW

Shenzhen Ampron Technology Co., Ltd. — known in Chinese as 深圳安培龙科技股份有限公司 and listed on the ChiNext board of the Shenzhen Stock Exchange under ticker 301413 ("安培龙", pinyin "Ān Péi Lóng") — is a mid-cap Chinese sensor manufacturer that designs, produces and sells four families of sensing products: thermistors and temperature sensors, pressure sensors (ceramic capacitive, MEMS, and glass-microfusion), oxygen and nitrogen-oxygen sensors, and force/torque sensors. The company sits at the intersection of three secular Chinese industrial themes — vehicle electrification, home-appliance smartification, and humanoid robotics — and is the first batch of "national-level Little Giant" (专精特新"小巨人") enterprises designated by the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, a status reserved for specialised, high-tech SMEs identified as strategically important for industrial-chain self-sufficiency ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF)).

In FY2025 (calendar year ending 31 December 2025), Anpeilong generated revenue of **¥1,183.5 million (US$163M)**, up 25.9% year-over-year from ¥940.2 million in FY2024 and ¥746.6 million in FY2023, a three-year CAGR of approximately 25.9%. Net income attributable to shareholders was **¥90.7 million** (+9.8% YoY), giving a net margin of 7.7%. Basic EPS was ¥0.92. The company carries approximately 98.4 million shares outstanding, a market capitalisation that — based on recent ChiNext multiples for sensor peers — sits in the ¥10–15 billion range, which is roughly 100–150× FY2025 earnings, reflecting the market's pricing of the humanoid-robot force-sensor optionality rather than current cash flows. Cash dividend for FY2025 was set at ¥3 per 10 shares plus a 3-for-10 capital reserve transfer ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 9 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF); [Yahoo Finance — 301413.SZ](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/301413.SZ/)).

The business model is straightforward original-equipment-manufacturer (OEM) supply: Anpeilong develops and manufactures sensing components, sells them directly to downstream OEMs and Tier-1 suppliers (98.9% direct, 1.1% distribution), and earns a gross margin of approximately 29% on the consolidated business. By geography, 85.7% of revenue is domestic (¥1,014M) and 14.3% is export (¥169M); export gross margin (45.8%) is meaningfully higher than domestic (26.3%), reflecting the higher value-add and lower price competition of overseas customers. By product, pressure sensors became the largest revenue line in FY2025 at ¥674M (57.0% of revenue, +44.1% YoY), overtaking the legacy thermistor and temperature-sensor business at ¥489M (41.3% of revenue, +7.6% YoY). The ascendance of pressure sensors — driven by ceramic-capacitive units in electric-vehicle thermal-management systems and a 433% jump in MEMS-pressure revenue from a low base — is the single most important narrative in Anpeilong's recent operating history ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 40-41 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF)).

The company operates three principal domestic manufacturing bases: a Shenzhen Pingshan headquarters and "Smart Sensor Industrial Park" (assembly, R&D, MEMS and force-sensor pilot lines), a Dongguan facility (electronics), and a Chenzhou Hunan facility (the largest, run through wholly-owned subsidiary Chenzhou Anpeilong / 郴州安培龙) that handles high-volume thermistor and ceramic capacitive pressure-sensor output. International footprint is being built out aggressively in 2025–2026: a wholly-owned Belgian subsidiary (Ampron Technology Europe BV) anchors automotive-grade IC and MEMS chip design, a new German subsidiary (Ampron Sensor Solutions Europe GmbH, set up July 2025) handles European sales and technical support, a Thailand entity (AMPRON TECHNOLOGY (THAILAND), capitalised at THB 230 million) is building a fully-automated temperature-pressure combo sensor line for export to North American customers, and a Wuhan microelectronics subsidiary was incorporated in November 2025 to coordinate domestic IC design ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 39、43 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF)).

Headcount is not separately disclosed in the FY2025 report excerpts examined here, but R&D headcount expansion was specifically called out as the main driver of the 56.2% YoY increase in R&D expenses to ¥97.8 million (8.3% of revenue, up from 6.7% in FY2024). Sales and marketing expenses grew 35.5% to ¥32.5M as the company built out overseas sales infrastructure. G&A grew 19.3% to ¥107.0M. The combined operating-expense intensity rose roughly 200 basis points in FY2025 — the proximate cause of operating-margin compression from 9.9% in FY2024 to 7.7% in FY2025 — a deliberate "invest-through-the-cycle" choice that management frames as positioning for the 2026–2028 ramp of force sensors and overseas capacity ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 41、55 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF)).

Anpeilong sits in the "Sensitive Element & Sensor Manufacturing" sub-category (C3983) of China's official statistical classification of computer, communications and electronic-equipment manufacturing, and is classified under the encouraged-development list of the 2024 industrial-restructuring guidance catalogue. Strategically, the company is one of a handful of Chinese players executing on full vertical integration from ceramic powder, electrode metallisation, multilayer co-firing, thick-film printing and MEMS-pressure die design through to automotive-grade module assembly and validation, and the only Chinese pure-play sensor company that simultaneously covers low-, medium-, and high-pressure ranges for automotive applications. The company's positioning could be summarised as: a vertically integrated, automotive-grade Chinese sensor specialist, in the middle of pivoting from a NTC-thermistor heritage business into a pressure-sensor growth engine, while planting seeds in MEMS chips and force sensors for the humanoid-robot decade ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 17、36-38 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF)).

## 2. COMPANY HISTORY

Anpeilong's roots trace to 1999, when the predecessor entity Shenzhen Ampershine Technology (深圳市安培盛科技有限公司) was set up by founder Wu Ruojun (邬若军) and several co-investors to manufacture NTC thermistors for the consumer-electronics and home-appliance industries — at a time when domestic substitution of imported ceramic thermistor production was still in its infancy and the Chinese white-goods industry was beginning its decade-long export boom. In November 2004, the founder team incorporated the current entity, Shenzhen Ampron Technology Co., Ltd., in the Longgang district of Shenzhen, with Wu Ruojun assuming the chairman and general-manager role he has held continuously for more than 21 years ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 8 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF)).

The first decade of the corporate entity (2004–2014) was dominated by scaling the thermistor and basic temperature-sensor business and building manufacturing scale: the company moved its registered address four times between 2004 and 2017 across Pingdou and Longgang industrial zones as it added production capacity, and incorporated a Dongguan electronics subsidiary in November 2009 (Dongguan Anpeilong Electronics, 东莞安培龙) to specialise in electronics packaging. By the early 2010s, Anpeilong had become one of the larger domestic suppliers of NTC thermistors to the Chinese home-appliance industry — counting Midea, Gree, Haier, Hisense and TCL among its established customers — but was, by the founder's own framing in IPO disclosures, vulnerable to the structural margin pressure of a commoditising thermistor industry serving an increasingly saturated white-goods market ([安培龙 2023 年年度报告](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2024-04-22/1219702621.PDF)).

The strategic pivot that defines the modern Anpeilong was the entry into automotive-grade pressure sensors. Starting around 2014–2016, the company began developing ceramic-capacitive pressure sensors — using a multilayer co-fired ceramic technology platform adjacent to its thermistor production know-how — targeting at first the air-conditioning, engine, and gearbox systems of Chinese automotive OEMs. The decision was prescient: the Chinese new-energy-vehicle (NEV) boom from 2018 onward generated unprecedented demand for thermal-management pressure sensors (heat-pump systems on EVs require multiple pressure and combined pressure-temperature sensors per vehicle, versus essentially zero on ICE air-conditioning), and Anpeilong was one of the few Chinese suppliers with both the ceramic process know-how and the automotive-grade quality system to capture share from Japanese (TDK, Murata-related) and European (Bosch, Sensata-adjacent) incumbents ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 22-26 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF)).

Through 2017–2020 the company built out a portfolio of sensor product lines beyond thermistors: ceramic-capacitive pressure sensors moved into mass production; an oxygen-sensor R&D program was launched targeting the China-VI emissions standard and the upcoming European Euro 7 mandate (which under Regulation EU 2024/1257 requires dual front/rear NOx sensors on petrol passenger cars from late 2026); a MEMS pressure sensor program was begun targeting the gap between low-pressure capacitive and high-pressure microfused glass technologies; and the Chenzhou Anpeilong (郴州安培龙) Hunan subsidiary was incorporated and equipped as the company's largest production base. The years 2020–2021 also saw the establishment of Shenzhen Ampron Intelligence (深圳市安培龙智能, 2020) and a long-running collaboration with a Belgian sensor IC design team that eventually became wholly-owned subsidiary Ampron Technology Europe BV ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 35-37 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF); [Regulation (EU) 2024/1257 — Euro 7](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1257/oj)).

The IPO milestone came on **18 December 2023**, when Anpeilong listed on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange's ChiNext board with Huatai United Securities (华泰联合证券) as sponsor. The continuous supervision period runs through 31 December 2026. The IPO raised gross proceeds that funded a capacity-expansion of pressure-sensor production lines and the smart-sensor industrial park in Pingshan that became the company's new headquarters in November 2023. The first full year as a listed company (FY2024) delivered revenue of ¥940M (+25.9% YoY) and earned the company the Shenzhen Stock Exchange's top-grade "A" rating for disclosure quality in October 2025 — a meaningful credibility marker for a newly-public small-cap, and one of the underappreciated reasons for the company's subsequent inclusion in the Shenzhen-Hong Kong Stock Connect ("深股通") eligible list in June 2025 ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 8、39 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF); [安培龙 2024 年年度报告](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-25/1223277465.PDF)).

The 18 months between IPO and the FY2025 report mark a deliberate acceleration of strategic investment. In April 2025 the company committed ¥41.3 million of IPO over-raise to a dedicated production line for EMB (Electronic Mechanical Brake) force sensors targeting next-generation EV brake systems. In April–November 2025 the company set up six new subsidiaries: NEXA Sens Technology (HK) and Sensavior Technology (HK) in April; Anpeilong Smart Technology Chongqing in July; Anpeilong Sensor Technology Europe (a new German entity for sales/service) in July; Anpeilong Technology Thailand (the manufacturing base, capitalised at THB 230M) in September; and Ampron Microelectronics Wuhan (the domestic IC-design coordination centre) in November. In January 2026, the board approved a **¥544 million private placement** to specific investors, earmarked for pressure-sensor capacity expansion (the biggest line item), ceramic-capacitive pressure line upgrade, dedicated force-sensor production capacity (¥60.4M for an annual capacity of 500,000 force-sensor units targeting humanoid robots and EMB brakes), MEMS-chip R&D and industrialisation, and working capital. As of the FY2025 report, the placement was awaiting regulatory approval but had cleared board and shareholder-meeting votes ([关于使用部分超募资金投资建设新项目的公告 (2025-034)](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-25/1223277392.PDF); [安培龙 2026 年度向特定对象发行股票预案 (募集说明书)](http://file.finance.sina.com.cn/211.154.219.97:9494/MRGG/CNSESZ_STOCK/2026/2026-1/2026-01-08/11904776.PDF)).

A noteworthy 2025 milestone is the supply-chain "win" with a North American new-energy-vehicle customer disclosed in the FY2025 management discussion. Although the customer is not named, the description ("北美某知名新能源汽车客户" — "a well-known North American new-energy-vehicle customer") and the context of the customer concentration table (Customer 1 contributed ¥188.8M, or 15.95% of revenue) is consistent with widespread market speculation that the customer is Tesla, which Anpeilong is supplying with ceramic-capacitive pressure sensors for thermal-management and air-conditioning systems. The company has also disclosed batch supply to Stellantis, NIO (蔚来), Li Auto (理想), and XPeng (小鹏) in 2025, which together with BYD, SAIC Maxus (上汽大通), Great Wall (长城), Dongfeng, Chery, Wanliyang (gearboxes), Lear, Gentherm and Magna constitute the company's automotive customer roster ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 37、43 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF)).

## 3. MANAGEMENT TEAM

The Anpeilong board is composed of eight directors: four non-independent (chairman/founder Wu Ruojun, his spouse Li Li, board secretary Zhang Yanhong, and institutional director Zhou Xuanhong), three independent directors (Zeng Zixuan, Meng Chun, Jiang Honghua), and one employee-representative director (Yan Bingyue). ## 4. PRODUCTS & SERVICES

Anpeilong's product portfolio splits across four sensor families. By revenue mix in FY2025: pressure sensors 57.0%, thermistors and temperature sensors 41.3%, oxygen and other sensors 1.7%; force sensors are in pilot production but the related revenue, plus "other sensors", is captured in the residual line ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 40-41 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF)).

**Thermistors and Temperature Sensors (¥488.8M, 41.3% of revenue, +7.6% YoY, gross margin 29.4%).** This is the company's legacy business and the foundation of its ceramic process technology. The product breadth covers PTC (positive temperature coefficient) thermistors, NTC (negative temperature coefficient) thermistors, and surface-mount chip thermistors, with end-application designs for: home appliances (refrigerator and freezer temperature monitoring, air-conditioner indoor/outdoor units, washing-machine drum and inverter circuits, water-heater temperature control, microwave oven, dishwasher); coffee machines (Keurig/Green Mountain and Nespresso are direct customers); automotive (NEV traction-motor, engine water and oil temperature, ambient temperature, PTC heating module, on-board charger ports, gearbox oil temperature, EGR intake, evaporator, traction battery pack, heated seat, heated steering wheel — the automotive line alone generated ¥77.5M in FY2025, +24.5% YoY); medical (oxygen concentrators, body-temperature monitoring); industrial control (motor protection, surge current suppression); and photovoltaic-storage-charger applications (BMS temperature monitoring, charging-gun temperature). Volume scale is enormous — 521.8 million pieces sold in FY2025 — and unit prices average sub-¥1.0, making this a high-volume, ASP-modest business. The principal value to the consolidated business is the depth of ceramic process know-how (multilayer cofired sintering, electrode deposition, thick-film printing) it generates as a platform technology, with downstream leverage into pressure sensors and force sensors ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 20-22 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF)).

**Pressure Sensors (¥674.3M, 57.0% of revenue, +44.1% YoY, gross margin 29.3%).** The growth engine. Anpeilong is one of the few Chinese sensor manufacturers covering the full pressure range — low, medium and high — for automotive-grade applications, achieved through three parallel technology paths: (1) ceramic-capacitive pressure sensors (低压、中压), the largest sub-line at ¥616M in FY2025 (+34.8% YoY), used for HVAC pressure monitoring, battery-pack air pressure, fuel-cell systems, charging-gun pressure, EV thermal management, and combined pressure-temperature sensing for heat pumps; (2) MEMS pressure sensors (低压), based on silicon piezoresistive die diffused with Wheatstone bridges, generated ¥58.3M in FY2025 (+434% YoY off a tiny base) and are being designed into a broader set of automotive body, powertrain and appliance applications including those of Stellantis, SAIC Maxus and Keurig; (3) glass-microfusion pressure sensors (高压), still in sample and validation stages, target high-pressure automotive applications such as gasoline direct-injection fuel rails, common-rail diesel injection systems, automotive brake-system pressure sensors for ESP, EHB hydraulic brake systems, CO₂ heat-pump systems, and air-suspension systems. The unique strategic position is the combined pressure-temperature sensor, which integrates a ceramic capacitive die, ASIC conditioning chip, embedded temperature probe, FPC and housing into a single packaged device; this combo sensor is the principal product being supplied to the unnamed North American NEV customer and the principal product being made in the new Thailand plant. Pressure-sensor unit volumes grew 60.8% YoY in FY2025 to 23.0 million units ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 17、22-26、37-38 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF)).

**Oxygen and Nitrogen-Oxygen Sensors (¥20.4M, 1.7% of revenue, +14.7% YoY).** The smallest current-revenue product line but with the highest potential incremental dollar-content per vehicle in the 2026–2028 window because of the Euro 7 emissions standard. Anpeilong manufactures planar oxygen sensors using zirconia ceramic sensing dies, in three flavours: switch-type (narrow-band) oxygen sensors for OBD; wide-band (UEGO) oxygen sensors for closed-loop air-fuel ratio control; NOx sensors for diesel SCR after-treatment systems (China-VI and Euro 7); cabin oxygen sensors for sealed cockpit monitoring (an emerging aerospace application); and medical limit-current oxygen sensors for oxygen concentrators. Wide-band oxygen sensors for OEM use (主机厂宽域氧传感器) are in active R&D, targeting an import substitution opportunity historically dominated by Bosch and NGK. The product is unusual among Chinese sensor companies in that it has been developed in-house from the zirconia ceramic powder up — a meaningful technology moat in a category where the international leaders historically held a near-monopoly on automotive-grade chips ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 35 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF); [Regulation (EU) 2024/1257 — Euro 7](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1257/oj)).

**Force Sensors (revenue captured under "其他", currently <2% of total but with the steepest narrative).** The most strategically important product line for the long-term investment thesis. Anpeilong is developing force sensors along two parallel technology paths: (1) traditional metal-foil strain-gauge with mature low-cost industrialisation prospects; and (2) MEMS silicon strain-gauge combined with glass-microfusion process, which targets higher performance at smaller form factor. Product types under development and pilot production include single-axis (unidirectional) force sensors used for quadruped-robot foot-load sensing and general industrial / lawn-mowing / humanoid-robot load sensing; bending-moment sensors for lawn-mower / garden-robot ground sensing and assist-bicycle pedal-force sensing; torque sensors for humanoid-robot joint modules and collaborative robots; and six-axis force-torque sensors (六维力传感器, six-axis F/T) that simultaneously measure three force components (Fx, Fy, Fz) and three moment components (Mx, My, Mz) — the headline product for humanoid robots, surgical robotics, aerospace, and EMB automotive brake systems. The company has publicly disclosed: the metal-strain six-axis sensor has completed design, sample fabrication, performance testing and process validation, and is in "market positioning" stage; the MEMS-glass-microfusion six-axis sensor is in customer sampling. As of the FY2025 report, force-sensor revenue is "small batch / sample" only; management explicitly cautions that contribution is immaterial to FY2025 results. The 2026 private placement allocates ¥60.4M to a dedicated force-sensor production line with annual capacity of 500,000 units ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 35-36、39 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF); [关于使用部分超募资金投资建设新项目的公告 (2025-034)](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-25/1223277392.PDF)).

**Other Sensors.** A small residual line includes distance-sensing microwave sensors (used in security, traffic, smart-home and industrial automation) and speed/centrifugal magnetic sensors (used primarily in washing-machine drum speed and centrifugal monitoring). Combined revenue is low single-digit millions and these products serve mostly as additional offerings to existing home-appliance customers ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 22 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF)).

**Pricing models and deal sizes.** All four product families are sold on a direct OEM-supply basis at component-level pricing, with annual or multi-year supply contracts that include nominated price-down trajectories typical of automotive supply (low single-digit percentage per year). Average selling prices vary by category: thermistors at sub-¥1; ceramic-capacitive pressure sensors in the mid-¥20–40 range per unit; MEMS pressure sensors at slightly higher ASPs; oxygen sensors in the ¥40–100 range; and force sensors at much higher ASPs (six-axis F/T sensors are typically thousands of RMB per unit, even at scale). The product mix shift toward higher-ASP categories explains why pressure-sensor revenue grew 44% on a 61% unit-volume increase (a positive mix shift inside pressure), and why force-sensor monetisation can become economically meaningful at relatively low unit counts ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 41-42 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF)).

## 5. CUSTOMERS & GO-TO-MARKET

**Customer segmentation.** Anpeilong serves three principal end-markets that together account for the overwhelming majority of revenue: automotive (the strongest growth, now the dominant pressure-sensor customer pool), home appliances (the largest absolute thermistor customer pool), and a long tail of photovoltaic, energy storage, charging piles, industrial control and emerging humanoid-robotics customers. The company is dominantly an OEM-direct supplier — 98.9% of FY2025 revenue was direct sales, only 1.1% distribution — with sales-cycle lengths of 18–36 months for new automotive programs (from RFQ through SOP validation) and 6–12 months for home-appliance projects ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 38、41 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF)).

The customer-concentration profile is moderate: in FY2025, the top five customers contributed ¥356.5M, or 30.13% of revenue. Customer 1 alone contributed ¥188.8M (15.95%), Customer 2 ¥49.3M (4.17%), and Customers 3–5 between ¥35–43M each. None of the top-five customers are related parties. The 15.95% concentration with Customer 1 is the single largest individual-customer dependency in the business and the most-watched line in the customer-disclosure table; market consensus, based on the company's own description of "a well-known North American new-energy-vehicle customer" and the timing of revenue ramp, is that the customer is Tesla, supplied with ceramic-capacitive combined pressure-temperature sensors for thermal-management and air-conditioning systems. The official supplier list disclosed in management discussion includes BYD, the unnamed North American NEV customer, Stellantis, SAIC, Great Wall, Dongfeng, NIO, Li Auto, XPeng, Chery, Wanliyang, Lear, Gentherm and Magna in automotive; Midea, Gree, Haier, Hisense, TCL, Keurig, Nespresso and Toshiba in home appliances and small appliances ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 37、43 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF)).

**Automotive go-to-market.** The company describes its strategy as "from domestic independent brands to joint-venture brands to overseas brands" (从国产自主品牌到合资品牌再到海外品牌). The 2018–2022 phase consolidated supply share with Chinese domestic OEMs (BYD, Great Wall, Geely, SAIC, Dongfeng, Chery) and new-energy startups (NIO, XPeng, Li Auto, Leapmotor). The 2023–2025 phase added joint-venture and global brands: Stellantis was a major 2024–2025 ramp, and the North American NEV customer ramped in 2025. Going into 2026, the focus is on European OEMs (the Germany subsidiary is the primary vehicle for this push), Japan and Korea (Hyundai/Kia, Honda), and India. The dominant entry product is the ceramic-capacitive pressure or pressure-temperature combo sensor, with cross-sell into MEMS pressure, thermistors, force sensors and oxygen sensors once a customer relationship is established. Vehicle dollar content varies but for a typical new-energy passenger vehicle with heat-pump thermal management, Anpeilong content can reach ¥150–300 per vehicle across multiple sensor types — a multiple of typical ICE vehicle content and a key reason management is so leveraged to the NEV mix ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 37-38 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF)).

**Home-appliance go-to-market.** Established 20+ year direct-OEM relationships with all five major Chinese home-appliance groups: Midea, Gree, Haier, Hisense and TCL. The 2025 government "trade-in" stimulus (with-subsidy categories expanded from 8 to 12, including dishwashers, water purifiers, microwaves and rice cookers) boosted home-appliance demand and lifted thermistor revenue. Direct-sale dominant; competitive dynamics are dominated by price and quality consistency rather than novel R&D. Anpeilong is also expanding into "non-traditional appliances" such as smart lawn mowers, outdoor cleaning equipment and pool-water-treatment devices, where the customer base is exporters benefiting from China's contract-manufacturing infrastructure ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 17-18、22 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF); [国务院《关于 2025 年加力扩围实施大规模设备更新和消费品以旧换新政策的通知》](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/202501/content_6996953.htm)).

**International go-to-market.** The "overseas sales, service and technology localisation" strategy is being executed through three nodes. The Germany subsidiary (Ampron Sensor Solutions Europe GmbH, July 2025) is the European sales and technical-support hub focused on European automotive Tier-1s and OEMs and on emissions-related oxygen-sensor wins under Euro 7. The Belgian subsidiary (Ampron Technology Europe BV) is the chip-design centre. The Thailand subsidiary (Ampron Technology Thailand, September 2025) is a manufacturing base for combined pressure-temperature sensors, set up in part to mitigate tariff exposure on shipments to the North American NEV customer. Future planned subsidiaries include a US sales and technical-support entity. Export revenue gross margin (45.8%) is materially higher than domestic (26.3%), making international mix shift a structural margin tailwind ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 38、41 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF)).

**Sales and partnership ecosystem.** The Sino-American Hong Kong investment vehicles set up in April 2025 (NEXA Sens Technology HK, Sensavior Technology HK) provide treasury and import-export flexibility. A minority stake in Jiangsu Dinghui Embodied-Intelligence Robotics Innovation Center (江苏鼎汇具身智能机器人创新中心) connects Anpeilong to the China robotics ecosystem; the company also holds minority stakes in Wuxi Ruizhi Microelectronics (无锡瑞知微, IC partner) and Shenzhen Haina Microsensor Technology (深圳市海纳微). These minority investments serve as both technology-scouting and supply-chain-vertical-integration tools ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 39、43 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF)).

## 6. INDUSTRY OVERVIEW

The global sensor industry is the connective tissue between the physical and digital worlds — every smart device, vehicle, factory and home requires sensors to translate temperature, pressure, force, position, sound, light, gas and motion into electrical signals consumable by control systems. Per CCID Consulting (赛迪顾问) data cited in the FY2025 annual report, the **global sensor industry reached ¥1,485.5 billion in 2024**, up 6% YoY, with Asia-Pacific the fastest-growing region. The **Chinese sensor market reached ¥406.1 billion in 2024**, up 11% YoY (nearly twice the global growth rate), and is forecast by CCID to grow at a 15.0% three-year CAGR through 2026, which would imply a ¥620 billion market by 2026. China has gone from ¥218.9 billion in 2019 to ¥406.1 billion in 2024 — an 86% increase, or roughly 13% CAGR over five years ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 13-14 页 (引用赛迪顾问)](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF)).

By application end-market in China for 2024: consumer electronics ¥107.6 billion (26.5%), automotive electronics ¥85.9 billion (21.2%), industrial manufacturing ¥83.3 billion (20.5%), and network communications ¥77.0 billion (19.0%). Anpeilong's primary playing fields are automotive (the second-largest segment, growing at the fastest pace because of NEV penetration) and home appliances (a sub-segment of consumer electronics that overlaps with industrial cooling). By sensor type, pressure sensors lead the Chinese market with ¥71.4 billion of revenue in 2024 (17.6% of total) — precisely the category Anpeilong has bet on most heavily ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 14 页 (引用赛迪顾问)](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF)).

**Automotive sensor sub-sector.** The Chinese auto market in 2025 broke 3,400 million units of production and sales for the first time, with passenger cars at 3.01M units (+9.2% YoY) and new-energy vehicles at 1.65M units (+28.2% YoY, 47.9% of new-car sales). Exports reached 7.09M units in 2025, with NEV exports doubling to 2.61M units. CAAM forecasts 2026 total sales of 34.75M units (+1%), NEV sales of 19.0M units (+15.2%), and total exports of 7.40M units (+4.3%). Each NEV requires three to five times the number of sensors of an equivalent ICE vehicle — particularly in thermal management (heat-pump systems alone need 4–6 pressure/temperature sensors), battery management, and electric motor systems. The Euro 7 emissions standard becomes mandatory in November 2026 for new EU vehicle types and fully enforced from November 2027, requiring dual front/rear NOx sensors on petrol passenger vehicles — a regulatory tailwind specifically benefiting Anpeilong's oxygen/NOx sensor portfolio ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 15-16 页 (引用中汽协)](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF); [Regulation (EU) 2024/1257 — Euro 7](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1257/oj)).

**Home appliance sub-sector.** Chinese home-appliance retail sales hit a record ¥1,169.5 billion in 2025, supported by trade-in subsidies that drove sales of more than 129 million subsidy-eligible units. Industry trends — premiumisation, integration, smartification — favour higher-sensor-content products and continue to provide steady (though not explosive) demand for Anpeilong's thermistor and temperature-sensor portfolio ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 17 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF); [国务院《关于 2025 年加力扩围实施大规模设备更新和消费品以旧换新政策的通知》](https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/202501/content_6996953.htm)).

**Force sensor sub-sector and humanoid robotics.** The most exciting frontier. Per industry forecasts cited in third-party Chinese media coverage of Anpeilong, the global humanoid-robot market is projected at ¥6.3 billion in 2025, ¥64 billion by 2030, and over ¥400 billion by 2035. Each humanoid robot of the current Optimus, Figure, Xpeng Iron etc. generation uses one to two six-axis force-torque sensors at the wrist and ankle plus several single-axis force sensors at finger and joint levels, with total per-robot force-sensor dollar content estimated at ¥3,000–10,000 — making this both a high-ASP and high-volume opportunity if humanoid mass-production materialises. CCID and other Chinese research houses project Chinese force-sensor revenue to compound at 30–40% CAGR through 2030 from a small 2024 base ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 18-19 页 (引用 GGII、QY Research)](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF); [Sohu — 安培龙人形机器人传感器布局, 2024-12](https://www.sohu.com/a/846714420_122014422)).

**Industry structure.** The Chinese sensor industry is structurally fragmented and import-dependent: imports still account for an estimated 60% of the high-end sensor demand in China by value as of 2024, with international leaders Bosch, Honeywell, Sensata, TE Connectivity, TDK/EPCOS, Murata, NGK, Continental, Allegro, Infineon and Texas Instruments dominating most high-end automotive, industrial and IoT categories. Domestic players generally compete on either commodity volume (Goertek, AAC for consumer electronics) or specialist niches (Anpeilong in ceramic pressure / thermistor / oxygen; Wonder/沃德尔 in MEMS pressure; Huagong/华工 in thermistor; SAIC-Magna affiliated suppliers in chassis sensors). The Chinese government's "Made in China 2025" and "specialised, refined, differentiated, innovative" (专精特新) programmes have specifically prioritised sensors as a key industrial-substitution category, and Anpeilong's status as a first-batch "national-level Little Giant" reflects this designation. Regulatory tailwinds also come from MIIT's electronics-manufacturing 2025-2026 growth action plan, the Humanoid Robot Innovation Development Guidance, the connected-vehicle standard system, and the manufacturing-reliability-improvement plan ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 13-19 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF); [工信部《电子信息制造业 2025-2026 年稳增长行动方案》](https://www.miit.gov.cn/zwgk/zcwj/wjfb/tg/art/2025/art_8de2169f6f694a1995e07e8f3c5a8b4e.html); [OFweek 传感器 — 安培龙行业报道, 2026-01](https://sensor.ofweek.com/2026-01/ART-81013-8120-30678660.html)).

## 7. COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE

Anpeilong operates across multiple sensor sub-categories, each with a different competitive structure. Below is a synthesis of the principal competitor sets, organised by product line ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 36-38 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF)).

**1. Pressure sensors (the largest revenue line).** Direct international competitors include **Sensata Technologies** (US, the global leader in automotive pressure sensors with broad ceramic and silicon portfolios), **TE Connectivity** (US, strong in automotive pressure and force), **Bosch / Robert Bosch GmbH** (Germany, vertically integrated, dominant in fuel-rail and brake pressure sensors), **Continental** (Germany), **Denso** (Japan), **Infineon** and **STMicroelectronics** (semiconductor MEMS pressure dies), **Honeywell** (industrial pressure), **Amphenol Thermometrics** (combined pressure-temperature). Chinese direct competitors include **Wonder Technology / Zhejiang Wonder (浙江沃德尔, ChiNext-listed)**, which is the closest peer by product mix (MEMS pressure for automotive) and where Anpeilong's VP Qin Yu spent seven years; **Saiyi Information (赛意信息)** in industrial pressure; and a long tail of smaller manufacturers. Anpeilong's stated competitive advantages are its full-range coverage (low/mid/high), in-house ceramic-capacitive process know-how, vertically integrated MEMS die development (with the Belgian IC team), and automotive-grade quality systems already validated by Tesla-type customers. Disadvantages are sub-scale relative to Sensata/TE/Bosch and continued external sourcing of MEMS pressure dies and conditioning ICs from third-party chip houses (an explicit reason for the 2026 private-placement MEMS-chip funding) ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 36-38 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF); [Sensata 2024 10-K, FY2024 net revenue](https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0001477294&type=10-K)).

**2. Thermistors and temperature sensors.** Direct international competitors include **TDK (incorporating the former EPCOS thermistor lines)**, **Murata Manufacturing**, **Vishay Dale**, **AVX**, **Kemet**, and **Shibaura Electronics** (Japan, the global leader in glass-encapsulated thermistors). Chinese competitors include **Huagong Hi-Tech (华工高理 / HG Gaoli)**, one of the world's largest production bases for NTC thermistors and PTC heaters, supplying Samsung, LG and BYD; **Nanjing Shiheng Electronics**; **Thinking Electronic Industrial (兴勤电子, Taiwan-listed)**; **Shenzhen Ampleon-related domestic suppliers**; and a long tail of smaller ceramic-thermistor producers. Anpeilong is a top-three Chinese pure-play thermistor manufacturer by revenue, with particular share strength in white-goods OEM supply. The category is highly commoditised and price-competitive; Anpeilong's ¥489M FY2025 thermistor revenue grew only 7.6% — reflecting the maturity of the segment ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 20-22、41 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF); [VanCera — Top Temperature Sensor Manufacturers in China](https://www.van-cera.com/top-temperatures-sensor-manufacturers-and-suppliers-in-china/)).

**3. Oxygen and NOx sensors.** International competitors are dominated by **Bosch** (the global market leader in OEM oxygen sensors), **NGK Spark Plug** (Japan, dominant in aftermarket and OEM oxygen sensors), **Denso**, **Continental**, **Delphi/BorgWarner (Sevcon Sevcon)**, and **Hitachi**. Chinese competitors include **Liaoning Hongtu (辽宁宏图)**, **WeiFu High Technology Group (威孚高科, SH-listed)**, and **Suzhou Tongdamao (苏州童达茂)**. Anpeilong's competitive edge is its zirconia-ceramic in-house process and the wide-band UEGO sensor R&D programme; the disadvantage is that international incumbents have decades of OEM-validation history. The Euro 7 regulation (mandatory dual NOx sensors on petrol cars from November 2026) is the principal opportunity. Volumes are still small for Anpeilong (¥20.4M total) but the regulatory event represents a meaningful step-change call option ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 35 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF); [Regulation (EU) 2024/1257 — Euro 7](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1257/oj)).

**4. Force sensors (the most strategically watched).** International competitors include **ATI Industrial Automation** (US, the dominant global supplier of six-axis F/T sensors for industrial robots), **Schunk** (Germany), **Bota Systems** (Switzerland), **Robotous** (Korea), **OnRobot** (Denmark), and **Honeywell-FUTEK**. Chinese competitors are themselves at an early stage: **Kunwei Technology (坤维科技)**, **Songling Robotics (松灵机器人)** for limited integration, **Bingchuan Sensor (冰川传感)**, **Yuli Instrument (宇立仪器)**, **Beipei Sensor (北蓓传感)**, and listed players such as **Hanwei Electronics (汉威科技)**, **Donghua Testing (东华测试, currently the most established Chinese F/T sensor provider)** and **Keli Sensing (柯力传感)**. Anpeilong is a late entrant relative to Donghua Testing or ATI, but the company is uniquely positioned by virtue of its dual technology paths (metal strain-gauge + MEMS-glass-microfusion) and the leverage from its ceramic process IP. Six-axis sensors are still in sample-customer validation as of the FY2025 disclosure ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 35-36 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF); [Caiwen News — 安培龙人形机器人 / 力传感器进展](https://www.caiwennews.com/article/1399653.shtml)).

**5. MEMS pressure dies (an upstream battleground).** This is where Anpeilong is currently weakest — most MEMS pressure dies and signal-conditioning chips are still externally sourced from international fabless designers and Taiwanese/Chinese fabs. International leaders include **Bosch Sensortec**, **Infineon**, **STMicroelectronics**, **TE Connectivity (MEAS)** and **NXP**. The Belgian subsidiary and the Wuhan microelectronics subsidiary are explicitly chartered to attack this gap. The 2026 private placement allocates capital to MEMS-chip industrialisation. This is the highest-risk, highest-potential portion of the strategy: a successful in-house MEMS chip programme would meaningfully improve product gross margin (potentially adding 300–500 basis points over time) and supply-chain resilience ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 36-37 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF); [安培龙 2026 年度向特定对象发行股票预案](http://file.finance.sina.com.cn/211.154.219.97:9494/MRGG/CNSESZ_STOCK/2026/2026-1/2026-01-08/11904776.PDF)).

**Overall competitive assessment.** Anpeilong is a mid-tier, ascending Chinese specialist. It is not a global top-five player in any single category and is structurally smaller than the international incumbents (Sensata revenue is roughly 30× Anpeilong's; Bosch's sensor revenue is multiples larger still). However, in the specific niche of ceramic-capacitive automotive pressure sensors for Chinese and global NEV OEMs, Anpeilong has become one of the two or three credible Chinese pure-plays, with proven design-in at Tesla-class, Stellantis-class and major Chinese OEM customers. The competitive moats are: (1) ceramic-process IP and manufacturing know-how built over 25 years, (2) demonstrated ability to navigate the multi-year automotive supplier-validation process, (3) full-range product coverage (low/mid/high pressure plus combined pressure-temperature) that single-product Chinese competitors lack, (4) deepening overseas footprint (Germany, Thailand, Belgium), and (5) explicit strategic capital commitment to upstream IC integration. The principal competitive vulnerabilities are sub-scale R&D vs Western incumbents, ongoing reliance on third-party MEMS chips, single-large-customer concentration (Customer 1 at 15.95%), and the unproven nature of the force-sensor commercial business ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 36-38、43 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF)).

## 8. MARKET OPPORTUNITY

Anpeilong's served addressable market is the sum of four overlapping wedges, sized below using FY2024 data and the company's own product positioning ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 13-19 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF)).

**TAM 1: Chinese pressure-sensor market.** CCID puts the 2024 Chinese pressure-sensor revenue at ¥71.4 billion. Assuming a 12–15% CAGR over 2024–2028 (lower than the industry-wide 15% because pressure is more mature than emerging categories like force), the 2028 Chinese pressure-sensor TAM is ¥110–125 billion. Anpeilong's FY2025 pressure-sensor revenue of ¥674M represents a Chinese market share of approximately 0.9% — modest, but ramping fast (the company's pressure-sensor revenue growth at 44% YoY is dramatically above industry growth at 11–12%, implying meaningful share gain). Capturing 2.5–3% share in 2028 — plausible given the customer wins disclosed — would imply ¥2.8–3.5 billion of pressure-sensor revenue, or roughly 4–5× the FY2025 level ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 14、40-41 页 (引用赛迪顾问)](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF)).

**TAM 2: Chinese thermistor and temperature-sensor market.** The temperature-sensor sub-segment of the Chinese ¥406B total is estimated by CCID at ¥45–55 billion in 2024. The category is mature and growing in the 7–10% range. Anpeilong's ¥489M FY2025 thermistor revenue equates to roughly 1.0–1.1% market share. Realistic 2028 revenue is ¥600–700M, implying low-double-digit growth and modest share consolidation through scale ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 14、40-41 页 (引用赛迪顾问)](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF)).

**TAM 3: Chinese oxygen / NOx sensor market.** A sub-¥10 billion sub-segment historically dominated by Bosch and NGK. Euro 7 and China VIb create a meaningful step-change in 2026–2028. Anpeilong's revenue ramp here is highly leveraged: a successful OEM wide-band oxygen sensor win could 5–10× the current ¥20M base. A reasonable 2028 case is ¥100–200M ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 35 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF); [Regulation (EU) 2024/1257 — Euro 7](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1257/oj)).

**TAM 4: Chinese force-sensor market.** Per third-party Chinese forecasts, the Chinese force-sensor market is in the ¥3–5 billion range in 2024, growing at 30–40% CAGR through 2030. By 2028 the Chinese force-sensor TAM is plausibly ¥10–15 billion. The wildcard is humanoid robotics: if Tesla Optimus, Xpeng Iron, Unitree and Chinese humanoid OEMs collectively reach the 100,000-unit annual production assumed in base-case 2028 industry forecasts, the per-robot force-sensor content of ¥3,000–8,000 implies ¥0.3–0.8 billion of humanoid force-sensor demand by 2028 — small absolute, but doubling to tripling every year. Anpeilong's stated capacity-addition of 500,000 force-sensor units in the 2026 private placement, at an average ASP of ¥1,500–2,500 per unit, implies maximum nameplate revenue of ¥0.75–1.25 billion when fully utilised in 2027–2028. The economic value of a successful force-sensor ramp is substantial ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 18-19 页 (引用 GGII、QY Research)](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF); [关于使用部分超募资金投资建设新项目的公告 (2025-034)](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-25/1223277392.PDF)).

**Total combined 2028 SAM and revenue scenario.** Aggregating: pressure ¥2.8–3.5B + thermistor ¥0.6–0.7B + oxygen ¥0.1–0.2B + force ¥0.5–1.0B + other small lines ¥0.1B → range ¥4.1–5.5 billion of 2028 revenue under a base case, or roughly 3.5–4.6× the FY2025 level. This implies a 2025–2028 revenue CAGR of 37–47%, more aggressive than the FY2024 and FY2025 actuals but consistent with the four-pillar capacity-investment programme and customer pipeline (built on [安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 13-19、40-41 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF) + [安培龙 2026 年度向特定对象发行股票预案](http://file.finance.sina.com.cn/211.154.219.97:9494/MRGG/CNSESZ_STOCK/2026/2026-1/2026-01-08/11904776.PDF)).

**Penetration strategy.** Anpeilong's penetration plan is articulated explicitly in the FY2025 report: continue to deepen automotive customer share by adding sensor types per vehicle (cross-selling MEMS pressure, force, thermistor and oxygen sensors into accounts initially won with ceramic-capacitive); aggressively localise overseas service through Germany, Thailand and planned US subsidiaries to win European Tier-1 and OEM contracts; vertically integrate MEMS-chip design to capture margin currently paid to external chip houses; and seed the humanoid-robot force-sensor market early to establish design-in relationships before competitors industrialise ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 37-39 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF)).

## 9. RISK ASSESSMENT

### Company-Specific Risks (6)

**1. Customer concentration with a single very large customer (Customer 1, 15.95% of FY2025 revenue, ¥188.8M).** The single largest revenue source is described as a major North American new-energy-vehicle customer, widely speculated to be Tesla. Loss of this customer — through price renegotiation, sourcing diversification, or production-volume cuts — would damage revenue and operating leverage. Mitigants: the company is supplying multiple sensor types and has won similar programmes at Stellantis, NIO, Li Auto and BYD; the Thailand plant reduces tariff vulnerability for this customer ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 37、43 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF)).

**2. Operating margin compression risk.** Operating margin has compressed from 12.2% in FY2023 to 9.9% in FY2024 to 7.7% in FY2025, driven by R&D and SG&A growth outpacing revenue, plus a ~3 percentage point decline in gross margin (32% to 29%). FY2026 is expected to remain pressured as the company continues to invest in MEMS chips, the Thailand plant, the Germany subsidiary, and force-sensor industrialisation. A failure to convert these investments into revenue within 2–3 years would prolong margin compression and pressure EPS growth despite revenue growth ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 41、55 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF)).

**3. Force-sensor commercialisation risk.** Force sensors are the highest-conviction strategic bet but are currently revenue-immaterial. Both technology paths (metal-strain and MEMS-glass-microfusion) are at sample or small-batch stages. Customer adoption — particularly the ramp of humanoid robotics — is uncertain and depends on factors outside Anpeilong's control. The ¥60.4M force-sensor capacity investment could yield low utilisation if humanoid mass production is delayed ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 35-36、55 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF); [关于使用部分超募资金投资建设新项目的公告 (2025-034)](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-25/1223277392.PDF)).

**4. Founder/CEO concentration of authority and key-person risk.** Wu Ruojun simultaneously serves as chairman, GM, and (since February 2025) GM of the force-sensor business unit. He holds 31.67% directly plus 5.64% through Ruihang Investment Partnership; he is also building a related-party sensor business empire (Wuxi Hairong, Jingrong Tech) outside the listed entity. Any health or governance event affecting Wu would materially affect strategic continuity. The board has three independent directors but governance is unusually founder-centric ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 65-67、70 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF)).

**5. MEMS-chip vertical-integration execution risk.** The 2026 private placement allocates substantial capital to MEMS pressure-chip R&D and industrialisation, with the Belgian and Wuhan subsidiaries as execution vehicles. Designing automotive-grade pressure dies and conditioning ICs at the quality bar of Bosch, Infineon and ST is a multi-year, multi-tape-out R&D effort with no guarantee of success. Failure to deliver in-house chips by 2027–2028 would maintain current external-IC sourcing dependency and the associated cost and supply-chain risks ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 36-37 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF); [安培龙 2026 年度向特定对象发行股票预案](http://file.finance.sina.com.cn/211.154.219.97:9494/MRGG/CNSESZ_STOCK/2026/2026-1/2026-01-08/11904776.PDF)).

**6. Pre-IPO restricted-share unlock overhang in December 2026.** Founder shares (31.16M, 31.67% of capital) and Ruihang Investment Partnership shares (5.55M, 5.64%) come off lock-up on 19 December 2026. While intent to sell is unconfirmed, the supply overhang is meaningful for a relatively thinly-traded ChiNext stock ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 70、113-115 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF); [关于首次公开发行战略配售股份上市流通的提示性公告 (2025-092)](https://www.cninfo.com.cn/new/disclosure/stock?stockCode=301413)).

### Industry / Market Risks (4)

**7. Automotive cycle and NEV policy risk.** The Chinese auto and NEV markets are heavily policy-dependent (trade-in subsidies, purchase-tax exemptions, dual-credit rules). A roll-back of subsidies, slowdown in NEV unit growth, or a step-down in NEV penetration would dampen the principal demand vector for Anpeilong's pressure-sensor business. CAAM's own 2026 forecast for total auto sales is only +1% growth, vs +9.4% in 2025 ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 15-16 页 (引用中汽协)](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF)).

**8. International trade and tariff escalation.** US-China trade tensions, EU CBAM, and EU/US tariff changes could affect both Anpeilong's direct exports (14.3% of revenue) and the cost competitiveness of its Chinese customers selling into those markets. The Thailand plant partially mitigates US tariff exposure for the North American NEV customer, but does not address EU tariff or sanction risk ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 38、55 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF)).

**9. Intensifying domestic and international competition.** The Chinese sensor industry is well-funded and increasingly crowded. Domestic competitors (Wonder, Huagong, Donghua Testing, Hanwei) are all expanding capacity; international leaders (Sensata, Bosch) are localising production in China; and some emerging force-sensor specialists (Kunwei, Donghua) have head-starts. Price erosion in core ceramic-capacitive pressure is a realistic 2027–2028 scenario ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 36-37、55 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF)).

**10. Technology disruption risk.** A shift in dominant pressure-sensor technology from ceramic capacitive to all-silicon MEMS, or alternative sensing modalities (optical, magnetic), could erode Anpeilong's ceramic-process moat. The MEMS pressure business is partially defensive against this shift but is itself dependent on external chip supply ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 36 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF)).

### Financial Risks (2)

**11. Cash flow weakening and capex intensity.** Operating cash flow declined from ¥95.7M (FY2023) to ¥90.2M (FY2024) to ¥70.3M (FY2025) — a deterioration despite revenue growth, reflecting working-capital tied up in inventory and receivables. Capex was ¥225M, ¥92M and ¥113M in FY2023–FY2025; free cash flow is now negative at –¥43M in FY2025. Combined with the ¥544M private placement, the balance sheet is being levered for growth, and net debt has risen from –¥359M (net cash) in FY2023 to –¥3M (essentially zero) in FY2024 to +¥276M in FY2025. The placement, if completed, will replenish equity, but execution depends on regulatory approval and market conditions ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 9 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF); [安培龙 2024 年年度报告](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2025-04-25/1223277465.PDF); [安培龙 2026 年度向特定对象发行股票预案](http://file.finance.sina.com.cn/211.154.219.97:9494/MRGG/CNSESZ_STOCK/2026/2026-1/2026-01-08/11904776.PDF)).

**12. Inventory and receivables build risk.** Inventory of pressure sensors grew 81% YoY in FY2025 — much faster than revenue. While management attributes the build to ceramic-capacitive ramp, an extended customer destock cycle would force inventory write-downs and gross-margin pressure ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 41-42 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF)).

### Macroeconomic Risks (3)

**13. FX risk.** Export revenue is denominated mostly in USD. A weaker USD or sharp RMB appreciation would compress reported revenue and gross margin from the export channel (which has structurally higher margin than domestic). Two-way RMB volatility under continued US-China tension introduces P&L volatility ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 41、55 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF)).

**14. Chinese capital-market sentiment and ChiNext liquidity.** Anpeilong is a ChiNext small-cap (¥10–15B market cap) reliant on retail and institutional flows; a broader Chinese equity de-rating, particularly of high-multiple AI/robotics-narrative names, would compress the equity-raise valuation and the cost of strategic capital ([Yahoo Finance — 301413.SZ](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/301413.SZ/); [Eastmoney — 安培龙 301413](https://quote.eastmoney.com/sz301413.html)).

**15. Interest-rate and credit conditions.** Total debt has risen from ¥359M (FY2024) to ¥445M (FY2025). PBOC rate moves and Chinese banking-sector credit conditions affect financing costs. Most debt is short-term working capital and not large in absolute terms, but rising rates would marginally pressure net interest expense ([安培龙 2025 年年度报告, 第 9 页](https://static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/2026-04-24/1225161084.PDF)).
