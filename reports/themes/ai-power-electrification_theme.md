# AI Power & Data-Center Electrification

**Created:** 2026-05-31 · **Last refreshed:** 2026-05-31 · **Last mutated:** 2026-05-31 · **Refresh cadence:** monthly · **Languages tracked:** en

## Thesis

Hyperscale AI compute is rewriting the next decade of electric-power capex. The four US hyperscalers (Microsoft, Amazon, Alphabet, Meta) are guiding to ~$700 bn of 2026 capex with data-center power infrastructure as the binding constraint; the marginal MW of dedicated AI capacity is now procured years ahead of build through behind-the-meter PPAs, on-site CCGT, restarted nuclear units, and grid-side transformer / switchgear orders that have pushed industry lead times into 2027–2030. GE Vernova's gas-turbine backlog crossed 100 GW in Q1 2026 with ~20 GW explicitly tied to data-center load against ~10 GW/yr capacity through 2030 — a decade of output on the books, with a 110 GW year-end target on top ([mgrid.org, 2026-04-22](https://mgrid.org/2026/04/22/ge-vernovas-gas-turbine-backlog-hits-100-gw-as-data-centers-drive-4-billion-in-q1-orders/)). Vertiv's Q1 2026 backlog hit $15.0 bn (+109% YoY) on TTM organic orders +81%, and Eaton's Electrical Americas data-center *orders* ran +240% YoY in Q1 2026 with revenue +50% — the cleanest read-through that AI compute is pulling power-equipment demand forward into the printed P&L ([Vertiv Q1 2026 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001674101/000162828026026379/q12026exhibit991vrt04222026.htm); [Eaton Q1 2026 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001551182/000155118226000010/etn03312026exhibit99.htm)).

The basket bets that the *equipment* and *generation* layers — not the data-center REITs and not the AI chips themselves — capture the cleanest incremental margin because they have the most binding capacity constraints. Morgan Stanley's "Energy Meets Compute" deck flagged this as a super-cycle restart, and the GS / JPM / UBS / Deutsche cohort converged on the same framing in a 3-day window 2026-05-29 to 2026-05-31 across a dozen+ broker notes ([MS zsxq 184152244582842, 2026-05-29](http://localhost:5001/zsxq-pdf/184152244582842); [SST AIDC zsxq 212485811815581, 2026-05-31](http://localhost:5001/zsxq-pdf/212485811815581)). Chinese broker notes layer the onshore industrial-policy angle: solid-state transformers (SSTs) are rotating "silicon-in, copper-out" of the AIDC supply chain, and the State-Grid 15th-Five-Year capex framework at RMB 4 trn (+40% YoY) is explicitly tagged to AIDC load growth ([State-Grid 4-trn tracker](https://600312.fupanwang.com/dsk/2026-01-19.html)).

The vulnerability is exactly the multiple this thesis now embeds. Vertiv, GEV, NXT, Doosan, ETN, MPWR and the Chinese 杰瑞 / 麦格米特 / 思源 cohort all run at +100% to +300% 1Y returns at inception — pricing in execution that hasn't fully printed (see Performance table below; yfinance pull 2026-05-31). A 12-month window where hyperscaler capex cadence even *pauses* (rate-driven, regulatory, or chip-supply-driven) is the cleanest single de-rating path; a deeper structural break would require AI training demand itself to disappoint, with macro stress from oil + rates the most-watched second-order driver ([UBS 全球天然气 zsxq 585412242541124](http://localhost:5001/zsxq-pdf/585412242541124); [JPM 原油速评 zsxq 812485814111422](http://localhost:5001/zsxq-pdf/812485814111422)). Drift watch separates the printed-P&L names (Vertiv, Eaton, GEV, MPWR, Talen) from the still-anticipatory names (NXT-Prevalon, Doosan US wins, 麦格米特's NVIDIA ramp, 杰瑞's offshore turbine pipeline).

## Scope rules

**In:** (a) power generation directly anchored to AI-DC load — gas turbines, nuclear restarts / uprates, behind-the-meter CCGTs, IPPs with disclosed hyperscaler PPAs; (b) grid-side hardware whose lead times have re-rated on AIDC demand — transformers, HVDC converter valves, GIS / switchgear, SSTs; (c) data-center power equipment one rack-row outside the chip — UPS, PDU, BBU, server-side AC/DC and 800-VDC, and *thermally adjacent* power-thermal products. All tickers need a 2025–2026 primary source naming the company as an AI-DC supply participant.

**Out:** (a) pure-renewable IPPs without a disclosed hyperscaler PPA; (b) oil-and-gas E&P unless explicitly AI-DC anchored (Tallgrass's gas-pipeline → on-site-CCGT model is a future addition if listed); (c) auto-electrification suppliers; (d) DC REITs (EQIX, DLR) — *demand*, not supply; (e) baseload utilities and Chinese state hydro / nuclear (长江电力, 中国核电) where the AI-DC PPA is still aspirational.
