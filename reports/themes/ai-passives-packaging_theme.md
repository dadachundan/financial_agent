## Thesis

**Anchor — a pricing/content anchor, not a dollar TAM (no third-party publishes a unified "AI passives" pool; the two legs below are the falsifiable trajectories).** Leg 1 — **MLCC (Murata's own guidance via MS, 2026-05-27):** AI/DC-related sales rise from **10-15% of MLCC sales in F3/26 to 20-25% in F3/27**, with AI/DC MLCC sales **+85-90% YoY**; an **additional ¥80bn capex** on top of the standing 10%/yr capacity adds; total MLCC sales growth accelerating **from 13% YoY in F3/27 to 20-25% YoY in F3/28** ([MS — Murata Cutting-edge MLCC, zsxq #585412224511444 p.1](http://xs-macbook-air.local:5001/zsxq/pdf/585412224511444/Morgan%20Stanley-Murata%20Manufacturing%20%EF%BC%886981.T%EF%BC%89Investment%20in%20Cutting~edge%20MLCC%20Products%20is%20Highly%20Efficient-260527.pdf#page=1)). Leg 2 — **CCL pricing (Citi, 2026-06-03):** China CCL average price **+76% YoY in 2026E to ~Rmb220/sheet from ~Rmb113/sheet in 2025**, with *"another approx. 30% further increase in CCL spot price during 2H26 to Rmb240/sheet by end-2026 from currently Rmb180-190/sheet"* ([Citi — Kingboard, zsxq #415288428282288 p.1](http://xs-macbook-air.local:5001/zsxq/pdf/415288428282288/CITI-Kingboard%20Laminates%20Holdings%20%EF%BC%881888.HK%EF%BC%89%20Raising%20TP%20to%20HK%2476%20for%20Higher~than~expected%20ASP%20Hike%20for%20June-260603.pdf#page=1)). **Swing factor: the CCL/e-glass pricing leg** — it is the input cost of every AI board, it has revised upward twice since May, and a fourth 2026 hike (or its absence) moves the China half of the basket most; `core` names Shengyi / Kingboard / WUS / Shennan ride it directly.

![AI passives anchor — Murata AI/DC MLCC trajectory and China CCL price path](../charts/theme_ai-passives-packaging_anchor.png)

**Sub-buckets and the value-chain dollar map.** The basket spans four physical layers of the AI rack: (a) **MLCC** — Murata, TDK, Taiyo Yuden (Japan trio) + Yageo, Walsin (Taiwan) + Sanhuan, Fenghua (A-share substitutes); (b) **ABF substrate / FC-BGA** — Ibiden + Unimicron / Nan Ya PCB / Kinsus (Taiwan trio) + AT&S, with Ajinomoto the sole upstream resin supplier; (c) **high-speed CCL → PCB** — Shengyi, Kingboard (laminate) → WUS, Shennan, Avary (boards); (d) **next-gen substrate / photomask** — Corning (glass core), Photronics (masks). Dollar context from Bernstein's Vera Rubin teardown: a VR NVL72 rack costs **~$9.1M** — **GPU ex-HBM ~$4M (the largest item), memory/storage ~$3.2M, networking ~$1.2M, cooling and power ~$150k each** — and all-in AI datacenter capex runs **~$47B per GW** ([Bernstein — AI Value Chain GW cost, zsxq #814528815844812 p.1/p.3](http://xs-macbook-air.local:5001/zsxq/pdf/814528815844812/Bernstein-AI%20Value%20Chain%EF%BC%9AHow%20much%20does%20a%20GW%20of%20Vera%20Rubin%20data%20center%20capacity%20actually%20cost%EF%BC%9F-260608.pdf#page=1)). Passives and substrates are small slices of that bill — which is precisely why despeccing them is not worth a customer's time and pricing power persists ("non-substitutable small-dollar BOM").

**The shortage claim is now multi-broker.** Bernstein's *"ABF Substrate: Renaissance of CPU drives ABF shortage"* (Ibiden PT ¥23,900, [zsxq #184152242111442 p.1](http://xs-macbook-air.local:5001/zsxq/pdf/184152242111442/Bernstein-ABF%20Substrate%EF%BC%9A%20Renaissance%20of%20CPU%20drives%20ABF%20shortage%EF%BC%9B%20Lifting%20Ibiden%20PT%20to%20%EF%BF%A523%EF%BC%8C900-260529.pdf#page=1)); BofA *"stay constructive on favorable supply/demand ahead; Raise PO on Unimicron/NYPCB/Kinsus"* ([zsxq #212451458225151 p.1](http://xs-macbook-air.local:5001/zsxq/pdf/212451458225151/BofA%20Securities-Technology%20Hardware%20~%20Taiwan%20ABF%20substrate%EF%BC%9A%20stay%20constructive%20on%20favorable%20supply%20demand%20ahead-260525.pdf#page=1)); and at Citi's Taiwan Tech Conference (06-07) all three CCL/foil names (Elite Material, TUC, Gold Circuit) said *"capacity expansion pace could not catch up with end-customers' demand"*, with new CCL capacity ramping slower than PCB capacity → continued *"CCL shortage and potential price hikes"* ([Citi — Taiwan PCB & Laminates, zsxq #814528251122222 p.1](http://xs-macbook-air.local:5001/zsxq/pdf/814528251122222/CITI-Taiwan%20PCB%20%26%20Laminates%EF%BC%9AWhat%E2%80%99s%20New%20at%20Citi%20Taiwan%20Tech%20Conference%202026%20%E2%80%93%20strong%20demand%20and%20shortage-260607.pdf#page=1)). A full two-sided S/D volume balance is **not yet published** by any mined source (capacity adds are disclosed per-company, demand only in revenue terms) — flagged in Data Used as a coverage gap rather than fabricated here.

**Staging — who benefits when.** The MLCC / CCL price-takers monetize **now**: the April-2026 MLCC hikes and the June e-glass hike land in FY27Q1/2Q26 prints (Jul-Aug 2026 — the dated gate). The ABF substrate names monetize the **capacity ramp**: Unimicron's +40% YoY ABF capacity by end-2026 with gross margin modeled *"to recover from high-teens in 1Q26 to ~30% by 2H2027, and c.35% in 2H28"* ([Bernstein, zsxq #214528222851451 p.1](http://xs-macbook-air.local:5001/zsxq/pdf/214528222851451/Bernstein-Asia%20Tech%20Hardware%EF%BC%9AMonthly%20sales~Unimicron%202Q26%20revenue%20tracking%20in%20line%20while%20Quanta%20tracking%20above-260608.pdf#page=1)). The glass-substrate `adjacent` names are stage 3: Intel shipped the **first commercial glass-core CPU in January 2026** and TSMC's CoPoS line is in pilot with output scaling **2028-2029** ([玻璃基板：AI封装的下一块关键拼图, zsxq #814528288121422 p.1](http://xs-macbook-air.local:5001/zsxq/pdf/814528288121422/%E7%8E%BB%E7%92%83%E5%9F%BA%E6%9D%BF%EF%BC%9AAI%E5%B0%81%E8%A3%85%E7%9A%84%E4%B8%8B%E4%B8%80%E5%9D%97%E5%85%B3%E9%94%AE%E6%8B%BC%E5%9B%BE.pdf#page=1)). This window's rotation — stage-1 names up, stage-2 ABF names down — is the market handing leadership back to the names with un-printed pricing still ahead of them.

**Scenario clause.** Bull: the GS *"largest cycles in history"* framing holds — two phases (AI-server infrastructure now, edge/physical AI devices next) push sector earnings, margins and ROE through prior peaks ([GS, zsxq #181245445245282 p.1/p.3-5](http://xs-macbook-air.local:5001/zsxq/pdf/181245445245282/Goldman%20Sachs-JAPAN%20ELECTRONIC%20COMPONENTS%20SEMICONDUCTORS%EF%BC%9APotential%20onset%20of%20one%20of%20the%20largest%20cycles%20in%20history%EF%BC%9B%20remain%20bullish%20on%20Murata%20Taiyo%20Yuden%20Renesas%20Ibiden%EF%BC%8C%20Rohm%20up%20to%20Buy%EF%BC%8C%20downgrade%20Nitto%20Denko%20JAE-260608.pdf#page=1)); the single condition that must hold is **AI-server build-out cadence not slipping** while consumer demand stays firm enough to absorb pass-through. Bear: passives cycles end faster than they start — the 2018/2021 MLCC and substrate episodes ended in 30-50% peak-to-trough corrections ([eeNews Europe, 2026](https://www.eenewseurope.com/en/ai-drives-mlcc-shortage/)); the cited bear marks are Citi's **Sell ¥2,900 on Taiyo Yuden** and **Neutral ¥3,900 on Murata** (both 05-21/27, see Valuation snapshot) — i.e. one bulge-bracket desk already prices the MLCC trio as a cycle top. **Conviction ranks (cited, not ours):** Citi ranks *"KBL > Han's Laser > Shengyi"* on the China AI-infra shortage theme ([Citi Kingboard, zsxq #415288428282288 p.1](http://xs-macbook-air.local:5001/zsxq/pdf/415288428282288/CITI-Kingboard%20Laminates%20Holdings%20%EF%BC%881888.HK%EF%BC%89%20Raising%20TP%20to%20HK%2476%20for%20Higher~than~expected%20ASP%20Hike%20for%20June-260603.pdf#page=1)); GS's Japan order of preference is Murata (Buy, on CL) / Taiyo Yuden / Renesas / Ibiden, with Nitto Denko and JAE explicitly downgraded as cost-squeezed non-AI names ([GS, zsxq #181245445245282 p.1](http://xs-macbook-air.local:5001/zsxq/pdf/181245445245282/Goldman%20Sachs-JAPAN%20ELECTRONIC%20COMPONENTS%20SEMICONDUCTORS%EF%BC%9APotential%20onset%20of%20one%20of%20the%20largest%20cycles%20in%20history%EF%BC%9B%20remain%20bullish%20on%20Murata%20Taiyo%20Yuden%20Renesas%20Ibiden%EF%BC%8C%20Rohm%20up%20to%20Buy%EF%BC%8C%20downgrade%20Nitto%20Denko%20JAE-260608.pdf#page=1)). *Analyst view (this note):* after the GS/Citi PT-tripling wave, the marginal information is in operating prints, not targets — Unimicron's "in line" was punished, which says positioning, not thesis, is the near-term risk.

## Scope rules

**In:** (a) **MLCC** suppliers with disclosed AI-server exposure — Japan trio (Murata, TDK, Taiyo Yuden), Taiwan (Yageo, Walsin), A-share substitutes (Sanhuan, Fenghua); (b) **ABF substrate / FC-BGA** fabricators in the NVIDIA / AMD / Broadcom / ASIC chain — Ibiden, Unimicron, Nan Ya PCB, Kinsus, AT&S — plus the upstream resin monopolist Ajinomoto; (c) **high-speed CCL and AI-server PCB** — Shengyi, Kingboard, WUS, Shennan, Avary; (d) **glass substrate / photomask** — Corning, Photronics. Every member needs at least one 2025-26 sell-side or IR document naming its AI-related business as a dated growth driver.

**Out:** foundry / wafer / semicap (separate themes); DRAM/NAND/HBM (tracked in [memory-upcycle](memory-upcycle_theme.md)); server ODMs (DELL/HPE/SMCI — integration margin, doesn't share the passives pricing rent); connector names (中航光电 etc. — tracked in [ai-power-electrification](ai-power-electrification_theme.md)); small-cap PCB/CCL without disclosed AI-server SKUs. Privately-held names surface in the Watch list, not the basket.
