# Rare Earth & Strategic Minerals — Geopolitics

**Created:** 2026-05-31 · **Last refreshed:** 2026-05-31 · **Last mutated:** 2026-05-31 · **Refresh cadence:** monthly · **Languages tracked:** en

## Thesis

The thesis is supply-chain de-risking *as a re-pricing event*, not as a slow industrial transition. Two compounding flashpoints anchor the trade. First, China's April 2025 export-licensing regime on seven heavy / medium rare earths (Sm, Gd, Tb, Dy, Lu, Sc, Y) followed by the November 2025 magnet ban tightening, has bifurcated REE pricing globally: terbium and dysprosium tripled in Europe within a month of the April rule, with European Dy reportedly fetching three times the Chinese spot quote, and Western buyers now pay an explicit "security premium" for non-Chinese supply ([CSET MOFCOM Notice No. 61](https://cset.georgetown.edu/wp-content/uploads/t0656_china_rare_earth_controls_2025_61_EN.pdf); [IEA commentary](https://www.iea.org/commentaries/with-new-export-controls-on-critical-minerals-supply-concentration-risks-become-reality); [Al Jazeera, 2025-10](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/10/10/china-tightens-export-controls-on-rare-earth-metals-why-this-matters)). Spot pricing remains dominated by China, but the dual-market structure (Chinese-domestic vs Western-security-premium) now embeds a structural arbitrage for any non-Chinese producer that crosses qualification at scale.

Second, the 2026 Latin American electoral supercycle stacks roughly a quarter to a third of marginal global Cu / Fe supply behind political dates. Chile (~25% of global Cu + ~30% of global Li), Peru (~12% of global Cu, third-largest producer), Brazil (top niobium, major Fe + Li), and Colombia all run general elections in 2026, with Brazil's Lula re-running, Peru's process generating a ~56-day uncertainty window, and Chile's transition shaping royalty / water-rights regimes governing Escondida-class assets ([JPM Private Bank LatAm electoral supercycle](https://privatebank.jpmorgan.com/latam/en/insights/markets-and-investing/ideas-and-insights/the-2026-electoral-supercycle-in-latin-america-pragmatism-or-more-fragmentation); [Mining South East Europe on Peru](https://www.miningsee.eu/peru-mining-politics-create-2026-investment-uncertainty-as-copper-sector-faces-electoral-risk-premium/); [Foreign Policy, 2026-01-30](https://foreignpolicy.com/2026/01/30/copper-prices-latin-america-trump-tariffs-critical-minerals/)). Indonesia's 2025 nickel-export rules and the September 2025 FCX Grasberg mud-rush incident extend the same electoral / resource-nationalism vector to Southeast Asia ([FCX FY25 ARS](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000831259/000083125926000023/fcx_arx2025xfinal.pdf)).

The investment expression is a 15-name basket spanning rare-earth pure-plays, NdFeB-magnet midstream, Cu / Fe diversified majors, gold as supply-disruption hedge, and lithium critical-mineral pure-plays. The mixed-commodity construction tests whether named-ticker active management beats the passive [REMX ETF](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001137360/000113736026000500/vaneckrareearthandstrategi.htm). The honest answer at inception (see Performance) is *no over 1Y* — REMX's heavy Australia / lithium weighting outran a more diversified basket. The basket's edge is intended to manifest in dispersion events: a Peruvian Tía María-style permit revocation, a Tesla Optimus magnet-supplier announcement, a new MP-style DoD off-take, or a Brazil Lula tax overhaul each move individual names hard while REMX averages through.

## Scope rules

**In:** rare-earth miners + magnet-midstream processors (NdFeB inputs); copper miners with primary or majority Cu exposure; iron-ore majors with Fe as a material segment; lithium miners (spodumene + brine); gold miners as a geopolitical-hedge slot; critical / strategic mineral pure-plays. Qualifies if investable as common equity with a named 2025–2026 primary source linking it to one of the five buckets.

**Out:** precious-metals streaming / royalty companies (Wheaton, Royal Gold, Franco-Nevada — capital-light financial intermediaries, not supply-of-rock plays). Oil & gas majors (XOM, Shell — equity dominated by hydrocarbon beta). Agriculture and coal. Precious-metals ETFs (GLD, IAU). Defense conglomerates with too-dilute REE-input cost lines (Lockheed, RTX). Generic copper smelters without mining exposure (Xiamen Tungsten qualifies as enabler via its NdFeB-magnet franchise; pure smelters do not).

## Drift signals

This is the inception write — drift detection becomes the value-add starting from the *next* refresh. Initial flags for next month's pass:

- **Active vs passive lag (central signal).** The 1Y basket gap to REMX (−19.4pp) is the central inception finding. Two diagnostic paths: (a) under-weight lithium / Australia vs REMX's 24.3% Australia (March 2026 NPORT) — adding LTR.AX (Liontown) or ILU.AX (Iluka mineral sands + Eneabba REE refinery) would tighten the lag; (b) over-weight in diversified Cu/Fe majors (5 names) that trade more with the broad market than with the specific supply-disruption thesis. Next refresh: either justify the 5-name Cu/Fe sleeve as a hedge against single-commodity REE risk, or drop one and add a second-tier REE processor.
- **Chinese A-share REE underperformance.** Shenghe (−34.8% 3M), Northern REE (−22.1% 3M), Xiamen Tungsten (−22.5% 3M) are the three deepest 3M drawdowns. Two readings: (a) the China policy posture is being repriced as the November 2025 "pause" suggests negotiability, compressing the China-domestic price floor; (b) the dual-pricing structure is widening *in favour of Western names* (MP / LYC / UUUU all positive YTD) and *against* the Chinese incumbents. If the gap widens at H1 2026 earnings, reduce A-share REE weight.
- **Lithium-thesis fragility.** PLS (+421% 1Y) and MIN (+231% 1Y) are the basket's biggest individual contributors. Both rallied on a spodumene-price doubling (~$1,094 → ~$2,105/dmt) in two quarters. If lithium retraces to $1,200–1,500/dmt, PLS / MIN / ALB give back the bulk of the 1Y move quickly. Track BYD / CATL battery-pack price disclosures at H1 2026.
- **Gold-hedge timing.** Gold leg (NEM, AEM) mid-pack at +110.5% / +56.3% 1Y, lagging the basket median — *correct* hedge behaviour in steady-state. The hedge only earns its keep on shock days (Grasberg mud-rush, Peru-election surprise, Indonesia export ban). If gold continues to lag *and* no shocks land into the next refresh, consider rotating NEM into AEM (cleaner Canada-only).
- **Election calendar gap — Peru.** Brazil (Oct 2026) covered by VALE, Indonesia by FCX, Chile by BHP / RIO; no direct Peru pure-play. The basket's lack of a Peru name is a coverage gap if Tía María-style permit revocations escalate ([Peru reauthorizes SCCO Tía María](https://www.mining.com/peru-reauthorizes-southern-coppers-1-8b-project-amid-election-chaos/)). Re-evaluate SCCO or HBM (Hudbay Constancia) at next refresh.
