## The thesis in one paragraph

The 2026-2028 memory cycle is being re-rated from "cyclical recovery" to **structural super-cycle**. JPM raised its FY28E memory TAM to **US$1.7trn (+37–53% vs Mar-26 model)** with DRAM/NAND prices already +220–250% YoY in 2026 ([JPM Global Memory, zsxq #212485541484181 p.1](http://xs-macbook-air.local:5001/zsxq-pdf/212485541484181#page=1)). The new structural drivers: (i) **AI demand is broadening from GPU to CPU** — the GPU:CPU ratio is collapsing from 5.4:1 (2023) → 2.4:1 (2028E), driving server-DDR/LPDDR5 bit demand up another 20–22% on top of HBM ([JPM #212485541484181 p.1](http://xs-macbook-air.local:5001/zsxq-pdf/212485541484181#page=1)); (ii) **HBM stays in deepening shortage** — JPM models the HBM S-D glut at **-36% in 2028E** with blended HBM ASP +32% YoY in 27E ([JPM #212485541484181 p.13](http://xs-macbook-air.local:5001/zsxq-pdf/212485541484181#page=13)); (iii) **NAND turns into an AI compute tier** — UBS calls 6 more quarters of QoQ NAND price rises peaking Q3 2027, driven by KV-cache offload and pSLC needing 3-4x more wafers ([UBS Kioxia, zsxq #812485584818522 p.1, p.12](http://xs-macbook-air.local:5001/zsxq-pdf/812485584818522#page=1)); (iv) **LTAs are locking the cycle in place** — Nomura calls "memory price increases alone could contribute >0.8% to US CPI" in 2026 ([Nomura Global Memory, zsxq #812452121512222 p.3](http://xs-macbook-air.local:5001/zsxq-pdf/812452121512222#page=3)) and UBS estimates **20–30% of industry C2027 DDR bit shipments under enhanced LTAs** ([UBS Micron, zsxq #585428582552154 p.1](http://xs-macbook-air.local:5001/zsxq-pdf/585428582552154#page=1)). Bernstein notes the NAND-to-HDD ASP gap has blown out to **20–25× per gigabyte** vs a TCO crossover of 2–3×, which structurally **re-tiers AI datacenter storage back toward HDD** while keeping NAND ASPs elevated ([Bernstein HDD vs NAND, zsxq #812458511845242 p.4](http://xs-macbook-air.local:5001/zsxq-pdf/812458511845242#page=4)).
