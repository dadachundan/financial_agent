### Cross-asset story — equity vol is the dissenter

This is the live finding. **Equity vol freaked out alone.** Three independent risk-asset classes — Treasury vol (MOVE at 75, well below caution), credit (HY OAS flat at 2.74%, IG flat at 0.74%), and forward inflation expectations (T5YIFR 2.24%, below 2.5% un-anchoring threshold) — did NOT confirm the equity-vol reflex.

Read the asymmetry:
- If the equity-vol move had been a regime change, **MOVE would have spiked through 100** (it didn't), **HY OAS would have widened 30–50bp** (it didn't), and **T5YIFR would have crept toward 2.5%+** (it didn't move). All three said "no, NFP was a single-day repricing, not a structural shift."
- The two-year yield's modest +7bp move on the week (vs the front-end mechanically pricing the NFP-implied path) confirms: bond market sees NFP as a *clean reset to the existing hold-through-2026 thesis*, not an upgrade to "actually hike."

**The trade.** Equity vol mean-reverts when cross-asset doesn't follow. **Sell vol into the bounce** — the IV crush + multi-asset confirmation that nothing structural broke is the high-probability path. **Hedge the tail with cheap upside calls** — SKEW at 152 means puts are paid for fat, calls are relatively cheap; a bull CPI surprise gets you long the asymmetric leg. **Do NOT take a directional bear bet** — credit didn't validate it, so the bear-thesis floor is higher than the equity-vol entry implies.

## Valuation regime: AI capex supercycle (§I)

Per [Barclays "Pricing a Capex Supercycle"](http://xs-macbook-air.local:5001/zsxq/pdf/814528851485142/Barclays-U.S.%20Equity%20Insights%EF%BC%9APricing%20a%20capex%20supercycle-260605.pdf), S&P 500 2026 capex is +40% YoY (35-year high; capex/CFO ≈ 50%, TMT ≈ 50% of total). This is a regime where **EV/EBITDA / EV/EBIT correlate to forward 3y returns much more tightly than PE** — D&A and interest expense distort earnings during heavy build-out. For ORCL specifically (heavy data-center capex, accelerating depreciation), the right multiple for thesis sizing is EV/EBITDA, not forward PE. The PE-vs-EV-multiple gap on ORCL has widened materially over the last 2 quarters as the OCI capex ramped; if you size ORCL on PE you're under-allocating relative to the cash-flow story.
