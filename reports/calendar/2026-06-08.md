# Week of 2026-06-08: catalyst preview

**The week in one paragraph.** Two prints carry the tape and one absence shapes the reaction function. **May CPI on Wednesday and May PPI on Thursday** are the only macro events that materially matter, and both land inside the **FOMC blackout period (June 6–18)** ahead of the **June 16–17 FOMC meeting** — meaning no Fed pushback for or against whatever the print delivers. Cross-asset will trade these prints clean against the dot-plot setup. On the corporate side, **Oracle Wednesday after the close** is the single biggest read-across for cloud / AI infrastructure (OCI growth + AI-revenue disclosure), and **Adobe Thursday after the close** is the cleanest read on whether GenAI is genuinely impairing creative-software incumbents. Outside of those, the week is light — no FOMC, no NFP, no retail sales (those land the following week), no triple witching (that's June 19). Position around CPI; the rest is single-name flow.

## Key events ranked

1. **Wednesday 08:30 ET — May CPI** ([BLS release schedule](https://www.bls.gov/schedule/news_release/cpi.htm)). The big one. CPI lands inside the Fed blackout window, so the print has no immediate Fed counterweight — it sets dot-plot expectations into next week's SEP cold. Whisper is for a soft headline given May's typical disinflationary seasonals; the surprise direction that moves the most is a hot core services print (sticky shelter + supercore) which would push out cut timing without giving the Fed a chance to walk it back.
2. **Wednesday AMC — ORCL Q4 FY26 earnings**. Consensus revenue ~$19.1B (+20% YoY), EPS ~$1.96 (+15% YoY) per [MarketBeat](https://www.marketbeat.com/stocks/NYSE/ORCL/earnings/) / [Seeking Alpha week-ahead](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4601031-earnings-week-ahead-orcl-adbe-fcel-acb-chwy-and-more). A miss on OCI growth + soft RPO commentary tags the AI-spend-deceleration narrative.
3. **Thursday 08:30 ET — May PPI + initial jobless claims** ([BLS PPI schedule](https://www.bls.gov/schedule/news_release/ppi.htm)). PPI matters mainly for the core-PCE bridge (services PPI ex-trade-services feeds Friday-after-next's PCE). Jobless claims are weekly noise unless the trend re-breaks higher (>250k 4-week average would matter; current run-rate is below that on most desk dashboards).
4. **Thursday AMC — ADBE Q2 FY26 earnings**. Adobe is the cleanest GenAI-disruption-of-incumbents trade on the tape. Stock is down materially over the trailing year per the [Kiplinger earnings week-ahead](https://www.kiplinger.com/investing/stocks/17494/next-week-earnings-calendar-stocks); the print needs to show either Firefly monetization traction OR Creative Cloud net-add stabilization to break the down-trend. Anything else and the stock-as-melting-ice-cube narrative survives another quarter.
5. **Friday — U-Mich preliminary June consumer sentiment** (typically released the 2nd Friday at 10:00 ET — verify against [Briefing.com](https://www.briefing.com/calendars/economic/) close to date). 1-year inflation expectations is the only sub-print that moves the rates curve; everything else is noise.

The biggest macro event of the week. Lands inside the FOMC blackout (June 6–18), so no Fed walk-back available before the June 17 SEP. Three-month trend is **re-accelerating headline + sticky core**. 1Y trend: [FRED — CPIAUCSL](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/CPIAUCSL).

| Apr 10, 2026 | Mar 2026 | +0.9% | **3.3%** (highest since Apr-24) | +0.2% | 2.6% (below consensus) | Muted — core came in soft despite +10.9% energy (Iran war); Fed could look through ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/10/treasury-yields-flat-as-traders-brace-for-march-cpi-print.html)) |

| **Jun 10, 2026** | **May 2026** | **TBD** | **TBD** | **TBD** | **TBD** | — |

**Trend read.** Headline has marched 2.4 → 3.3 → 3.8% YoY over three prints, mostly energy-driven (Iran war pushed gasoline >$4 in April). Core is stickier than the headline math implies — even when March's core looked tame (+0.2%), April's +0.4% confirmed tariff pass-through and services ex-housing pressure that several strategists flagged in the February print. The May number is the proof point: does core revert toward +0.2% as the energy shock fades post-ceasefire, or does it stick at +0.4% confirming the tariff/services-inflation transmission? With CPI printing inside the Fed blackout, neither outcome gets walked back before June 17.

**Bull / Bear print (May CPI):**
- **Bull (revives cut path)** — **Core MoM ≤ +0.2%**, headline MoM ≤ +0.1%, headline YoY ≤ 3.5%, core YoY ≤ 2.7%. This reverts the April +0.4% core spike and signals the tariff/services pass-through faded with the energy shock. SPX gaps up, 10y rallies (yield down) 8–15bp, 2y rallies harder (curve steepens), Fed-funds-implied cut-path back to 2H26.
- **Bear (kills cuts, raises hike odds)** — **Core MoM ≥ +0.4%**, headline YoY ≥ 4.0% (would be highest since early-2024), core YoY ≥ 3.0%. Confirms April was regime change, not energy outlier. 10y back through 4.50%, 2y through 4.05%, hike-odds-by-year-end > 40%, SPX gap down. Inside the Fed blackout, this just sits on the tape until the June 17 SEP confirms / pushes back.
- **In-line / neutral**: Core MoM +0.3%, headline YoY 3.6–3.9%. Probably small risk-on rally given hot whispers floating around, but the rates path doesn't meaningfully shift.

**Trend read.** April's +1.4% was the strongest PPI print in nearly 4 years, with energy contributing ~75% of the goods-side surge (final-demand energy +7.8%; gasoline +15.6%). Two-thirds of the trade-services jump (+2.7%) was attributed to tariff pass-through — that's the part the Fed cares about because it persists after the energy shock fades. May PPI is the read on whether April was an Iran-war outlier or a regime change.

**Bull / Bear print (May PPI):**

Final-demand energy disinflating consistent with post-ceasefire oil. Sets up a benign May core-PCE print on Jun 27.
- **Bear (Fed has to wait through Q3)** — Headline MoM ≥ +0.6% (sticky after April), trade services accelerating sequentially, services-PPI-ex-trade > +0.3%. Would imply core-PCE on Jun 27 prints +0.3%+ MoM. Reinforces the CPI-bear case if both inflation prints land hot this week.
- **In-line / neutral**: Headline MoM +0.3–0.5%, trade services mildly cooling. Limited rates impact; market focus shifts to Friday's U-Mich inflation expectations.

**Bull / Bear print (week ending Jun 6):**
- **Bull (Goldilocks — stable labor, no recession panic)** — Initial **200k–220k**, 4-week MA flat-to-down, continuing claims flat-to-down. Confirms the year's stable-range narrative. No rates impact.
- **Dovish-but-not-broken (the Fed's preferred outcome)** — Initial **220k–235k**, 4-week MA drifting up slightly toward 218–220k, continuing creeping toward 1.80M. Signals enough cooling for the Fed to credibly consider cuts later this year without triggering recession headlines. Mildly bullish for rates / risk.
- **Bear (labor market breaking)** — **Initial > 235k, 4-week MA breaking 230k, continuing back above 1.85M.** This is the recession-risk trigger; 2y yields drop hard (5–10bp), curve steepens aggressively, and the "growth scare" narrative resurrects regardless of what CPI did the day before. Note: a single 235k print is not the signal — it's the 4-week MA breaking the band that matters.
