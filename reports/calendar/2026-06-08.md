# Week of 2026-06-08: catalyst preview

**The week in one paragraph.** Two prints carry the tape and one absence shapes the reaction function. **May CPI on Wednesday and May PPI on Thursday** are the only macro events that materially matter, and both land inside the **FOMC blackout period (June 6–18)** ahead of the **June 16–17 FOMC meeting** — meaning no Fed pushback for or against whatever the print delivers. Cross-asset will trade these prints clean against the dot-plot setup. On the corporate side, **Oracle Wednesday after the close** is the single biggest read-across for cloud / AI infrastructure (OCI growth + AI-revenue disclosure), and **Adobe Thursday after the close** is the cleanest read on whether GenAI is genuinely impairing creative-software incumbents. Outside of those, the week is light — no FOMC, no NFP, no retail sales (those land the following week), no triple witching (that's June 19). Position around CPI; the rest is single-name flow.

## Key events ranked

1. **Wednesday 08:30 ET — May CPI** ([BLS release schedule](https://www.bls.gov/schedule/news_release/cpi.htm)). The big one. CPI lands inside the Fed blackout window, so the print has no immediate Fed counterweight — it sets dot-plot expectations into next week's SEP cold. Whisper is for a soft headline given May's typical disinflationary seasonals; the surprise direction that moves the most is a hot core services print (sticky shelter + supercore) which would push out cut timing without giving the Fed a chance to walk it back.
2. **Wednesday AMC — ORCL Q4 FY26 earnings**. Consensus revenue ~$19.1B (+20% YoY), EPS ~$1.96 (+15% YoY) per [MarketBeat](https://www.marketbeat.com/stocks/NYSE/ORCL/earnings/) / [Seeking Alpha week-ahead](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4601031-earnings-week-ahead-orcl-adbe-fcel-acb-chwy-and-more). The hyperscaler-adjacent print of the week. OCI growth rate and RPO build are the only two numbers that matter; an RPO bookings number that re-accelerates AI-infra read-across will lift NVDA / AVGO / TSM into Thursday. A miss on OCI growth + soft RPO commentary tags the AI-spend-deceleration narrative.
3. **Thursday 08:30 ET — May PPI + initial jobless claims** ([BLS PPI schedule](https://www.bls.gov/schedule/news_release/ppi.htm)). PPI matters mainly for the core-PCE bridge (services PPI ex-trade-services feeds Friday-after-next's PCE). Jobless claims are weekly noise unless the trend re-breaks higher (>250k 4-week average would matter; current run-rate is below that on most desk dashboards).
4. **Thursday AMC — ADBE Q2 FY26 earnings**. Adobe is the cleanest GenAI-disruption-of-incumbents trade on the tape. Stock is down materially over the trailing year per the [Kiplinger earnings week-ahead](https://www.kiplinger.com/investing/stocks/17494/next-week-earnings-calendar-stocks); the print needs to show either Firefly monetization traction OR Creative Cloud net-add stabilization to break the down-trend. Anything else and the stock-as-melting-ice-cube narrative survives another quarter.
5. **Friday — U-Mich preliminary June consumer sentiment** (typically released the 2nd Friday at 10:00 ET — verify against [Briefing.com](https://www.briefing.com/calendars/economic/) close to date). 1-year inflation expectations is the only sub-print that moves the rates curve; everything else is noise.

## Fed / central bank speakers

- **None this week.** The Fed enters its [FOMC blackout period](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/files/fomc-blackout-period-calendar.pdf) on Saturday June 6 ahead of the June 16–17 FOMC meeting. No Powell, no Williams, no Waller, no regional Fed commentary on policy. This is structurally important: CPI prints into a Fed that **cannot speak**. The market reaction trades clean both directions because there is no Fed walk-back available before next week's SEP. Position size accordingly into the print.

## Positioning implications

- **Size around CPI, not earnings.** ORCL/ADBE are single-name binaries that won't move the index. CPI inside-blackout is the only print that moves SPX cleanly because it has no Fed counterweight. If you're SPX-vol long, Wednesday is your day; if you're a single-name shop, Thursday AMC into Adobe is where the gamma lives.
- **Lean into the AI-infra read-across pre-Oracle.** NVDA / AVGO / TSM into ORCL Wednesday is a positive-skew setup if you believe AI-spend deceleration is not yet the right call; tighten the stop into the print.
- **Hedge the FOMC blackout vacuum.** The week after (June 16–17 FOMC + SEP + Powell presser + triple witching same Friday) is where the gamma compresses. If you're long delta into both weeks, the cleanest hedge is short-dated SPX puts dated to June 19 (covers both the CPI tail and the FOMC reaction).
- **No reason to be in retail-sales beta names this week.** Retail sales lands next week (~June 17); WMT / TGT / COST positioning that week, not this.
