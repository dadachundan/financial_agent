# Week of 2026-06-08: catalyst preview

**The week in one paragraph.** Two prints carry the tape and one absence shapes the reaction function. **May CPI on Wednesday and May PPI on Thursday** are the only macro events that materially matter, and both land inside the **FOMC blackout period (June 6–18)** ahead of the **June 16–17 FOMC meeting** — meaning no Fed pushback for or against whatever the print delivers. On the corporate side, **Oracle Wednesday after the close** and **Adobe Thursday after the close** are the two single-name binaries. **But the entry-state has flipped vs the original brief**: Friday's May NFP printed **+172k vs +85k consensus** ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/05/jobs-report-may-2026.html)), spiking VIX from 15.4 to 21.5 (+40%), flipping the VIX term structure into backwardation, and pushing 10y to 4.54%, DXY to 100, and hike-by-year-end odds from ~3% to ~3.5% on FedWatch with no-cuts-this-year now at 57% per [Polymarket](https://polymarket.com/dashboards/fed-rates). Vol entered the week **rich**, not cheap — which inverts the original positioning call. Long-gamma into CPI is no longer the asymmetric trade; the IV crush is.
