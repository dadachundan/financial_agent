# The Boeing Company (BA) — SEC filings summary, FY2020–FY2025

*Source: six consecutive Form 10-K filings (FY2020–FY2025) pulled from SEC EDGAR / `db/financial_reports.db`, read via the sec-report-summary skill. As of 2026-06-14.*

> **Thesis (FY20→FY25): a six-year crisis arc bottoming and inflecting — production recovering, defense losses shrinking, the balance sheet repaired, but free cash flow not yet positive.** Boeing's revenue collapsed under COVID + the 737 MAX grounding (FY2020 $58.2bn, net cash *used* by ops −$18.4bn, debt up to $63.6bn), then re-broke in FY2024 when the Alaska Airlines 737-9 door-plug blowout (Jan 2024) triggered an FAA cap of **38 aircraft/month** and a 53-day IAM strike (net cash used −$12.1bn). FY2025 is the inflection: revenue rebounded to a record **$89.5bn (+35% YoY)**, the company posted its **first net profit since 2018 (+$2.24bn)** — though that figure is propped up by a one-time **$9.57bn gain** on the Digital Aviation Solutions divestiture — shareholders' equity flipped from a **−$3.9bn deficit to +$5.5bn**, the FAA approved a rate increase to **42/month**, and Boeing **reacquired Spirit AeroSystems (closed Dec 8, 2025)** to re-vertically-integrate 737/787 fuselage production ([FY2025 10-K, filed 2026-01-30](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/12927/000162828026004357/ba-20251231.htm)). The thesis-relevant read: the survival question is largely answered; the open question is the *pace* of the cash-flow recovery, gated by the 737 ramp to 47→52/month, the 777X certification (now slipped to 2027), and defense fixed-price losses (KC-46A, T-7A, MQ-25, VC-25B, Commercial Crew).

## Per-filing highlights (newest → oldest)

### FY2025 10-K — [SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=BA&type=10-K) (filed 2026-01-30, period 2025-12-31)
- **Business**: Three segments unchanged (BCA, BDS, BGS). Total backlog hit a record **$682.2bn** (BCA $567.3bn, BDS $84.8bn, BGS $29.7bn). Workforce ~182,000. Reacquired **Spirit AeroSystems** (closed Dec 8, 2025), with $2,571M of the consideration funding Spirit's payment to Airbus for the carve-out of Airbus-program work.
- **Key risks**: `ESCALATED` tariffs / global trade (new U.S.-China trade détente extended to Nov 2026, but Chinese carriers temporarily paused deliveries during 2025); `ADDED` Spirit integration risk (M&A non-realization); `PERSISTS` fixed-price defense losses (five major programs); `PERSISTS` 777X/737-7/737-10 certification timing.
- **New this year**: Returned to **net profit ($2.24bn)** and **positive shareholders' equity (+$5.5bn vs −$3.9bn)**; FAA approved 737 rate to **42/month** (Oct 2025) with plans to 47 in 2026; 777X first delivery **slipped to 2027** ($4.9bn incremental reach-forward loss); DAS divested to Thoma Bravo for a $9.57bn gain; 101-day IAM District 837 (St. Louis) strike disrupted F-15/F/A-18/T-7A/MQ-25.

### FY2024 10-K — [SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=BA&type=10-K) (filed 2025-02-03, period 2024-12-31)
- **Business**: The crisis year. Alaska Airlines **737-9 door-plug blowout (Jan 5, 2024)** triggered an FAA investigation of Boeing's and Spirit's quality systems and a **cap of 38 737s/month**, no new lines until quality KPIs met.
- **Key risks**: `ESCALATED` production-system / quality risk (FAA oversight, traveled work); `ESCALATED` labor (53-day IAM 751 strike, Sept–Nov 2024, halted most commercial + some BDS production); `ESCALATED` liquidity (net cash used −$12.1bn).
- **New this year**: ~$24bn equity-and-equity-linked raise (Oct 2024) to shore up liquidity and protect the investment-grade rating; net loss −$11.8bn; agreed to reacquire Spirit (announced July 2024); Kelly Ortberg became CEO (Aug 2024).

### FY2023 10-K — [SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=BA&type=10-K) (filed 2024-01-31, period 2023-12-31)
- **Business**: Recovery underway — revenue $77.8bn (+17%), narrowest operating loss of the period (−$0.8bn), positive operating cash flow $5.96bn. 737 and 787 deliveries ramping.
- **Key risks**: `PERSISTS` 737 MAX legal/regulatory; `ESCALATED` defense fixed-price development losses (KC-46A, MQ-25, T-7A, VC-25B, Commercial Crew); `PERSISTS` supply-chain and labor instability.
- **New this year**: Last 747 delivered (Feb 2023); momentum toward positive FCF — the best year of the window before the FY2024 relapse.

### FY2022 10-K — [SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=BA&type=10-K) (filed 2023-01-27, period 2022-12-31)
- **Business**: Revenue $66.6bn; net loss −$4.9bn, widened by **$3.4bn of additional fixed-price development charges** at BDS.
- **Key risks**: `ADDED` defense fixed-price program losses as a named, recurring theme; `PERSISTS` COVID supply-chain lingering; `ESCALATED` inflation.
- **New this year**: First positive operating cash flow since 2018 ($3.5bn); 787 deliveries resumed after the 2021 pause.

### FY2021 10-K — [SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=BA&type=10-K) (filed 2022-01-31, period 2021-12-31)
- **Business**: 737 MAX recertified and returned to service across most jurisdictions; revenue $62.3bn (+7%); net loss −$4.2bn.
- **Key risks**: `PERSISTS` 737 MAX ramp & legal; `ADDED` 787 production halt and rework (delivery pause); `PERSISTS` COVID demand.
- **New this year**: 787 delivery pause due to fuselage non-conformance; January 2021 DOJ Deferred Prosecution Agreement ($744M charge in Q4 2020) in force.

### FY2020 10-K — [SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=BA&type=10-K) (filed 2021-02-01, period 2020-12-31)
- **Business**: The trough. COVID + 737 MAX grounding crushed revenue to $58.2bn; **net cash used by operations −$18.4bn**; debt ballooned to **$63.6bn (from $27.3bn in 2019)**.
- **Key risks**: `ADDED` COVID-19 pandemic (demand collapse, customer financing defaults); `ESCALATED` 737 MAX grounding/legal; `ADDED` 777X reach-forward loss.
- **New this year**: 737 MAX recertified by FAA (Nov 2020), deliveries resumed Dec 2020; entered the DPA with DOJ.

## Forward guidance & capital-return deltas (Section 3b)

- **Production-rate trajectory (the key "guidance" for an aircraft maker)**: 737 `<38/mo (early 2025) → 42/mo (FAA-approved Oct 2025) → 47/mo (planned 2026) → 52/mo+ (2027, North Line at Everett)`; 787 `5/mo → 7/mo → 8/mo (Q4 2025) → 10/mo (target 2026)`; 777X first delivery `2025 → 2026 → 2027 (slipped twice)` ([FY25 10-K Program Highlights](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/12927/000162828026004357/ba-20251231.htm)).
- **Capital return**: Dividend `suspended since 2020 → still suspended`; common dividends junior to the 6.00% Series A mandatory convertible preferred ($5.75bn liquidation preference, $345M of preferred dividends accrued in FY2025). No buyback authorization active. Capital priority is debt reduction.
- **Capex outlook**: `$1.5bn (FY23) → $2.2bn (FY24) → $2.9bn (FY25)` rising with the production ramp and Spirit integration ([FY25 10-K Cash Flows](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/12927/000162828026004357/ba-20251231.htm)).
- **Debt**: Total debt `$53.9bn (FY24, ST $1.3bn + LT $52.6bn) → $54.1bn (FY25, ST $8.5bn + LT $45.6bn)` — roughly flat, but the current portion jumped as near-term maturities approach ([FY25 10-K Balance Sheet](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/12927/000162828026004357/ba-20251231.htm)).

## Changes over the years (ranked by thesis impact)

1. **Threshold crossing — first net profit since 2018 and positive equity restored (FY2025).** Net earnings flipped to **+$2.24bn** from −$11.8bn, and shareholders' equity from a **−$3.9bn deficit to +$5.5bn**, driven by the $24bn FY2024 capital raise plus the $9.57bn DAS divestiture gain ([FY25 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/12927/000162828026004357/ba-20251231.htm)). The "going-concern-adjacent" balance sheet of FY2024 is no longer the story.
2. **Backlog re-rating — total backlog $521bn → $682bn (+31%) over one year**, led by BCA ($435bn → $567bn) as Chinese and other orders converted ([FY25 10-K Backlog](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/12927/000162828026004357/ba-20251231.htm)). Multi-year revenue visibility is the highest in the company's history.
3. **Defense losses shrank — fixed-price development charges $5,013M (FY24) → $802M (FY25)**, the single biggest swing in BDS, which moved from −22.6% to −0.5% operating margin ([FY25 10-K BDS MD&A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/12927/000162828026004357/ba-20251231.htm)). The bleeding has slowed, but high-single-digit BDS margins remain a multi-year aspiration.
4. **Vertical re-integration — Spirit AeroSystems reacquired (Dec 2025)**, reversing the 2005 spin-off, to bring 737/787 fuselage quality back in-house after the door-plug crisis ([FY25 10-K Note 2](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/12927/000162828026004357/ba-20251231.htm)). Goodwill jumped $8.1bn → $17.3bn.
5. **New risk category — tariffs / global trade** escalated from a generic mention to a named, material watch item (U.S.-China delivery pauses, aluminum/steel/copper tariffs) in FY2025 ([FY25 10-K Global Trade](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/12927/000162828026004357/ba-20251231.htm)) — a genuinely net-new emphasis vs the FY2020–FY2023 filings.
